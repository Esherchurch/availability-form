Giving

The church's bank details, shown to people who have signed in — on the Give screen in the app and the Give page on the website. Type them once; nothing else needs them again.

Notifications

Rota reminders, booking news, new maintenance jobs, urgent notices and DBS and training reminders, by phone, and by email for anyone without the app.

What does your church use?

Switch off any part your church does not use: it goes from the Menu, the app and Home for everyone. Nothing is deleted, and switching it back on brings it all back. The add-ons are the extras a church may not need.

Private forms

Care & support, prayer requests and “I’m new here”. Until the hub goes live they save nothing, because the database is not locked yet: they show 999 and when they open.

Who gets told

When somebody asks for pastoral care, raises a concern or asks for prayer, the people named here are told that something has come in — never who or what. They can involve abuse, so they never go to the church office by default: only if you type its address here on purpose. With nobody named, they go to the safeguarding lead and deputy.

API.Bible

The church's own key for API.Bible, so the session planner can bring in passages from the Bibles on it (for example the NIV, if the key allows it). It is kept by the hub's server and never shown again.

Microsoft 365

For Present: with your church’s Microsoft 365 connected, PowerPoint slides are drawn by Microsoft’s own PowerPoint.

Email and Canva keys

Church HQ's email (Resend) keys are kept in Google Secret Manager; this says whether they are set. Canva's keys are pasted here once; the hub's server keeps them and never shows them again.

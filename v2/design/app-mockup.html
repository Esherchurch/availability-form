EGBC Hub on your phone

One app for everyone. Your own space comes first. If you serve on a team or lead something, that team gets its own space, one tap away along the top.

See it as
  • Four tabs at the bottom, never more, and no hidden menus.
  • Every team gets its own space, coloured its own way. People only see the teams they are on.
  • Home answers "what do I need to know this week?"
  • The rows along the top switch between your spaces.
  • No jargon, and no "…" menus hiding actions.

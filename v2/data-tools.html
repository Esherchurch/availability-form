Data tools

Back up, restore, and copy between Firebase projects

1 · Back up

Reads every collection from egbc-worship-planner and downloads it as a single JSON file. Read-only — nothing is changed. Worth doing before any significant change, and periodically regardless.

2 · Restore or copy

This writes data. Point it at a test project unless you are deliberately restoring live. Documents with matching IDs are overwritten. Nothing is deleted.

Both are on the Firebase console under Project settings → General → Your apps. They are public values, not secrets.

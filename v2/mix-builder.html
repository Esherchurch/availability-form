Mix Builder

One saved project: the whole running order on a timeline, a transition designed at every junction, and a render that bounces the set to a single continuous WAV. Everything runs in this page — no audio is uploaded anywhere, and nothing is stored but your settings.

Offline Saved
Your music is read from disk. Nothing is copied, and the folder is remembered.
Running order

Choose the spreadsheet, or paste the sheet with its header row. The columns it looks for are #, Track, Artist, BPM, Section, Mix, Note. The Mix column already carries the junction decisions (HARD CUT, STOP / START, COLD START, HALF-TIME, TEMPO CHANGE, Lock on, Straight blend), so the project seeds itself rather than asking.

The set
100% Widen the timeline to click a junction directly, or use the transition rows in Tracks below.
Tracks
Sample library

Hooks, stabs and risers cut from any track. Stored unstretched at their own tempo and stretched per placement to whatever is playing where they land, so one sample can be dropped at a 108 BPM junction and a 124 BPM one and be in time at both.

Render

Bounces the set to one continuous WAV. Each track is stretched once with its tempo easing from the tempo the previous track needed to the tempo the next one needs, and tracks overlap at each junction rather than being spliced — so there are no seams to click.

Bench

Alternates and anything you have taken out. Swapping is always reversible — the track you replace comes here rather than being lost.

How to present

Two minutes. The same in a session and (soon) in a video meeting.

1

Have your PowerPoint ready

Use the .pptx file, with your speaker notes in it if you like. Keynote or Google Slides? Save or download it as PowerPoint first (File, then Export or Download, then PowerPoint).

2

Add it

In the session plan, add a PowerPoint step and choose the file, or paste its OneDrive link (Share, then "Anyone with the link can view"). In about half a minute it becomes slides and your notes. Hidden slides are left out, as in PowerPoint.

3

Press Present

Open Present on your phone or laptop and press Present. Everyone following sees your first slide, large, and the TV shows it full screen. You see the slide, what’s next, your notes and a timer.

4

Move on

Next and Back, or the arrow keys, Space, Page Down or a clicker. Everyone moves with you at once, even on weak Wi-Fi: the slides were fetched before you started. Blank the screen (or B) when you want their attention.

Q

A quiz

Write it in the session plan as a Quiz step. When you reach it in Present, choose: Teams on the TV (no phones: you tap which teams got it right, with a bonus for the first team if you like) or Everyone on their phones (they scan the QR code or go to churchhq.co.uk/quiz and type the code; a nickname, no account). You can remove a rude name. End the quiz deletes everything.

5

Hand over, or stop

Another leader can Take over presenting; you’ll see who has it. When you’re done, Stop presenting. Anyone can Browse on my own to look back, then Back to presenter.

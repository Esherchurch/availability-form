Trying the new e-news

Thank you for helping. We want to know whether making the e-news in the new church hub is as easy as it is in Mailchimp.

It's the hub being tried out, not you. If something is hard, that is exactly what we need to find.

How it works

  • Work as you would in Mailchimp. There's no rush.
  • Please say out loud what you're looking for and what you expected to happen.
  • The person with you will only help if you've been stuck for about three minutes, or if you ask.
  • This week's e-news has already been drafted by the hub: What's On, Sunday and the notices are in it.

What to do

  1. Open this week's e-news, then open the editor.
  2. Put this week's picture at the top. Choose one from the church's pictures.
  3. Add this week's verse. Any verse you like.
  4. Make sure two events are in it, each with its picture and a few words. Take out any others you don't want.
  5. Colour one word teal.
  6. Make one word a link to the church's website.
  7. Send yourself a test, and check it arrives. (On the test PC it arrives in the “Inbox (this PC)” tab, not your own email.)

At the end

We'll ask you: was it quicker, slower or about the same as Mailchimp? And what was harder than it should be?

The aim: a normal week's e-news in under 30 minutes, without help.

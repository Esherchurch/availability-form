# Permissions

This is where master admins choose who may do what in the hub, section by section. Master admins can always do everything. Here you tick who else may.

## Who can use it
- Only master admins can open it.
- Anyone else is told they cannot open the page.

## Find a permission
1. Type in the search box, for example "upload" or a person's name.
2. Under **By section**, each section is a card, such as **Events & rooms**, **Communications** or **Money**. Press a section's name to open or close it.
3. Each card says how many actions it has, and how many exceptions.

## Choose who may do something
1. Open the section and find the action.
2. **Master admins** is always ticked. You cannot change that.
3. Tick who else may. The groups of ticks are:
   - **Core teams covering**: the core teams that look after a job's teams.
   - **Everyone on these jobs' teams**: everyone on the teams that do that job.
   - **Their admins**: the admins of those teams.
   - **Leaders of the teams ticked here**: the leaders of the teams you tick for this action.
   - **Choose teams…**: press it to tick single teams.
4. A bar appears at the bottom. Press **Save** to keep your changes, or **Cancel** to undo them.

## Give one person an exception
1. Under the action, find **Exceptions: these people too** and press **+ Add a person**.
2. In **Person**, pick someone from the address book.
3. Choose **Until (optional)** if it should end on a date.
4. Fill in **Why (master admins see this)** so others know the reason.
5. Press **Add**. It is saved straight away.

## Remove an exception
1. Press **Remove** beside the person, either under the action or in **Exceptions**.
2. Say yes. It is taken away straight away.

## See all exceptions
1. Press **Exceptions**. The number shows how many there are.
2. Each row shows the person, what they may do, why, who added it and when it ends.
3. An exception ending within a week is marked "ends in ... days".

## See what has changed
1. Press **History**.
2. It lists the last 100 changes: when, what changed, and who did it.

## Good to know
- Changes reach people the next time a page opens.
- An exception can only add a permission. It never takes one away.
- Some actions are marked "Set in code today: moves here later". They are shown so you know who can do them, but cannot be changed here yet.
- **Safeguarding** and **Pastoral care** are not set here. Use the link on each card to go to [Safeguarding settings](safeguarding-settings.html) or [Teams & groups](teams-admin.html).
- Every change, exception added and exception removed goes into the history.
- If an action has no ticks, only master admins (and any exceptions) may do it.

- The church office, if you are not sure who should be allowed something.

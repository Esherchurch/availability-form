# Rota

This page shows the church's rota, so you can see who is serving and when. You can also download the rota as a PDF. You cannot change the rota here.

## Who can use it
- Anyone on one of the church's teams, anyone who runs a team, and master admins.
- You see the slots for your own teams. If you are on two teams, you see both. Master admins see everything.
- Anyone on no team is told the page is for people who serve on one of the church's teams, and to ask a team leader to add them.

## Read the rota
1. The rota is grouped by term. The page opens at the next date.
2. Each date shows the day, time, service and its slots. **---** means nobody is on that slot yet.
3. Press a term's name, or **Hide** / **Show**, to fold it away or open it again.
4. "Nothing on this rota for your team yet" means your team has no slots in that term yet.

## See past dates
1. Press **Show past dates** at the top.
2. Press **Hide past dates** to hide them again. Your choice is remembered on this phone or computer.

## Download the full rota
1. Press **Full Rota PDF**.
2. You get every upcoming date for your teams.

## Download your own rota
1. Pick your name in the list at the top (**Choose a name**).
2. Press **Individual PDF** for the dates you are on.
3. Press **Household PDF** for the dates anyone in your household is on.
4. Press **Wall Planner** for a calendar, one month per page, for your household. Good for the fridge.

## Good to know
- The PDFs only show dates from today onwards.
- The PDFs carry the church's logo from Church settings. If no logo is set, they have none. The full rota's file is named after the church.
- A date with no start time set shows no time.
- The PDFs are the same as the ones sent out with the term's rota email.
- The name list shows people on your teams, and you.
- The rota updates by itself when a leader changes it.

## Who to ask
- Your team leader, if your slot is wrong or you cannot serve.
- The church office, if your household is wrong in the PDFs.

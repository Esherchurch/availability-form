# Your services

This page sets the kinds of service and meeting your rota offers, and the people a service names, such as who leads it and who preaches. Each service keeps its own rota, plan and building.

## Who can use it
- The church office and master admins can open it and change everything on it.
- Anyone else is told they cannot open the page.
- It is also the "Your services" step when a new church is being set up.

## Add a kind of service or meeting
1. Under **Kinds of service and meeting**, type a name in the box, such as "10am Parish Eucharist".
2. Press **Add**.
3. Or press one of the ideas under the box, such as **+ Morning Prayer**.
4. Press **Save** at the foot of the page.

## Set up a service
1. On its card, check the name.
2. Tick **A service** if it should show on What's on, the preaching plan and the planners. Leave it unticked for a meeting or practice.
3. Fill in **Team arrives** and **Ends**. For a service, also fill in **Starts (public)**.
4. If the church has more than one building, choose the **Usual building**.
5. Under **The teams it needs**, tick the teams this service needs.
6. Under **Rota places it starts with**, type the rota places, with a comma between each.
7. Press **Save**.

## Change the order
1. Press **↑** on a card to move it up.
2. The order here is the order the rota offers them.
3. The first service is the one "Populate term" fills each Sunday.
4. Press **Save**.

## Add a role a service names
1. Under **The roles a service names**, type a role, such as "Intercessor".
2. Press **Add**, or press one of the ideas, such as **+ Preacher**.
3. Leave **On the slides** ticked if the role should go on the team slides. Untick it if not.
4. Press **Save**.

## Remove a service or role
1. Press **Remove** on its card.
2. Press **Save** to keep the change.

## Good to know
- Nothing is kept until you press **Save**. If you leave the page first, your changes are lost.
- When saved, it says "Saved. New services on the rota use these from now on."
- If something needs fixing, the page lists it in red next to **Save**.
- Roles are people typed on each service, not rota places.
- If nothing has been saved yet, the page shows starting lists for you to change.

## Who to ask
- The church office.
- A master admin, if you cannot open the page.

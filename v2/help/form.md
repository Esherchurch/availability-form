# Form

This page is where you fill in a form the church has sent you, such as a consent or medical form for an event. You reach it from the link in your email.

## Who can use it
- Anyone with the link from their email can fill it in.
- Some forms are only for attenders, church members or a team. Then you must sign in first. The page says who the form is for, with **Sign in to fill it in**.
- If you are signed in as someone the form is not for, the page says so.
- If the link is not complete or the form cannot be found, the page says so.

## Fill in the form
1. Open the link in your email.
2. Answer the questions. A star (*) means you must answer it.
3. Where a section is for each child or person, press **Add another** to add one more, or **Remove** to take one off.
4. To upload a file, choose a PDF or a picture, up to 10 MB.
5. To sign, type your full name and tick the box under it.
6. Press **Send the form**.
7. If something is missing, the page tells you what. Fix it and press **Send the form** again.

## When the church already has your answers
1. You may see **What we have on file**, with the answers you sent before.
2. If nothing has changed, press **Yes, all still correct**.
3. If something has changed, press **Something has changed**. Your answers are filled in for you, apart from health and medical details, which you give again.
4. Make the changes and press **Send the form**.

## See what you sent
1. After sending, you get an email with a link to see what you sent. Keep it: only you have it.
2. Open that link to see your answers, when they were sent, how long they are valid, and when they will be deleted.
3. The thank-you screen may also have a **See what you sent** button that does the same.

## Good to know
- Answers marked with a lock, such as health, allergies or needs, are kept apart. Only the people responsible for safeguarding see them.
- Health and medical details are not shown on the "on file" screen. The link in your "form received" email shows them.
- The top of the form says how long your answers are valid for.
- A form can only be sent once from each link. If it has been sent, the page says so.

## Who to ask
- The person who asked you to fill in the form.
- The church office, if the link does not work or you think the form should be for you.

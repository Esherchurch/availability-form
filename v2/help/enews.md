# E-news

This is where the church's weekly e-news and other emails to the church are started, scheduled and sent. It also holds the subscriber list, the e-news look and footer, the templates and the welcome emails.

## Who can use it
- The church office and master admins can open it and do everything on it.
- The e-news has to be switched on for the church first. If it is off, the page says so. A master admin can turn it on in Church settings, under "What does your church use?".
- Anyone else is told "This page is for the church office and master admins."

## Write this week's e-news
1. Open this page. If nothing has been started since Monday, the hub drafts this week's e-news for you and opens it in the editor.
2. Or press **New e-news**. The hub fills it in from What's On (about the next two weeks), this Sunday's services from the rota, and the notices showing now.
3. In the editor, add this week's picture and verse, check the words, then choose **Done** and **Schedule it** to come back here.

## Send yourself a test
1. Open the e-news. Under **Try it first**, press **Send me a test**.
2. The church members' copy goes to your own email address only.
3. If there is a yellow note ("Add this week's picture..."), fill in what it asks for in the editor first, or remove that block.

## Schedule it
1. On the **Send** card, choose the day and time under **When to send**.
2. Press **Schedule** and say yes to the question.
3. It now shows **Scheduled**. You can still change it until then, and your changes are sent too.
4. To stop it, press **Don't send yet (back to a draft)**. Nothing will be sent.

## Preview and share the web version
1. On a draft or scheduled e-news, press **Preview web version**.
2. You get a link only people you send it to will know. Use **Open**, **Copy link** or **Share to WhatsApp**.
3. Once sent, the same link shows the web version. A sent e-news also has its own **Share to WhatsApp** button.

## Send a one-off email
1. Press **New email** and pick a template. **Ours** are the church's own; the others are starters every church has.
2. For **Event invitation**, pick the event from What's On, or press **Choose later**.
3. It opens in the editor. Fill in anything in [square brackets].
4. On the Send card, choose **Who it goes to**: **E-news list**, **Church members**, **A team or group** (then choose which), or **People you pick** (then **Pick people**).
5. Schedule it as above.
- **Church members' meeting** starts a members-only notice with the next members' meeting already in it.

## Manage templates
1. In the editor, **Done** then **Save as a template** keeps an email as a template.
2. Here, **Templates** lists them, with **Use**, **Rename**, **Copy** and **Archive**.
3. Put **{date}** in a template's subject and it becomes the event's or meeting's date (or that day) when an email is started from it.

## Add a subscriber by hand
1. Press **Subscribers**.
2. **Add from the address book**: search names, or choose a whole team or group, tick people, then say **How did they say yes?**
3. **Add someone else**: type their email, first name if you like, how they said yes, and an optional note. Tick **Special notices too** if they want those.
4. Press **Done**. Only add people who have asked.

## Look and footer
1. Press **Look and footer**.
2. Choose the **Starting look**, change any colours, and fill in the footer: short name, address, charity number, phone, office hours and social links.
3. **Back to the look's own colours** undoes your colour changes. Press **Done** to save. It applies to every e-news.

## Welcome emails
1. Press **Welcome emails**. There are two: **New subscriber welcome** and **Newcomer welcome**.
2. Press **Write it** or **Edit the words** to write each one in the editor.
3. Tick **Send it by itself** once it is ready. It can only be ticked when nothing is left to fill in.

## Good to know
- Sending to subscribers opens when the church goes live on the new hub. Until then, a test goes to you only.
- A sent e-news can be looked at but never changed.
- Church members, and people on a team, get the members-only blocks. Everyone else, and the web version, never see them.
- Notices come in as members-only until someone changes that in the editor.
- Each person gets their own unsubscribe link. People who unsubscribe are kept as "Unsubscribed" so they are never emailed again. Only they can sign up again.
- Under-18s are never emailed, and are not offered when adding from the address book.
- When, how and who added each subscriber is kept with their record.
- Events missing a picture or write-up are listed; add them in What's On and they come in here.

## Who to ask
- Whoever looks after the e-news, or the church office.
- A master admin, if the e-news is switched off or you can't open the page.

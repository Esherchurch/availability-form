# I'm new here

This is a simple welcome form for visitors. A newcomer says hello, and can ask for someone from the church to get in touch. The church's welcomers see each hello here.

## Who can use it
- Anyone can fill in the form. Visitors do not need to sign in.
- The welcomers the church chose, and master admins, also see the **Hellos** below the form.
- Only master admins see **Who hellos go to**.
- If the church does not use this page, it says "Not available."

## Say hello (for visitors)
1. Type **Your name**.
2. Choose **Yes please** if you would like someone to get in touch, or **No thanks, just saying hello**.
3. Give an **Email** or a phone number. One is enough.
4. Add anything you would like to tell us or ask, if you like. You can press **Speak** and say it instead of typing.
5. Press **Send**. You see a thank-you message.

## See the hellos (for welcomers)
1. Open this page while signed in.
2. Under **Hellos**, the newest is at the top. Each says "would like contact" or "just hello", with their email or phone and message.
3. When you have been in touch, press **Mark contacted**.

## Choose who sees the hellos (master admins)
1. Under **Who hellos go to**, start typing a name in **Add someone**.
2. Pick the person and press **Add**.
3. To take someone off, press **Remove** next to their name.

## Choose where hellos are emailed (master admins)
1. Type an address in **Email them to**.
2. Press **Save the address**.
3. Leave it empty to use the church's enquiry address.

## Good to know
- Each hello is also emailed to the address the church chose, so nobody has to open the hub to see it.
- Nothing is kept until the visitor presses **Send**, and only what they send is kept.
- Before the church goes live on the new hub, the form shows but **Send** is closed. It says when sending opens, and what to do if someone is in danger.
- Every save says whether it worked.

## Who to ask
- The church's welcome team, or the church office.
- A master admin, to be added as a welcomer.

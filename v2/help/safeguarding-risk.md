# Safeguarding risks

This is the church's safeguarding risk register. It holds the standing risks of each activity, how they are controlled, and who looks after them. It holds risks, controls and dates only: never a child, a family or a concern.

## Who can use it
- The safeguarding lead and deputy keep the register for their own site.
- The trustees and master admins can read it, take snapshots and record review items. They see "Read only".
- A leader of a group that is in the register sees that group's risks only, read only.
- Anyone else is told who the page is for.
- Safeguarding, and the register, must be switched on for the church in Church settings.

## Move around the register
1. If the church has more than one site, choose it under **Site**.
2. Use the tabs: **Dashboard**, **Standing risks**, **Event assessments**, **Warnings**, **Annual review** and **Snapshots**.

## Read the dashboard
1. Open **Dashboard**. It shows dials for leaders' checks, sessions with enough leaders, consent forms, event assessments and incident follow-ups.
2. It also lists what is still needed for this year's annual review.
3. Press **Work them out now** to bring the numbers up to date.

## Add an activity
1. Open **Standing risks**.
2. Under **Add an activity**, press one of the church's own groups to add it.
3. Or choose the **Kind**, type a **Name**, and press **Add it**.

## Add or change a risk
1. On an activity, press **Add a risk**, or **Edit** on a risk already there.
2. Fill in **What could happen** and **Who could be harmed**.
3. Choose **How likely** and **How bad**, from 1 to 5.
4. Tick any **Controls the hub watches**, and write **Other controls, in words**.
5. Choose how likely and how bad it is after the controls.
6. Add an **Owner** and a **Review by** date.
7. Add anything **Still to do**, with who and by when.
8. Press **Save**. Tick **Closed** to close a risk. It is kept, not deleted.

## Use the starter list
1. On an activity, press **Add from the starter list**.
2. Untick any you do not want. Ones already added are marked.
3. Press **Add the ticked ones**, then change each one to fit how your church runs it.

## Link an incident to an activity
1. On **Standing risks**, **Incidents to link to an activity** lists this year's incidents. Only the date and the event show.
2. Choose the activity, and press **Link**. That activity's risks are then marked to look at again.

## Check the warnings
1. Open **Warnings**. Each one says **Act now** or **Soon**, and what to do.
2. The safeguarding lead can press **Go to it** to fix it.
3. Press **Refresh now** to work them out again.

## Event assessments
1. Open **Event assessments**. It lists coming events at this site that need a safeguarding sign-off, and where each has got.
2. The lead presses **Open the event's assessment** to write or sign it.
3. Press **Read it** to read one. A trustee can say the trustees have noted it.

## The annual review
1. Open **Annual review**. Each item says who records it.
2. Press **Record it**, add a note if you like, and press **Record it** again.

## Take a dated snapshot (trustees)
1. Open **Snapshots** and press **Take a snapshot**.
2. Add a note if you like, and press **Take it**.
3. Press **Open it** to read an old one, or **Print** it.

## Good to know
- Use general words only. Never name a child, a family or a concern.
- **Print / save as PDF** on **Standing risks** prints the whole register.
- Room hazards (trips, stairs, the kitchen) are in the building's own health and safety assessment. **Standing risks** shows its colour and review date, with a link for those who look after health and safety.
- A risk past its review date shows **Review overdue**.
- Warnings are counts only. Names stay on the pages that fix them.

## Who to ask
- The church's safeguarding lead or deputy.
- A master admin, if the register is switched off.

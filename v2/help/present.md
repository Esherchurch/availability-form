# Present

This page shows a group session's slides, Bible verses, pictures and quiz. A leader presents from their phone or laptop. Everyone else follows on their own phone. The TV view shows the slides full screen on the TV.

## Who can use it
- A group's leaders can present its sessions. Master admins can too.
- The group's team can open a session and follow along, but cannot present.
- Anyone else is told the session belongs to a group they are not part of.

## Find a session
1. Open **Present** from the Menu.
2. You see your groups' sessions, the next ones first.
3. Press **Open** on the one you want.
4. If there are none, plan one in the session planner with slides, verses or pictures to show.

## Present a session
1. Open the session and press **Present**.
2. Everyone following sees the first slide straight away.
3. You see the slide everyone sees, **Up next**, **Your notes** and a timer.
4. Press **Next slide** to move on, or the back arrow to go back. You can also tap any small slide picture to jump to it.
5. Arrow keys, Space, Page Down or a clicker also work on a laptop.

## Show it on the TV
1. On the computer connected to the TV, open the session, then press **Open TV view** (bottom left). Leaders see this button.
2. A new window shows the slides full screen, with nothing else on it.
3. Present from your phone or laptop as usual. The TV follows you.
4. If it says to click for sound, click once on the TV screen.

## Blank the screen
1. Press **Blank the screen** (or the B key) when you want people's attention.
2. Press **Show the slide again** to bring it back.

## Play a video in the slides
1. On a slide with a video, press **Play**. It plays in step on every screen.
2. Press **Pause** or **Start again** as you need.
3. Sound is off on phones. Press **Sound off here** to turn it on for your screen.
4. If the video is only a link, press **Open the video**.

## Hand over or stop
1. Another leader can press **Take over presenting**. You see who has it.
2. When you are done, press **Stop presenting**.

## Follow on your phone
1. Open the session. If nobody is presenting yet, it says so and waits.
2. When the leader presents, your phone shows their slide and moves with them.
3. Press **Browse on my own** to look back through the slides.
4. Press **Back to presenter** to catch up again.

## Run a quiz
1. When you reach a quiz in the session, choose how to play.
2. **Play in teams**: no phones. Name the teams (**+ Add a team** if you need more), tick the bonus point if you like, then **Start: question 1**. Tap each team that got it right, then **Show the answer on the TV** and **Next question**.
3. **Play on phones**: a code and a QR code show on the TV. People join on their phones with a nickname. Press **Start: question 1**, then **Show the answer now**, **Show the leaderboard** and **Next question**.
4. To remove a rude name, tap it under **Who's playing**, then **Remove**.
5. Press **End the quiz and delete everything** when you finish.

## Good to know
- The whole session is fetched when it opens, so slides change at once even on weak Wi-Fi.
- If the Wi-Fi drops, a screen keeps its slide and catches up when it is back.
- If a session has nothing to show yet, add slides, verses or a picture to it in the session planner.
- **How to present** opens a two-minute guide.
- Phone quiz nicknames, answers and scores are deleted when you end the quiz. A quiz nobody ends is deleted about a day after it started.
- A quiz on phones can also be run from ChurchShow in Lesson mode, on the computer in the room. It needs the group's building set in the session planner (**Meets in**), the same as for its lessons.

## Who to ask
- The group's leader, if you cannot see a session.
- A master admin, if you should be a leader of the group but are not.

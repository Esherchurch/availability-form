# My evacuation plan

If you would need help to get out of the building in a fire, a short plan here means the people on duty know what to do. The plan is yours. You can change it or delete it whenever you like.

## Who can use it
- Anyone in the church's address book can make their own plan.
- A master admin can also enter a plan for someone else, with that person's agreement.
- If you are not in the address book, the page asks you to talk to the church office.
- Building compliance has to be switched on for the church. If it is off, the page says so.

## Make your plan
1. Under **What help would you need to get out quickly?**, say what help you need. For example, "I cannot use stairs."
2. If you like, fill in **Who helps?**, **Where do they go?** and **Any equipment?**
3. Tick the box to agree that the people on duty may see the plan.
4. Press **Make the plan**.

## Change your plan
1. Open the page. Your plan is already filled in.
2. Change what you need to.
3. Press **Save the plan**.

## Check your plan once a year
1. Each plan has a date to review it by. You see it under the buttons.
2. When that date has passed, a note asks you to check the plan is still right.
3. Check it and press **Save the plan** again. This sets the next date a year on.

## Delete your plan
1. Press **Delete the plan (no longer needed)**.
2. Say yes to the question. The plan is gone.

## Enter a plan for someone else (master admins)
1. Under **Whose plan?**, choose the person. **My own** goes back to yours.
2. Fill in the plan with them, and tick to say they have agreed.
3. Press **Make the plan** or **Save the plan**.
4. The plan then says it was entered by you with their agreement.

## Good to know
- Please do not put medical details in the plan.
- Only you and master admins can open your plan here.
- When you are in the building, the fire wardens and the duty stewards on duty for that building see it on the fire roll-call. Nobody else sees it.
- It is never sent by email.
- Every change, and every delete, is kept in a log.
- Until the hub goes live, plans cannot be made here, because the hub is not yet private. Tell a steward or the church office in person instead.
- Under-18s are not offered in the **Whose plan?** list.

## Who to ask
- The church office, if you are not in the address book or need help with your plan.
- A master admin, if you would like them to enter a plan for you.

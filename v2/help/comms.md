# Emails sent

A list of every email the hub sent for the church, and any that failed: sign-up confirmations, bookings, forms, reminders, and the emails sent from events.

## Who can use it
- Master admins only.
- Anyone else who opens it sees "This page is for master admins."
- The page only shows the list. Nothing can be changed or deleted here.

## See what was sent
1. Open the page. It shows the last 30 days, newest first.
2. To look further back or less far, use **When** and choose **The last week**, **The last 30 days** or **The last 3 months**.
3. The line at the top sums it up: how many emails, to how many people, and how many failed.
4. Each row shows **When**, **What** (with the subject under it), **To**, **Sent by** and **Result**.

## Find the ones that failed
1. Tick **Only the ones that failed**.
2. Each failed row says **Failed**, with the reason under it.
3. Untick it to see everything again.

## Download the list
1. Choose the time and, if you like, tick **Only the ones that failed**.
2. Press **Download**.
3. You get a spreadsheet file: "Emails sent.csv", or "Email errors.csv" when only failures are shown. It opens in Excel or similar.

## Reading the list
- **To** shows the first person's address and "and N more", or just how many people.
- **Sent by** is the person who sent it. "The hub" means the hub sent it by itself, such as a reminder or a confirmation.
- **Result** is **Sent**, **Failed**, or **Not sent (test)** for an email that was only a trial and never went out.
- Emails from events show what kind they were: an invitation, an invitation reminder, an event cancelled, a checklist task given, or an event email, with the event's name when it is known.

## Good to know
- The list gives the outline of each email (when, what, who, the result), never the email itself.
- It can't show more than the last 3 months at once.
- **Download** gives the same rows you can see: the time chosen, and only the failed ones if that is ticked.
- If the list won't load, the reason is shown in red.

## Who to ask
- Another master admin.
- The church office, about a particular email that went out or failed.

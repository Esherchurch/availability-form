# Hire our rooms

This is the church's public page for hiring rooms. People say what they need, and it shows the rooms that fit and, if they give a time, the ones that are free.

## Who can use it
- Anyone can open it. There is no need to sign in.
- It only shows rooms that are open for hire.

## Find a room that fits
1. Under **What do you need?**, fill in **How many people**.
2. Choose **Set out as** if you want a certain layout, or leave **Any layout**.
3. Tick any facilities you need.
4. The rooms that fit are shown as cards, with their size, how many they hold and their facilities.
5. Rooms that do not fit are listed under **Not shown**, with the reason.

## Find a room that is free
1. Choose a date in **On (optional)**.
2. Choose the times in **From** and **Until**.
3. Only rooms free for all of that time are shown as fitting. The others say "booked then".
4. Choose a room card to go straight to the booking form, with the date and times filled in.

## Look at a room
1. With no date and times chosen, choose a room card to open that room's own page.
2. It shows photos, sizes, facilities and when it is free.

## Compare rooms
1. Press **Compare rooms side by side**.
2. Two rooms are shown next to each other. You can change which rooms.
3. Press the button again to hide it.

## Good to know
- If no room fits, the page says so. Try asking for fewer things, or ask the church.
- Prices depend on the room and the time. They are given when you ask to book.
- The church's picture at the top appears only if one has been set up.

## Who to ask
- The church office, about hiring a room or prices.

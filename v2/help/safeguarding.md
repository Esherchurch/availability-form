# Safeguarding

This is where the church keeps each event safe: who is leading, whether there are enough leaders, their DBS checks and training, incidents and accidents, and concerns. Anyone signed in can report a concern from here.

## Who can use it
- Anyone signed in can open the page and press **Report a concern**.
- The front page shows events coming up in the next month. Admins see all of them. Leaders see the events they lead. The safeguarding lead and deputy see their own site's events.
- An event's own page is for its leaders, admins and the safeguarding lead. Anyone else is told so, and can still report a concern.
- Only the safeguarding lead and deputy see the concerns reported to them.
- The safeguarding lead, deputy and admins also see buttons for **Safeguarding settings** and the **Safeguarding risk register**. Master admins see **Due for deletion**.

## Report a concern
1. Press **Report a concern**.
2. If you are not on an event, choose **Where**.
3. Fill in **Who it is about (if you know)**, and **What you saw, heard or were told, in the words used, and when**.
4. Press **Send to the safeguarding lead**.
5. It goes to the safeguarding lead only. You cannot read it back, so say everything now.
- If a child or adult is in danger right now, call 999.

## Read and note concerns (safeguarding lead and deputy)
1. Open the page. **Concerns reported to you** lists each one, marked **New** or **Seen**.
2. Press **Mark seen and add notes**, write **What you have done**, and press **Save**.
3. Press **Notes** later to add more.

## Open an event
1. Under **Coming up**, find the event and press **Open**.
2. The top shows the event, with **Check-in** (for admins and leaders) and **Report a concern**.

## Add leaders and helpers
1. On the **Leaders** card, press **Add**.
2. Type a name under **Find by name**, then press **Leader** or **Helper**.
3. Or press **From the rota**, tick the people on the rota that day, and press **Add the ticked people**.
4. Press the cross next to a name to take them off.
- Only people who have signed in can be found with **Add**.

## Check there are enough leaders
1. The tiles show **Children booked**, **Under 8**, **Leaders** and **Leaders needed**.
2. A green bar says there are enough. An amber bar says how many more are needed.
3. A child whose consent form has not come in is counted, but not as under 8.

## Set the event's ratios and rules
1. On the **Leaders** card, fill in **One leader for every … children** and **… and for every … under-8s**.
2. Set how many years a DBS check and training count for.
3. Tick **Works with under-18s** for a kids club or youth event. Then every leader and helper must have an in-date DBS check and training.
4. Or tick **Every leader must have an in-date DBS check and training**.
5. Press **Save**.

## Record a leader's checks (safeguarding lead)
1. Next to a leader, press **Checks**.
2. Choose the **DBS check** status and the **Level of the DBS check**.
3. Fill in the dates: DBS seen, training, a church leader's reference, and the confidential declaration.
4. Press **Save**. Never record the DBS certificate number, or a copy of it.

## Someone not cleared yet
1. On an under-18s event, a person without in-date checks is marked **not cleared yet**, or is not added.
2. Bring their checks up to date, then press **Check again**.
3. Or the safeguarding lead can press **Exception**, give **The reason**, and press **Record the exception**. It is kept with their name.

## Share medical details with the leaders
1. Some families say their consent form is still correct. Their medical details came from an earlier event.
2. The safeguarding lead sees **Medical details to share with the leaders**. Press **Share with this event's leaders**.

## Record an incident or accident
1. On **Incidents and accidents**, press **Record one**.
2. Fill in **When**, **Who was involved** and **What happened**.
3. Say whether first aid was given, and by whom, and whether a parent or carer was told.
4. Press **Save**.
5. The safeguarding lead can press **Follow-up** to say what was done afterwards, and **Download** to save the list.

## The safeguarding assessment
Trips, overnights, holiday clubs and off-site events that work with under-18s need one. Bookings open only once it is signed off.
1. Choose a template under **Start from a template** and press **Start the assessment**. Or describe the event and press **Draft it** for a first draft by AI. Check every line of it.
2. If the host sent their own requirements, add the file, their web pages or the words, and press **Read the requirements**. Check the questions, then press **Confirm these as required questions**.
3. Answer every question on the checklist, or tick **Not applicable** and say why.
4. Check each risk: how likely, how bad, and what you do about it. Press **Add a risk** for more, and **Add something still to do** for open jobs.
5. Press **Save**, then **Ready for sign-off**.
6. The safeguarding lead or deputy presses **Sign it off**. Bookings then open.
- **Print / save as PDF** gives a copy to keep.
- The safeguarding lead or a master admin can open **Not needed for this event?** and press **It isn't needed**, with a reason.

## Good to know
- Use general words only. Never name a child, a family or a medical detail in an assessment.
- Changing a signed assessment sends it back for sign-off, and bookings close until then.
- **DBS checks and training running out** lists the next six weeks, and any already run out.
- If you help with under-18s, **Your checks** shows your own dates.
- Someone whose check has run out cannot be added to anything that works with under-18s.

## Who to ask
- The church's safeguarding lead or deputy.
- The church office, if no safeguarding lead is set for your site.

# Drivers and vehicles

This page shows who may drive for the church, and the vehicles it uses. It keeps the licence and insurance checks for each driver, the dates for each vehicle, and the check you do before each journey.

## Who can use it
- Anyone signed in can see the **Vehicles** tab and record a walk-round check.
- The people the church names to keep driver records, and master admins, also see the **Drivers** tab.
- The church office and master admins also see the **Settings** tab, and can add and change vehicles.
- Everyone else sees only who is cleared to drive, and until when. They never see the driver records.

## Check a vehicle before you drive
1. On the **Vehicles** tab, find the vehicle and press **Walk-round check**.
2. Tick each thing that is fine: tyres, lights, mirrors, seatbelts, doors, fuel, first aid kit, and the wheelchair lift if it has one.
3. Add anything else under **Anything else (optional)**.
4. Press **Record it**.
5. Anything not ticked is recorded as a problem. Tell the office, and don't drive if it isn't safe.

## Read a vehicle's dates
1. Each vehicle shows its MOT, insurance, road tax and service dates.
2. Its label says **In date**, **Check the dates** or **Must not be used**.
3. It also shows the last walk-round check, who did it, and any problems.

## Add or change a vehicle (office and driver record keepers)
1. Press **Add a vehicle**, or **Change** by a vehicle.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Kind**, **Registration** and **Passenger seats**. Tick **Wheelchair accessible** if it is.
3. Fill in the date the MOT, insurance and road tax run out, and when the next service is due.
4. Press **Save**. **No longer used** takes it off the list.

## Add a driver (driver record keepers)
1. Open the **Drivers** tab.
2. Under **Add a driver**, choose the person.
3. Fill in the form (see below) and press **Save**.

## Record a driver's checks
1. Press **Change** by the driver.
2. Tick their **Licence categories**.
3. Fill in **Licence last checked on** and **How** it was checked. Add when the photocard runs out, if it does.
4. Under **Insurance**, say if it is their own or on the church's policy. If it is their own, say when it runs out.
5. Under **May they drive the minibus?**, choose the answer that fits.
6. If the church asks for the minibus driver course, fill in when it was done and which kind.
7. Press **Save**.

## Remove a driver
1. Press **Change** by the driver.
2. Press **No longer a driver**. Their record is deleted.

## Settings (office)
1. Under **Who keeps the driver records**, use **Add someone…** to add a person. Up to 20 people. Press **×** to take someone off.
2. Tick the box if minibus drivers must have done the minibus driver course, renewed every 4 years.
3. Choose how often each licence is checked again under **Check each licence again every**.
4. Press **Save**.

## Good to know
- Each driver shows **Cleared**, **Due soon** or **Not cleared**, for cars and for the minibus, with the reason.
- Only what the checks need is kept. Never the licence number, a date of birth, an address or any points.
- Event risk assessments choose drivers and vehicles from this page.
- Under-18s are not offered as drivers.

## Who to ask
- The church office, about vehicles or a problem found on a walk-round check.
- The people who keep the driver records, about your own driver checks.

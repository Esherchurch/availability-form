# A room's page

This page shows one room: its photos, size, how many people it holds, its facilities and when it is free over the next seven days. The church website links to it for each room that can be hired.

## Who can use it
- Anyone can open it, with no need to sign in. They only see rooms that are for hire.
- Church members who come from Book a room see a members' version. It shows any room, for hire or not, and also lists the room's kit.
- The floor plan shows only to the people the room's setting allows: everyone, members and the office, or the office only.
- If a room cannot be found, or is not for hire, the page says so and points back to all the rooms.

## Look at the room
1. Click a small photo to see it large.
2. **The room** gives its floor area, size, ceiling height, where to find it, step-free access and the nearest toilets and kitchen, where these are filled in.
3. **How many it holds** gives the number for each way it can be set out, and the most allowed for fire safety.
4. **Facilities** lists what the room has, and says if you may bring your own caterers.
5. **House rules** shows any rules for the room.

## Ask to book it
1. Look at **When it is free** for the next seven days.
2. Click a free time. The booking form opens with the room, date and time filled in.
3. Or press **Ask to book this room** to open the booking form for this room.
4. On the members' version the button is **Book a room** instead, and takes you to the members' booking page.

## Ask a question
1. If the church has an email address set up, it is shown next to the button.
2. Click it to email the church about this room.

## Good to know
- Only what has been filled in for the room is shown. A blank item is left out.
- On the members' version, **Kit** shows what is in the room and what can be brought in.

## Who to ask
- The church office, about hiring a room or anything on this page.
- Whoever looks after the rooms, if a detail or photo is wrong.

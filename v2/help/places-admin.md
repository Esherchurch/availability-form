# Places

This is where the church's buildings, rooms, kit and outside venues are set up, with who approves bookings, how members' bookings work, and the prices for hiring rooms. Everything else in the hub uses these, so renaming is always safe.

## Who can use it
- The church office, the bookings admins and master admins can open it.
- Anyone else is told "This page is for the church office, the bookings admins and master admins."
- Master admins also see a **Church details** button, for the church's name, enquiry email and logo.

## Add a building
1. Open **Sites**. Type the **Name**, **Postcode** and **Address**.
2. Press **Check** to make sure the postcode can be found. It still saves if not.
3. Press **Add site**.
4. For a new church, **Seed the starting rooms** makes one site with a set of starting rooms. Rename them as you like. It will not run twice.

## Add a room
1. Open **Rooms**. Type the **Name**.
2. Choose **Kind**: **A room at a site** or **Online (a video room)**.
3. Choose the **Site**, how many it holds under **Capacity**, and a **Colour** for the calendar.
4. Tick **Step-free access**, **Members may book** and **Outside hirers may book** as they apply.
5. Press **Add room**.

## Fill in a room's profile
1. **Fill in your rooms** lists what each room still needs. Press **Fill in**, or **Profile** on the room.
2. Add **Photos**. The first is the main picture; use **Make main** to change it.
3. Fill in the size, floor, nearest toilets and kitchen, and how many it holds for each layout.
4. Give a **Fire-safety maximum**. No booking can ever be for more people than this.
5. Tick its **Facilities**, and write a **Short description** and **House rules for this room**.
6. Under **Members' bookings**, choose whether members' bookings are confirmed straight away or wait for approval.
7. Press **Save the profile**.

## A room made of other rooms
1. In a room's profile, under **Made up of other rooms**, tick its parts (at most four). An example is a hall with a folding wall.
2. Booking the whole room then takes every part, and booking any part makes the whole unavailable.

## Kit, catering and outside venues
1. **Kit**: type the **Name**, where it **Lives in**, and **How many**, or tick **Unlimited**. Press **Add kit**. You can set a hire price and tick **Can be moved to another room**.
2. **Catering**: add things people can ask for with a room, with a price **Per person** or **Per item** and the **Notice needed (working days)**. Press **Add to the menu**. Each item asked for with a booking goes on its quote: the number asked for times the price. The office checks the quote before approving and invoicing.
3. **Outside venues**: search by name or place, or fill in the boxes by hand, and press **Save venue**.

## Who approves bookings
1. Open **Who approves**. Each building is listed.
2. Choose the **Safeguarding lead** and **Safeguarding deputy**.
3. Under **Bookings admins**, choose a person and press **Add**. Press the cross to take someone off.
4. Core Team can always approve a booking, whatever is set here.

## Booking settings for each building
1. Open **Bookings**.
2. Choose whether members' bookings are confirmed straight away or wait for the office.
3. Fill in **Send booking requests and cancellations to**. Use an office address, not a person's own.
4. Tick **Email the day's setup sheet to that address each morning** if you want it.
5. Set the minutes after which a booking not marked arrived is flagged. 0 turns it off.
6. Set the opening hours used for the room use report.
7. Under **Services on the rota keep rooms busy**, press **Add a service**, fill in **What**, **From**, **To**, the days and the rooms, then **Save the services**.

## Hire prices
1. Open **Hire prices**. Under **Kinds of booking**, type a name and press **Add a kind of booking**.
2. Tick **For the public** to put it on the public booking form, and **Charged** if it is priced. Choose a **Members' rate** if members pay less.
3. Under **Terms and conditions**, choose the kind, write the terms and press **Save as a new version**.
4. Under **A room's prices**, choose the **Room** and **Kind of booking**, fill in the prices, and press **Save these prices**.

## Good to know
- **Take out of use** hides a building, room or piece of kit without deleting it. **Bring back** undoes it.
- A building or room still needed by bookings to come stays in use, and you are told why.
- Floor plans can only be put up once the church goes live on the hub.
- Hirers who accepted older terms keep the version they accepted.

## Who to ask
- A master admin, if you cannot open the page or need Church details changed.
- The church office, about prices and who approves bookings.

# Preaching plan

One table of the coming services: what each one is, who is preaching, who is leading, and who is reading what.

## Who can use it
- The Pastor, the church's leaders and master admins can change it.
- People who plan the rotas can read it but not change it.
- Anyone else is told "For the preaching team and the rota planners".
- The preaching plan has to be switched on for the church. If it is off, the page says "The church does not use the preaching plan."

## Read the plan
1. Each row is one service, for the next three months.
2. The columns are **Date**, **Description**, **Speaker**, **Service leader** and **Readers**.
3. Tick **Show past services** to see the ones already gone.
4. Where a sermon has been planned, a **Sermon plan** link under the speaker opens it.

## Change a service
1. Type in **Description**. This is what What's On shows for the service.
2. Type the **Speaker** and **Service leader**. Names come up from the address book. You can type a guest's name too.
3. Move to another box. It saves by itself and says "Saved".

## Add readers
1. Press **+ Add a reader**.
2. Type the name in **Reader** and the Bible passage in **Passage**, for example John 3:1-17.
3. Press **×** to take a reader off.

## Choose the Pastor (master admins)
1. Master admins see a card called **The Pastor** at the top.
2. Press **Choose the Pastor in Team emails** to pick them there.
3. The Pastor can then change this plan without being an admin.

## Good to know
- There is no Save button. Each box saves when you leave it.
- If a box says "That did not save", try again.
- These are the same details as the [Rota Planner](Planner.html), What's On and the app. Change one here and they all change.
- The list shows the church's kinds of service, and anything ticked to show in What's On.

## Who to ask
- The Pastor or the church's leaders.
- A master admin, if you cannot open the page or the Pastor needs changing.

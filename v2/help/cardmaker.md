# Card maker

Make a picture card for an event from What's On, or for a Bible verse, in every size you need: a GIF for the e-news, a short video for social media and the screens, or a picture for print. It also writes the caption for Instagram and Facebook posts, and gets countdown posts and a verse of the day ready for you.

## Who can use it
- The church office and master admins. Anyone else sees "This page is for the church office and master admins."
- The card maker has to be switched on for your church. If it is off, a master admin can turn it on in Church settings, under "What does your church use?".
- Kept hashtags, countdowns, the verse of the day settings and "Posted" ticks are shared: everyone who can use the page sees the same ones.

## Make a card for an event
1. Under **Make a card for**, choose **An event**.
2. In **Event from What's On**, pick the event. The list shows events from yesterday up to about three months ahead. Cancelled, unlisted and team-only events are left out. You can also choose **None (type the words below)**.
3. Choose a **Size**: E-news, Instagram (portrait), Square, Facebook, Story, Screen or A5 print.
4. Choose a **Style**: **Words on the photo**, **Colour panel beside** or **Words below the photo**.
5. Choose a **Movement**: **None**, **Gentle zoom**, **Slow pan** or **Button pulse**.
6. Under **Words**, change anything for this card only: **Small line above**, **Button words (blank for none)**, **Title**, **When and where** and **Colour**. The words start from What's On. If the event takes sign-ups, the button says "Sign up".
7. To use a different photo, press **Search Unsplash** and pick one. The photographer's credit is added to the caption for you.
8. Under **Save**, press one of the buttons (see below).

## Make a card for a Bible verse
1. Under **Make a card for**, choose **A Bible verse**.
2. Type a **Reference**, for example Psalm 23:1, and choose a Bible from the list. Bibles marked "(free)" are free to use; ones marked "(church key)" come from your church's own Bible account.
3. Press **Bring it in**, or **Today's verse** for the verse of the day.
4. You can change the words in **The verse**, and the **Copyright line** that goes on the picture where the version needs one.
5. Choose a **Design**: **Plain colour**, **On a photo**, **Framed** or **Band**.

## Save the card
- **Animated GIF (for the e-news)**: only for the E-news size.
- **Short video**: a 6-second video for the social media sizes and Screen. It saves as MP4 or WebM, depending on your browser. If your browser can't make videos, try Chrome or Safari.
- **Picture (PNG)**: a still picture, for print and anything else.
- **Save every size to the picture library**: saves a picture of the card in every size to the picture library, named and tagged with the event.
- GIFs and videos download to your device for now; they don't go into the picture library yet.

## Write a social post
1. Make the card as above.
2. In **Social post**, type a **First line**, for example "Popcorn at the ready!".
3. Check the **Hashtags**. Press **Keep these for this event** to remember them for this event, or **Keep as the church's usual** to use them by default.
4. The **Caption** is written for you: the first line, then the day, time, place and a link to sign up or find out more, then the hashtags. You can edit it. Once you edit it by hand, it stops updating by itself.
5. Press **Copy caption**, **Share** or **Download the pictures**.
   - **Share** opens your phone's own share sheet with the picture, so you can send it to Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp. Paste the caption in. Where a browser can't share pictures, the caption is copied and the picture downloads instead.
   - **Download the pictures** gives the Instagram (1080 x 1350), Square, Facebook and Story sizes.

## Make a countdown
1. Choose the event and make the card first.
2. In **Social post**, open **Make it a countdown**.
3. Tick the days you want: **7 days before**, **3 days before**, **2 days before**, **The day before** and **On the day**. Each has its own first line, which you can change.
4. Press **Save the countdown**.
5. On each of those days, the post waits in **Today's posts**, ready to go. Days that have already gone by are skipped.

## Today's posts
- At the top of the page, **Today's posts** shows any countdown posts due today and the verse of the day (if it is on).
- For each one: **Copy caption**, **Share** or **Download**.
- After you have put it on social media, press **Posted ✓** so others know it's done.

## Set up the verse of the day
1. In **Verse of the day**, tick **Have one ready each morning**.
2. Choose the **Bible** and a **Design**.
3. Tick **Use a picture from the picture library** if you want a different photo each day.
4. Add a **Credit line** if you like; it goes in the caption.
5. Tick **Email me each morning when a post is ready** if you want a reminder.
6. Press **Save**.

## Good to know
- Nothing is posted for the church automatically. You always share or download the post yourself.
- The card is made on your own device; nothing is sent anywhere until you save it to the picture library or share it.
- Movements only move the photo or the button. The words stay still, so email programs that show only the first frame of a GIF still show them.
- The words shrink to fit their space, so a long title gets smaller rather than being cut off.
- The morning reminder email is sent at about 7am, only to people who ticked it, and only when there is a post for that day.
- If an event's photo can't be loaded, the card uses a colour instead.
- The verse of the day comes from a list of well-known verses, one for each day of the year.

## Who to ask
- The church office, for what the church likes to post.
- A master admin, if the card maker is switched off or you can't open the page.

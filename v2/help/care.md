# Care & support

This page is where anyone can ask for help: pastoral care for themselves, or help for somebody they are worried about. It is a short list of choices, and whatever you choose is private.

## Who can use it
- Anyone can open it. You do not need to sign in to see the choices.
- The pages the choices lead to will ask you to sign in.
- The same choices also open as a small box from the **Care & support** button on the website bar and the hub.

## If someone is in danger
1. The first line on the page always says **If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.**
2. Do that first. The hub is not watched all the time.

## Ask for pastoral care for yourself
1. Press **I'd like some pastoral care**.
2. It opens a short form. Say as much or as little as you like.
3. It goes to the people the church has named to look after care, and stays private.

## Tell someone you are worried about another person
1. Press **I'm worried about someone**.
2. It opens a short form. You can tell the pastoral team, or the safeguarding lead.
3. Say what you noticed, and whether you are happy to be contacted.

## Ask for prayer or share a praise report
1. Press **Prayer requests and praise reports**.
2. It opens the [Prayer](prayer.html) page. There you can ask for prayer, for you or someone else, or share how God has answered.
3. This choice only shows once the church's Prayer page is ready.

## Good to know
- What you send is private. It goes only to the people the church has named for it, never to everyone.
- Safeguarding concerns go only to the safeguarding lead and deputy.
- Before the church goes live on the new hub, the forms may not save anything yet. They will say so, and say when they open. Until then, speak to a church leader in person.

## Who to ask
- A church leader, in person, for example after a Sunday service.
- The safeguarding lead, if you are worried about someone's safety.
- In an emergency, call 999.

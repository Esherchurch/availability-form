# Care & support: what has come in

This page lists the requests for pastoral care and the concerns people have raised through Care & support. It is the one place to read them and mark them as dealt with.

## Who can use it
- The pastoral team can read requests for pastoral care and pastoral concerns.
- People named under "Who gets told" in Church settings can read the kinds they are named for.
- The safeguarding lead and deputy can read everything, including safeguarding concerns.
- Nobody else can read them. Not master admins, not the person who raised it, and not the person it is about.
- Anyone else who opens the page is told it is for the people the church has named, with a link to [Care & support](care.html) if they need help themselves.
- The line at the top tells you what you can see.

## Read what has come in
1. Open the page. The newest is at the top.
2. The count at the top says how many are **still to deal with**.
3. Each one says what kind it is: **Asked for pastoral care**, **A pastoral concern** or **A safeguarding concern**.
4. It shows who it is about, what was said, who raised it, and when.
5. For a concern, it also says if the person who raised it is **happy to be contacted** or **would rather not be contacted**.
6. If someone asked for care and did not say more, it says so. Please get in touch with them.

## Mark one as dealt with
1. Under **What was done**, write a short note if you like. It is optional.
2. Press **Mark as dealt with**.
3. It now shows **Dealt with** and leaves the list.

## See the ones already dealt with
1. Tick **Show the ones dealt with**.
2. They come back into the list, with any note that was written.

## Good to know
- Safeguarding concerns go only to the safeguarding lead and deputy, never to the pastoral team.
- The note under **What was done** is seen only by people who can read this page.
- Once marked as dealt with, it cannot be changed back on this page.
- Who is named to be told is set by a master admin in Church settings, under "Who gets told". The safeguarding lead and deputy are set in Safeguarding settings.

## Who to ask
- The safeguarding lead, about anything to do with safety.
- A master admin, if you should be able to read these but cannot.

# Watch & Listen

This page is where you watch the church's services and videos and listen to sermons. It has this week's live service when there is one, the latest video and sermon, and lists of everything else.

## Who can use it

- Anyone signed in can open it.
- Full services marked for **church members** are only shown to people the church has marked as church members.
- Nobody changes anything here. The people who look after sermons manage the videos and sermons on the [Watch](watch-admin.html) and [Sermons](sermons-admin.html) pages.

## Watch the live service

1. On the day of the service, a card at the top says **Live now**.
2. Press **Watch live**. The service plays on the page.

After the service, the card may change to **Watch again** for a few days, or it may disappear. That depends on what the church has chosen.

## Listen to a sermon

1. Press **Listen** (under the latest video and sermon).
2. Press a sermon in the list. It starts playing at the top of the page, under **Now playing**.
3. To find an older one, type in **Search by speaker, book, title or date**.

Without a search, the newest 40 sermons are listed. Each one shows its series or Bible passage, the speaker and the date.

You can also press **Listen** on the **Latest sermon** card.

## Watch a video

1. Press **Watch**.
2. Under **Full services** are services put up for the church. Under **Latest videos** are the newest videos from the church's YouTube channel.
3. Press a video to play it on the page.
4. Press **The church's YouTube channel** to see the whole channel on YouTube.

You can also press **Watch** on the **Latest video** card.

## Good to know

- The page opens on Listen if there are sermons, and on Watch if there are none yet.
- If the church doesn't use Watch, only sermons are shown.
- Up to 12 of the channel's latest videos are shown here.
- New sermons from the church's podcast and new videos from its YouTube channel appear by themselves, usually within the hour.
- Videos play through YouTube. Full services are unlisted on YouTube, which means anyone who has the link can pass it on.
- If you see "Sermons will appear here once they are put up" or "Videos will appear here once the church puts them up", there is nothing there yet.
- If a sermon says "This sermon could not be loaded", try again.

## Who to ask

- If a service or sermon is missing: whoever looks after sermons, or the church office.
- If you think you should see church members' services but can't: the church office.

# Building risk assessment

This is where the church writes its own health and safety risk assessment and fire risk assessment for each building. A lead signs it, then a trustee countersigns it.

## Who can use it
- The office, the trustees and master admins can open it for every building.
- Each building's health and safety lead, deputy and fire safety lead can open it for their own building.
- Anyone else is told who the page is for.
- Building compliance has to be switched on for the church. If it is off, the page says so.
- Until the church goes live on the hub, nothing can be saved here. Keep your paper record for now.

## Choose the building and the assessment
1. If there is more than one building, press its name at the top.
2. Press **Health and safety** or **Fire**.
3. **The live risk register** takes you to the building's register. **Print** gives a copy.

## Fill in the assessment
1. Under **About this assessment**, fill in **Carried out by**, **On** and **Review by**.
2. Work through each numbered step. Each one has **Notes** to write in.
3. Some steps list the building's checks, with their colours.
4. Press **Save** often. Your changes are not kept until you do.

## Add hazards
1. Press **Add the starter hazards** for a ready-made list, with this building's checks as their controls.
2. Or press **+ Add a hazard**.
3. Fill in **What could go wrong** and **Who could be hurt, and how**.
4. Set the likelihood and severity, before and after the controls. The hub works out the rating.
5. Choose **Controls: the building's checks**, and add **Other controls, in words** and **Who looks after it**.
6. **Take this hazard off** removes one.

## Add actions
1. Press **+ Add an action**.
2. Fill in **What needs doing**, the **Priority**, **By** and **Who**. The date follows the priority.
3. Press **Make it a maintenance job** to put it on the Maintenance list as a safety job.
4. Tick **Done** when it is done.

## Get a first draft from the AI
1. Describe the building in the box, and press **Get a first draft**.
2. The draft is marked as by AI. Check every line, then press **Save**.
3. A suggested answer to a question has **Use it** next to it.

## Read a professional's report
1. Under **Read a professional's report**, press **Choose the report and read it**. A PDF, a Word file or a photo of each page will do.
2. Check what was read. Untick anything wrong, and set any ratings it asks for.
3. Press **Add the ticked lines to the assessment**.

## Answer every question
1. Under **Before it is signed: every question answered**, answer each one.
2. If a question does not apply, write "N/A: " and the reason.

## Sign it and countersign it
1. Once every question is answered, the lead presses **Sign it as the …**.
2. A trustee who did not sign it presses **Countersign as a trustee**. That version is then kept as it is.
3. To change it later, press **Change it**. This starts a new version, to be signed again.

## Good to know
- The hub helps you record the assessment. The responsible person remains responsible for it.
- For a complex building, or if you are unsure, a professional should carry out or check it.
- The banner at the top says if the review is due soon or overdue.
- People's own evacuation plans are counted here, never named.

## Who to ask
- The building's health and safety lead or fire safety lead.
- The church office.

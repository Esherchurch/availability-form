# Your place

This page shows one booking you have made for a church event. You reach it from the link in your confirmation email.

## Who can use it
- Anyone with the link from their confirmation email. You do not need to sign in.
- If the link is not complete, the page says so. Use the whole link from the email.
- If the booking cannot be found, the page says so and offers **See what's on**.

## Check your booking
1. Open the link in your confirmation email.
2. You see the event, when and where it is, who booked, the email address, how many places, and who is coming.
3. A note at the top tells you if you are on the waiting list, if your place was cancelled, or if the event itself was cancelled.

## Check in at the door
1. Under **Checking in**, there is a code for each person on the booking.
2. Show the code at the door. A screenshot works too.
3. The codes are not shown once the event is over, or if the place is cancelled or on the waiting list.

## Pay for your place
1. If something is owed, you see **To pay** or **To pay on the day**.
2. Where the church takes payment online, press **Pay by card** or **Pay by bank**. The button shows the amount.
3. When you come back, the page says it is confirming your payment. It shows **Paid** once it is through.
4. If your place is only held for a while, the page says until when.

## Add it to your calendar
1. Press **Add to calendar**.
2. Open the file it gives you to put the event in your own calendar.

## Cancel your place
1. Press **Cancel my place**.
2. Your place is given back. You can sign up again while there is room.
3. If there is no cancel button, the event does not allow it. Reply to your confirmation email to change the booking.

## A series of dates
1. If you booked several dates at once, **The whole series** lists them all. Press a date to see its own page.
2. Each date has its own place and its own check-in code.
3. **Cancel all remaining dates** cancels every date still to come, after you say yes.

## My usual people
1. If you are signed in, **My usual people** lists the names offered when you sign up again.
2. Press **Remove** to take someone off. Press **Put back** to bring them back.

## Good to know
- You cannot cancel once the event is over.
- Signed in, there is a link to **My messages**, which shows what the church has emailed you.
- Press **What's on** to see other events.

## Who to ask
- Reply to your confirmation email about this booking.
- The church office, if the link does not work.

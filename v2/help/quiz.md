# Join the quiz

This is the page people use to play a quiz on their own phone. The leader shows a code on the TV, and each person joins with a nickname.

## Who can use it
- Anyone in the room with the code. No account and nothing to sign.

## Join
1. Scan the QR code on the TV, or go to the web address it shows.
2. Type **The code on the screen**, if it is not already filled in.
3. Type **Your first name or a nickname**.
4. Press **Join**. You see "You're in!".

## Answer a question
1. The question and four answers show on your phone, with a countdown.
2. Tap your answer. On a computer you can press A to D, or 1 to 4.
3. You get one answer per question. Then look up at the screen.

## See how you did
1. When the leader shows the answer, your phone says if you were right, and your points.
2. A quick right answer scores more.
3. Your place and score show, then the top five on the leaderboard.

## Good to know
- Your nickname, answers and score are deleted when the leader ends the quiz. If nobody ends it, they are deleted about a day after it started.
- If the leader removes your name, you are asked to join again with another name.
- When the quiz ends, press **Join another quiz** to play again.

## Who to ask
- The leader running the quiz.

# Address book

This is the church's list of people. Here you add someone, change their details, put them on teams and groups, and archive them when they leave.

## Who can use it
- The church office and master admins can open it, see everyone and change anyone. The church office is whichever team does the office job in Teams & groups.
- Anyone who is an admin for a team can open it too. They see only the people on the teams they look after, and can only change those people.
- A team admin can only tick or untick their own teams. Other teams' boxes are greyed out.
- Only a master admin can change **Admin for**, **Master admin**, **UNDER 16** and **UNDER 18**.
- Anyone else is told they cannot open the page.

## Find someone
1. Type in the search box (**Search by name, email or team**). Typing a team's name finds the people on it.
2. The list shows only the people who match. If nobody does, it says "Nobody matches that."
3. Tick **Show archived** to see people who have been archived as well.

## Add a person
1. On a computer, the **Member entry** form is on the left. On a phone or tablet, press **+ Add a person** to open it.
2. Fill in **Full Name** and **Email Address**. Both are needed.
3. **Max / Mo** is the most times a month they can be put on a rota. It starts at 2.
4. **Household**: pick the head of their family, or leave it on **None / New Household**.
5. Tick their **Teams** and any **Groups**.
6. Tick anything else that fits (see below), then press **Add to Address Book**.

## Change someone's details
1. Find them and press **Edit** on their row.
2. The form fills with their details. Change what you need.
3. Press **Update Member Details** to save.
4. On a phone, press **Close** to go back to the list without saving.

## Teams, groups and admins
- **Teams** puts someone on a team's rota and pages.
- **Groups** appears only when the church has groups. A person joins a group the same way as a team.
- **Admin for** says which teams a person looks after: its pages, resources and people. Leave it blank for most people.
- **Most per team — optional** lets you limit how often one team can use someone. It lists the church's teams that have a rota. **Max / Mo** is still the total across everything. Blank means no separate limit.

## Ticks about the person
- **Church member**: they have formally joined the church. This opens members' meetings and members-only documents.
- **Master admin**: can do everything, everywhere, including making other people admins. Keep this to very few people.
- **UNDER 16**: they cannot sign in. They use an access code emailed to a parent.
- **UNDER 18**: the hub never emails them. A 16 or 17 year old still signs in with their own email. Under 16 counts as under 18 too.

## Instruments and kids church roles
1. Under **Instruments (Primary)**, tick what they usually play or do.
2. Under **Instruments (Secondary)**, tick what they can also do.
3. Under **Kids Church Roles**, tick **Usually** for what they normally do and **Can cover** for what they will step into.
4. A role marked "(old name)" is kept from before. Tick the right new one and untick the old one.

## The e-news
The church office and master admins also see the e-news, when the church uses it.
1. Each person's row says **Gets the e-news**, or that they have unsubscribed.
2. Open a person with **Edit**. Under their email you see if they get it.
3. To add them, choose **How did they say yes?**, then press **Add to the e-news**. Nobody is added without saying how they agreed.
4. To stop it, press **Stop sending it** and say yes.

## Archive or restore someone
1. Press **Archive** on their row and say yes.
2. They disappear from lists, rotas and pickers. Nothing linked to them is lost.
3. To bring them back, tick **Show archived**, find them and press **Restore**.

## Good to know
- People are archived, never deleted. Fully removing someone's details is done with the privacy tools.
- A person on no team yet can only be changed by the church office or a master admin.
- Someone who unsubscribed from the e-news can only sign up again themselves, from the e-news sign-up page.
- Under-18s are never offered the e-news.
- Saving rebuilds the person's teams and roles from what is ticked, so check the ticks before you press save.

## Who to ask
- The church office, for anyone's details or a person not on your team.
- A master admin, to make someone an admin, a master admin, or to mark someone under 16 or under 18.

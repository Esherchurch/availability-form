# What's on

This page lists everything coming up at the church. Anyone can open it, and you can book from here by choosing an event.

## Who can use it
- Anyone can open it, signed in or not.
- If you are not signed in, you see the public events only. A note at the top says so, with a **Sign in** link.
- Signed in, you also see the members' and team events you are allowed to see.

## Find an event
1. Type in **Search events** to look for a word in the title, the write-up or the labels.
2. Or press one of the coloured buttons under the search box to show one kind of event. Press **All** to see everything again.
3. Featured events are shown first, under **Featured**. The rest come under **Everything else**.

## Open an event or book a place
1. Press an event to open it.
2. Its own page shows the details. Where booking is open, you can book a place there.

## Add events to your calendar once
1. Use the search or the coloured buttons to show the events you want.
2. Press **Add to calendar**.
3. You get one calendar file with all the events shown, except cancelled ones. It is a copy of things as they are now. Later changes do not follow.

## Subscribe in your own calendar
1. Press **Subscribe**.
2. If you are signed in, choose **Public events only** or **Everything I can see here (my own link)**.
3. Under **Which events**, tick the kinds you want. None ticked means everything.
4. Then add it. On a computer: **Add to Google Calendar** or **Apple or Outlook**. On an iPhone: **Add to my phone's calendar**. On Android, follow the steps shown, or press **Email me the link** to open it on a computer.
5. **Copy the link** copies it, if you want to paste it in yourself.

## Good to know
- A subscribed calendar keeps itself up to date: new events appear, and changes and cancellations follow. Google can take a few hours to catch up.
- Your own link shows members' and team events, so keep it to yourself. **Reset my link** makes a new one and the old one stops working.
- Labels on each event tell you about it: **Cancelled**, **Not public yet**, **Full — waiting list**, or how many places are left when there are five or fewer.
- **Bookings open after safeguarding sign-off** means the event is waiting to be checked before anyone can book.
- Tags such as **Attenders**, **Church members** or a team name show who the event is for.

## Who to ask
- The person running the event, for anything about that event.
- The church office, if you think you should see an event and cannot.

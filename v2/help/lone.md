# Working alone

This page is for anyone working on their own in one of the church's buildings. You sign in when you start and sign out when you leave. If you don't sign out, the hub makes sure someone checks you are all right.

## Who can use it
- Anyone signed in to the hub can use it to sign in and out.
- A building's contacts also see who is working alone at that building.
- The church office and master admins see every building, and set it up.
- Building compliance has to be switched on for the church. If it is off, the page says so.

## Sign in when you start
1. Under **Where**, choose the building.
2. Under **I expect to leave by**, choose the time. It must be within 12 hours.
3. Check **A phone number to ring you on**. The contacts will use it.
4. Press **I'm in**.

## Sign out when you leave
1. Open the page. It shows where you are signed in.
2. Press **I've left**.

## Stay longer
1. Under **I need longer, from now:**, press **30 minutes**, **1 hour** or **2 hours**.
2. The new time is saved from now.
3. If your time has passed, a red note asks you to do this, or to press **I've left**.

## When someone hasn't signed out (contacts)
1. Under **Working alone now**, each person shows their building and times.
2. Their label shows if they are past their time, or if the contacts or the office have been told.
3. Press **Ring** to phone them.
4. Once you know they are fine, press **They're safe** and add a short note.
5. Follow the steps in the yellow box. Never go to the building alone. If you think they are hurt or in danger, ring 999 first.

## Choose each building's contacts (office)
1. Under **Setting it up**, each building is listed.
2. Use **Add a contact…** to add a person. A building can have up to 10.
3. Press the **×** by a name to take them off.
4. If none are chosen, the building's health and safety lead and deputy are told instead.

## Set the times (office)
1. Fill in **Contacts are told after (minutes past the time)**.
2. Fill in **The office is told after a further (minutes)**.
3. Each is 5 to 120 minutes. Press **Save the times**.

## Good to know
- Only the building and the times are kept. Never where you are.
- If you don't sign out, the hub asks you first. Then it tells the building's contacts. If it goes on, it tells the office.
- The list of people working alone updates by itself.
- Online meeting places are not offered as buildings.

## Who to ask
- The church office, to be added as a building's contact or to change the times.
- The building's health and safety lead.

# Prayer

This page is for prayer requests at any time, and for praise reports: how prayers have been answered. Your requests are private.

## Who can use it
- Anyone signed in can ask for prayer, see their own requests, and read and share praise reports.
- The pastoral team also see a **Requests to pray for** tab with every request.
- If the church has a prayer team, it sees a **Prayer team** tab with the requests people chose to share with it.
- The pastoral team, the prayer team, and the person leading the coming Sunday's service may see a **For Sunday** tab.
- Master admins also see **The prayer team** box, to name the church's prayer team.
- If the church does not use prayer requests, the page says so.

## Ask for prayer
1. Open the **Prayer request** tab.
2. Write what you would like prayer for. Or press **Speak** and say it.
3. Under **Who may see it**, choose **The pastoral team only**, or the pastoral team and the prayer team (if the church has one).
4. Under **Happy for this to be prayed for in the Sunday service?** choose **No**, **Yes, with my name** or **Yes, without my name**. It is No unless you choose yes.
5. Press **Send my request**.
6. To ask for something more, press **Ask for something else**.

## See and change your requests
1. Open the **My requests** tab.
2. Each request shows when you sent it and who can see it.
3. To change your mind about Sunday, use **In the Sunday service?** under the request.
4. To take a request away, press **Take it back** and say yes. The pastoral team will no longer see it.

## Say a prayer has been answered
1. In **My requests**, find the request.
2. Under **Has it been answered? Say how**, write what happened if you like.
3. Tick **Share the answer with the church, to encourage others** if you want to. Leave **with my name** ticked, or untick it to share without your name.
4. Press **It has been answered**. It now shows **Answered**.

## Share a praise report
1. Open the **Praise reports** tab. You see what others have shared.
2. Under **Share an answer to prayer**, write it, or press **Speak** and say it.
3. Leave **with my name** ticked, or untick it.
4. Press **Share it**.

## Pray for others (pastoral team and prayer team)
1. Open **Requests to pray for** (pastoral team) or **Prayer team** (prayer team).
2. Each request shows who asked, when, and whether it may be prayed for on Sunday.
3. These are private. Please do not pass them on.
4. The pastoral team can press **Hide it** on a praise report to take it off the list.

## The Sunday list
1. Open **For Sunday**.
2. It lists the requests people are happy to have prayed for in the Sunday service, from the last four weeks, that are not yet answered.
3. A name shows only where the person asked for it. Otherwise it says **No name given**.
4. Press **Print** to print the list.

## Name the prayer team (master admins)
1. At the foot of the page, in **The prayer team**, type the name of the team or group exactly as it is in the address book.
2. Press **Save**. Leave it empty if the church has no prayer team.

## Good to know
- Your name goes with your request, so you can be prayed for by name.
- Nobody else sees a request, apart from the pastoral team and, if you choose, the prayer team.
- A request is never read out on Sunday unless you said yes.
- **Take it back** removes the request. It cannot be undone.
- Before the church goes live on the new hub, nothing can be sent from this page. The button says when requests open. Until then, speak to a church leader in person.
- If the church has not yet named who receives requests, the page says messages cannot be sent from here yet.
- If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.

## Who to ask
- The pastoral team, or a church leader in person.
- A master admin, if you cannot send a request or the prayer team is not shown.

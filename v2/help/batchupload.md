# Batch Music Importer

This is where the worship team adds many music PDFs at once: lead sheets, chord sheets, lyrics and bass tabs. Each file is added to its song in the music database, or starts a new song.

## Who can use it
- Leaders of the teams doing the worship job, and master admins.
- Everyone else is told the page is not for them.

## Add the files
1. Drag files or a whole folder onto the box, or press **Choose Files** or **Choose Folder**. Only PDFs are picked up.
2. Each file gets a row. The hub reads its name for the song, the key and the kind of sheet.
3. Name files like **Song title-chords-E.pdf** or **Song title lead Ab.pdf**. The key goes at the end, or after "in" or "key".

## Check each row
1. **Title** is the song's name, without the key or the kind of sheet. Brackets and accents are kept, for example **Psalm 8 (Hallé)**.
2. **Key** is read from the end of the name. If the hub isn't sure, it is left blank and the row says **REVIEW**: choose the key.
3. **Type** is the kind of sheet: chords, lead, lyrics or bass. Blank means **REVIEW**: choose it.
4. **Match** is the song already in the database. It is chosen for you only when the name clearly is that song (**sure**).
5. If a row says **check**, the likely songs are listed under **Match**. Pick the right one, or leave **New Song**.
6. The lead sheet and chord sheet of one song sit together under one heading, so they go into one song. **Apply Title** and **Apply Match** copy a row's choice to the rest of its song.

## Upload
1. Press **Upload** and say yes.
2. Each row says how it went. A row that needs a key or type is not uploaded and says what to choose; the rest still go.
3. If a file could not be saved, its row says why straight away.
4. If a save is still going after 30 seconds, its row says so. It may still finish; check your internet connection.
5. At the end, a summary lists anything not uploaded, and why.

## Good to know
- **Skip** leaves a row out. **Force** uploads a file even when the song already has that key and kind of sheet.
- **Find YouTube** and **Search CCLI** look the song up, so you can paste its links.
- Nothing is uploaded until you press **Upload**.

## Who to ask
- The worship leader, about songs and keys.
- A master admin, if you can't open the page.

# Safeguarding settings

This page sets who looks after safeguarding, and the periods and rules that every new form and event starts with. It also holds the settings for the safeguarding risk register.

## Who can use it
- Admins and the safeguarding lead or deputy can open it.
- Only admins can change who the safeguarding lead and deputy are. The lead sees the name, and "An admin changes who it is."
- Anyone else is told "These are set by admins and the safeguarding lead."

## Name the safeguarding lead and deputy (admins)
1. Under **Who looks after safeguarding**, choose the **Safeguarding lead** and the **Deputy** for each building.
2. If there is more than one building, pick the one whose lead the forms reach first.
3. Add **The lead's phone number (optional)**. It shows beside "call 999" on the "I'm worried" form.
4. Press **Save the settings**.
- Only adults are offered. The lead and the deputy must be two different people.
- Until a lead is named, concerns, requests for care and prayer requests cannot be sent.

## Set how long form answers last
1. Under **Forms**, find each kind of form.
2. Choose **Answer lasts**: one event, the end of the term, the end of the school year, or a number of days or months.
3. Choose **Kept after that**: how long it is kept before it is due for deletion.
4. Press **Save the settings**.

## Set the leader rules
1. Under **Leaders**, fill in **One leader for every … children** and **… and for every … under-8s**.
2. Choose how long **A DBS check counts for** and **Safeguarding training counts for**.
3. Tick **Every leader must have an in-date DBS check and training** if you want that for every event.
4. Press **Save the settings**.

## Set up the safeguarding risk register
1. Under **Safeguarding risk register**, type which team are the trustees. If Church settings already says who does the trustees job, it is changed there instead.
2. Set what **"Due soon" means**, in weeks.
3. Set when each dial on the register turns green or amber.
4. Set how many **AI-drafted assessments a day** the church and each person may have.
5. Tick which signed assessments **The trustees note**, such as camps, overnights or anything overseas.
6. Press **Save the settings**.

## Name your lead and your guidance
1. In **What your church calls its safeguarding lead**, type your own name for the role, such as "Parish safeguarding officer". Leave it empty to keep "safeguarding lead".
2. In **The safeguarding guidance your church follows**, type its name and its web address. It shows on every event assessment.
3. Press **Save the settings**.

## Add your church's own questions
1. Under **Your church's own required questions for event assessments**, press **Add a question** and type it.
2. Press **Remove** to take one away.
3. Press **Save the settings**. They are asked on every event assessment, as well as the fixed checklist.

## Apply the settings to forms and events already made
1. Save the settings first.
2. Under **Forms and events already made**, the page counts the forms and events that differ.
3. Press **Also apply to … forms** or **Also apply to … events**, and say yes.
- Answers already given keep their dates. Events with no leaders yet take the new settings on their own.

## Good to know
- New forms and new events take these settings from now on. An event can still change its own.
- **References and declarations count for** sets how long a reference and the confidential declaration last.
- **Flag public liability cover under** marks an event amber if its insurance is lower. It is £1 million unless you change it.
- **Look at a group's risks again** sets how much a group can grow or shrink before its risks are looked at again.
- The trustees never see a child, a concern or anyone's medical details on the register.
- Fire wardens and duty stewards are now set in Health and safety settings.

## Who to ask
- The church's safeguarding lead.
- An admin, to change who the lead or deputy is.

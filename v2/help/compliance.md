# Building compliance

This page keeps track of the safety checks each building needs: what is in date, what is due soon and what is overdue. It also holds the trustees' yearly report and sign-off.

## Who can use it
- The office, the trustees and master admins can open it. The page names who does the office and trustees jobs.
- The office sets up buildings and changes the checks. The trustees see everything and sign off.
- If a check has been given to you, it is on **The building** page instead.
- Building compliance has to be switched on for the church. If it is off, the page says so.

## See every building
1. Open the page. Each building shows a colour, and how many checks are overdue, due soon and in date.
2. Press a building to open it.
3. **Live risk register** and **Health and safety settings** (for the office) are at the top.

## Set up a building (office)
1. Open the building and press **The building**.
2. Answer the questions under **About this building**.
3. Press **Save the answers**. The answers decide which checks the building needs.
- A check that no longer applies keeps its history.

## Find a check
1. Open the building. **Checks** lists them by area, overdue first.
2. Use **Area**, **Status** and **Person** to narrow the list.
3. Each check is marked **Legal requirement**, **Recommended standard** or **Good practice**.

## Record a check done
1. Press the check.
2. Under **Record it done**, fill in **Done on**, and **Certificate expires** if it says.
3. Add **Notes**, and a **Certificate or photo** if you have one.
4. Press **Done**.

## Raise a job from a check
1. Press the check.
2. Under **Something needs doing**, fill in **What**, **Due** and **Who**.
3. Leave **A safety job** ticked if it affects safety. It changes the building's colour.
4. Press **Raise the job**. It goes on the Maintenance list.

## Change how often a check is done (office)
1. Press the check. Under **The church's copy**, set **Every** and choose **Responsible** people.
2. A recommended standard can only be done less often with a reason. A legal requirement can never be done less often.
3. Press **Save the copy**. **Switch it off…** asks why first.

## Add your own check or building work (office)
1. Press **+ Add the church's own check**. Fill in **What**, **Area** and **Every**, and press **Add it**.
2. Press **Record building work** when work is done. Checks that wait for building work become due.

## Extinguishers and fire blankets
1. Open the building and press **Extinguishers**.
2. The office chooses the **Kind of room** for each room. The guide then suggests what each room needs.
3. Press **+ Add an extinguisher or blanket**. Fill in **Type**, **Size**, **Label or number** and **Next service**.

## The trustee report and sign-off
1. Press **Trustee report and sign-off**.
2. It lists every check in every building. Use **Print or save as PDF** or **Download CSV** for meetings.
3. A trustee adds a note if they like, ticks **I have reviewed the building compliance**, and presses **Sign off for** the year.

## Good to know
- **History** on each building lists every change, newest first.
- When the guidance changes, the church's checks follow it by themselves.
- Until the church goes live on the hub, certificates and photos cannot be added. Keep your paper record for now.

## Who to ask
- The church office.
- The trustees, about the yearly sign-off.

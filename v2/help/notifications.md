# Notifications

This page turns on messages from the church hub on your phone or computer. You choose which messages you get.

## Who can use it
- Anyone signed in can use it, on each of their own devices.
- If you are not in the church's address book yet, only one kind of message is offered: being called to collect your child. The office can add you.

## Turn on notifications
1. Press **Turn on notifications on this device**.
2. Your phone or computer asks if it may show them. Say yes.
3. The page now says **On for this device**.

## On an iPhone
1. An iPhone only allows notifications once the hub is on your Home Screen.
2. In Safari, tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open the hub from its new icon on your Home Screen.
4. Come back to this page and tap **Turn on notifications**.
5. Your iPhone needs iOS 16.4 or later. If it is older, the page tells you how to update it.

## Choose what you get
1. Under **What I get**, tick the messages you want. Untick the ones you don't.
2. Each change is saved straight away.

## Set quiet hours
1. Under **Quiet hours**, choose **From** and **Until**.
2. In those hours messages still come, but silently.
3. Calling you to collect your child, and urgent notices, are never quiet.

## Send yourself a test
1. When notifications are on, press **Send me a test**.
2. It should arrive in a few seconds.

## Turn them off
1. Press **Turn off on this device**.
2. Your other devices are not changed.

## If they are blocked
1. If the page says **Notifications are blocked on this device**, they were turned off in your device's settings.
2. Follow the steps the page shows for your phone or browser.
3. Then come back and turn them on again.

## Good to know
- If notifications are not set up on any device, the hub emails you instead.
- Room booking news is emailed to you anyway, so it only comes to your device.
- Nothing asks you on its own. Notifications only start when you press the button.
- If your browser cannot show notifications, use Chrome, Edge or Safari on a computer, Chrome on Android, or the hub on an iPhone's Home Screen.

## Who to ask
- The church office, if you are not in the address book.
- The church office, if you need help setting up your phone.

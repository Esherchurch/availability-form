# Youth Service Planner

This is where the youth team plans a youth-led service: the songs, the keys, the order of service and notes for the team. You can then email the plan to everyone on that service.

## Who can use it
- People on the youth worship team can open it. So can master admins.
- Anyone else is turned away.
- You can only change and send youth services. Any other service shows as "(view only)". A yellow note says you can look at it as an example but cannot make changes.

## Choose the service
1. At the top, pick the date from the list of coming services. Use **◀** and **▶** to move between them, or **Pick another date**.
2. Choose the service in **Select Service...**. A youth service is chosen for you if there is one.
3. **Leader** shows the worship leader from the rota.
4. If a plan was saved for that service, it opens. If not, you get a starting order of songs, **Welcome and Notices** and **Message**.

## Fill in the top
1. **General Notes**: anything the whole team needs to know.
2. **Opening Remarks**: this goes at the start of the team email.

## Plan the songs
1. Type in **Song title**. Pick the song from the list that comes up.
2. If it is **Not in library**, use **SongSelect** or **YouTube** to find it, or **Upload Song** to add it.
3. Choose up to three keys in **Key 1**, **Key 2** and **Key 3**. The files for each key show, with **Open File**.
4. **Available** and **Missing** show which keys have chord sheets.
5. Type in **Notes for the band...**.
6. The small number by a song is how many Sundays it has been used. A dark number means its highest ever, so you may want to rest it.

## Change the order
1. **+ Add Song** adds a song. **+ Add Item** adds anything else.
2. Drag the dots (**⠿**) to move a song or item.
3. Press **▶** or **▼** to open or close a song.
4. Press **×** to remove it.
5. **Songs Database** opens the song library.

## Notes for each person
1. **Service Team & Roles** lists who is on the rota for that service, with the service leader and speaker.
2. Type anything for one person in **Role Specific Notes**.
3. **Closing Remarks** goes at the end of the team email.

## Save the plan
1. Press **Save Schedule**.
2. It says "Youth service saved."

## Email the plan
1. Press **Email Team**. This saves the plan first.
2. In **Your Email (reply-to)**, type your email. Replies come to you.
3. Press **Email Whole Team**. It goes to everyone on the service and the church's leadership team.
4. Or choose a name under **Or email one person:** and press **Email This Person**.

## Good to know
- Nothing is saved until you press **Save Schedule** or **Email Team**. If you leave first, your changes are lost.
- A service counts as a youth service when its kind or its notes on the rota have the word "youth" in them. If yours shows "(view only)", ask whoever runs the rota.
- The email has a link to a simple song list, with the songs, CCLI numbers and notes.
- The **?** button opens the step-by-step guide.

## Who to ask
- The youth team leader.
- Whoever runs the [Rota Planner](Planner.html), if a youth service is missing or shows as view only.
- A master admin, if you cannot open the page.

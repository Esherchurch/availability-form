# Your booking

This page shows one room booking to the person who asked for it. They get to it from the link in the church's email. It shows where the booking stands, the quote, any invoice and payments, and lets them accept the quote or ask to cancel.

## Who can use it
- Anyone with the link from the email can open it. There is no need to sign in.
- Each booking has its own private link. Without the right link, the page says "We cannot find that booking."

## See where your booking stands
1. Open the link in your email.
2. The top card shows the room, when, how many people and your reference.
3. The label shows **Confirmed**, **Waiting for us to confirm**, **Not going ahead** or **Cancelled**.
4. If you asked for time to set up or clear away, it says when the room is yours from and to.
5. For a repeating booking, it says which date this is, such as "date 2 of 10".

## Accept the quote
1. Once the booking is confirmed and has a price, the quote is shown line by line.
2. Read the **Terms and conditions** below it, if there are any.
3. Tick **I accept this quote**, check **Your name**, and press **Accept**.
4. It then says who accepted it and when.

## Print or save the quote
1. Press **Print, or save as a PDF**.
2. In the print window, choose a printer, or save it as a PDF.

## Say you have arrived
1. On the day of a confirmed booking, press **We've arrived** when you get there.
2. This lets the church know the room is in use. It then says you are marked as arrived.

## Ask to cancel
1. Press **Ask to cancel**.
2. Say why in **Why? (optional)** if you want to.
3. Press **Send**.
4. This only asks. The booking stands until the church replies by email, and tells you about any refund.

## Good to know
- An **Invoice** card shows the invoice number, the date to pay by and how to pay, once one has been sent.
- **Payments** shows how much has been paid so far.
- **Damage deposit** shows whether it is held, returned or kept.
- If accepting fails, the price or terms may have just changed. Reload the page and look again.

## Who to ask
- The church office. Reply to any of their emails, or use the email address at the bottom of the page.

# Headcounts

This page keeps a quick count for each Sunday service or event: how many adults, children and people online. It is numbers only, no names.

## Who can use it
- The Kids Church leads and master admins can open it.
- Anyone else is told "This page is for the Kids Church leads and master admins."

## Add a count
1. Under **Add a count**, check the **Date**. It starts on today if it is Sunday, or the Sunday just gone.
2. In **What**, choose **Sunday morning service**, **Sunday evening service**, or one of the recent events. Choosing an event sets its date for you.
3. If it is none of these, choose **Something else…** and type it in **Name it**.
4. Type the numbers in **Adults**, **Children** and **Online**.
5. Add **Notes** if you like, such as half term or baptisms.
6. Press **Save the count**.

## Look at the counts
1. **Counts so far** lists every count, newest first.
2. Each row shows adults, children, online and the total.
3. **Change** shows how the total moved since the last count with the same name. Sunday mornings are compared with Sunday mornings.

## Take the counts away as a spreadsheet
1. Press **CSV** at the top of **Counts so far**.
2. A file is saved that opens in a spreadsheet.

## Remove a count
1. Press the bin button at the end of the row.
2. Say yes to "Remove this count?".

## Good to know
- The events offered in **What** are from the last three weeks, up to tomorrow. Cancelled events are not offered.
- You must say what was counted and choose a date, or it will not save.

## Who to ask
- Your Kids Church lead.
- A master admin, if you cannot open the page.

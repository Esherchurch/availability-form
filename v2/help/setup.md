# Setting up your hub

This page is the list of steps a new church works through to set up its hub. Each step opens the page that does it, and you can come back here at any time.

## Who can use it
- Only master admins can open it.
- Anyone else is told they cannot open the page.

## Choose what you will use
1. At the top, under **What will you use?**, press **Worship and AV only**, **Sundays and teams** or **Everything**.
2. The parts you don't choose are switched off, and their steps are left off the list.
3. You can switch any part on or off later in [Church settings](church-settings.html). Nothing is deleted.

## Work through the steps
1. Open the page. The bar at the top shows how far you have got, and the line under the title says how many steps are done.
2. Press **Start** on a step. It opens the page that does that step.
3. Do the step on that page. A bar along the foot of the page says which step you are on.
4. Press **Next** in that bar to go to the next step, or **All the steps** to come back here.
5. A step that is done shows a tick, and its button says **Change**.

## The steps
1. **Your church**: its name, logo, address and words, its giving details and the e-news.
2. **Your buildings**: each building and its rooms, and who looks after its safety.
3. **Your teams**: the teams and groups people belong to, and which jobs each one does.
4. **Your services**: the kinds of service and meeting, and the roles a service names.
5. **Your people**: the address book. Everyone the hub knows, and the teams they are on.
6. **Who gets told**: who reads requests for care, pastoral concerns and prayer requests.
7. **Safeguarding**: the safeguarding lead and deputy, how long checks last, and the ratios.
8. **Data protection**: your data protection lead, and the record of what the church holds.
9. **Pastoral visitors** (if you want it): the trained people who record pastoral visits.

## Good to know
- You cannot tick a step yourself. A step shows as done once the hub sees it has been done on its own page.
- **Your church** is done once it has a name, an address and an enquiry email.
- **Your buildings** is done once there is a building, a room in it, and a health and safety lead.
- **Your teams** is done once there is a team and someone has chosen who does each job.
- **Pastoral visitors** is optional. It is not counted in the steps done.
- A step marked **Coming soon** cannot be done yet.
- When every step is done, the page says Church HQ will take you through going live.

## Who to ask
- Church HQ, about setting up and going live.
- Another master admin of your church.

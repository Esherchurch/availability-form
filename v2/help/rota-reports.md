# Rota reports

This page shows who serves, how often, and where the gaps are on the rotas. It only reads the rotas as they are saved. It changes nothing.

## Who can use it
- Master admins see every rota team.
- Leaders of a rota team see only the teams they lead. Being an admin of the team, or on a core team that covers it, counts too.
- The rota teams are the church's own teams that have a rota, as set in Teams & groups.
- Rota reports have to be switched on for the church. If they are off, the page says so.
- Anyone else is told "This page is for master admins and the leaders of rota teams."

## Choose the dates and team
1. Set **From** and **To**. To start with, it shows three months back to three months ahead.
2. If you can see more than one team, pick one under **Team**, or leave it on all teams.
3. Practices are left out unless you tick **Include practices**.

## Find unfilled slots
1. Press **Unfilled slots**. This is where the page opens.
2. You see each empty slot, with its date, service, slot and team.

## See an overview of each service
1. Press **Overview**.
2. Each service shows its date, time, how many slots are filled, and which are still empty.

## Find clashes
1. Press **Clashes**.
2. You see anyone who is down for two places at once on the same day, and why.

## See how often people serve
1. Press **How often people serve**.
2. Each person shows how many services and slots they served, on which teams, and when they last served.

## See one person's serving
1. Press **One person's serving**.
2. Pick them under **Who**.
3. You see every time they are on the rota in these dates.

## Find people not on the rota
1. Press **Not on the rota**.
2. You see people on a team with its own places on the rota who were not on the rota at all in these dates. Someone on a smaller group inside a team (a choir inside the worship team) counts as on that team.

## Download a report
1. Open the report you want.
2. Press **Download (CSV)**. It opens in a spreadsheet.
3. The button only shows when the report has something in it.

## Good to know
- This page shows serving only. It does not show who came to church.
- To fill a gap, use the rota planner, not this page.

## Who to ask
- Your team leader.
- A master admin, if you cannot open the page or reports are switched off.

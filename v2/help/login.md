# Sign in

This is the page where you sign in to the church's hub. There is no password: the hub emails you a link, or you sign in with Google.

## Who can use it
- Anyone can open this page.
- You can only get in if the church already has your email address. Use whichever address the church has for you.
- Once you are signed in, the page takes you straight on to the hub, or back to the page you were trying to open.

## Choose who is signing in
1. The first question is **Who's using this?** This is because people often share an email address.
2. Press **I'm on a team** if you serve on one of the church's teams. You then sign in with your email.
3. Press **I'm part of the youth group** if you are in the youth group. This opens the youth access page, where you use the access code that was emailed to your parent. You only need the code once.
4. **Back** takes you back to this question.

## Sign in with an emailed link
1. Press **I'm on a team**.
2. Type your address under **Your email address**.
3. Press **Email me a sign-in link**.
4. You see **Check your email**. Open the email and press the link, on the same phone or computer. You are signed straight in.
5. Typed the wrong address? Press **Use a different address**.
6. If you open the link on a different device, you may be asked to type your email address again to finish.

## Sign in with Google
1. Press **I'm on a team**.
2. Press **Continue with Google** and pick your Google account.
3. This only works if the church has your Gmail address.

## Sign in from the installed app on a phone
1. Press **I'm on a team**. In the app, Google's own sign-in button may show, or Google may offer your account straight away. Pick it.
2. If Google's sign-in cannot finish inside the app, you see **Sign in once in your phone's browser**.
3. Press **Open the hub in the browser** and sign in there once.
4. Then open the app again. It will be signed in too.

## Good to know
- A sign-in link can only be used once, and it runs out. If it says the link has expired or has already been used, ask for a new one.
- "No connection" means your phone or computer is offline. Check your network and try again.
- If a Google sign-in did not finish, the page says so. Try **Continue with Google** again.

## Who to ask
- Your team leader, if you have trouble signing in or the church does not have your email address.
- The church office, if your email address needs changing.

# Find a group

This page lists the church's small groups. They meet through the week, in homes, at church and online. You can find one, ask to join, and see your own groups.

## Who can use it
- Anyone can open it. If you are not signed in, you see only the groups open to everyone.
- Sign in to see members' groups too. You only see the groups you are able to come to.
- Your own groups are always shown, at the top under **Your groups**.

## Find a group
1. Type in **Search groups**, or use the filters: **Any day**, **Any kind** and **Anywhere**.
2. Tick **Taking new members** to hide groups that are full or closed.
3. Tap a group to see when and where it meets, who it is for and who leads it.
4. If nothing shows, try fewer filters.

## Ask to join a group
1. Tap the group. If it is taking new members, you see **Ask to join**.
2. Fill in **Your name** and **Your email**. Add **Your phone** if you would like a call.
3. Add anything you would like the leaders to know.
4. Press **Ask to join**. It goes to the group's leaders, who will get in touch. A copy comes to you by email.

## See your own group
1. Tap a group under **Your groups**.
2. You see its address or online meeting link, which only members see.
3. **Coming up** shows the next meetings, with any notes or study plan, and when it is not meeting.
4. **Messages** shows the latest messages sent to the group.

## Leave a group
1. Tap the group under **Your groups**.
2. Press **Leave this group** and say yes.
3. You can ask to join again later.

## Run your group (leaders)
1. If you lead a group, it says "You lead this".
2. Press **Run your group: members and requests** to go to the small groups page.

## Good to know
- A group that is not taking new members says so. Look at the other groups, or ask the church office.
- You can share a link straight to one group. It opens that group when the page loads.

## Who to ask
- The group's leaders, named on the group.
- The church office, to find the right group.

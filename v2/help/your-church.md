# Your church

Tell the hub who your church is: its name, address, logo and enquiry email, the words your church uses, the bank details for giving, and whether you send an e-news. The hub uses these on its pages, in its emails and in the e-news.

## Who can use it
- Master admins only. Anyone else can't open this page.
- The church office can also keep the giving (bank) details up to date, on the [Giving details](giving-details.html) page. It is the same form as on this page.

## Set your name, address and logo
1. In **Name, address and logo**, type **Your church's name** as people know it.
2. Type the **Postal address**. It goes at the foot of emails and the e-news. Each building's own address is kept separately, with your buildings.
3. Type the **Enquiry email**. This is where questions go, and it is the reply address on emails the hub sends.
4. Press **Save**.
5. To add a logo, press **Add a logo** (or **Change the logo**) and choose a picture. PNG, JPEG or WebP pictures work. A square picture works best. It is saved straight away.

## Set your words
1. In **Your words**, type **Your minister's title**, for example vicar, rector, minister or pastor. You see how it reads in a sentence, such as "Letter from the vicar". Left empty, the hub says "minister".
2. Type **What you call a member**, for example "Member" or "On the electoral roll". These are the people who may come to members' meetings and read members-only words. Left empty, the hub says "Church member".
3. Choose **Your main day of worship**. The e-news and the screens in church look ahead to this day. It starts as Sunday.
4. Press **Save**.

## Add the giving details
1. In **Giving**, fill in **Account name**, **Sort code** and **Account number**. The sort code can be typed with or without dashes.
2. Type a **Suggested reference**, so the treasurer can tell who a gift is from (for example "their own name").
3. Under **Gift Aid**, choose **Do not mention Gift Aid** or **Mention Gift Aid, with a link to the form**. If you mention it, paste the **Link to the Gift Aid form**. It must start with https://. Left empty, the Gift Aid line still shows, without a link.
4. Add **Anything else to say** if you like: one optional line shown under the details.
5. Press **Save the giving details**.

## Switch the e-news on or off
1. In **The e-news**, press the **Send an e-news** switch.
2. When it is on, **Open the e-news ›** takes you to it.
3. When it is off, the e-news is hidden everywhere in the hub.

## Good to know
- Each part of the page saves on its own, with its own button. Saving one part doesn't change the others.
- Your church's name, address, logo and enquiry email can be seen on pages people use without signing in, such as the sign-in page and room-hire pages. Don't put anything private in them.
- The bank details are only shown to people who are signed in to the hub.
- When you change the logo, the old one is removed. The logo is made smaller automatically, as it is shown small in page headers and at the top of emails.
- Switching the e-news off doesn't delete anything. Switching it back on brings it all back.

## Who to ask
- Another master admin, if you are stuck or can't open this page.
- The church office, for the right bank details and the enquiry email address.
- The church's treasurer, for the giving reference and Gift Aid form.

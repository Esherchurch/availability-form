# Meeting

This is where the church's video meetings happen. Opened from the Menu, it lists your upcoming video calls and your meeting rooms. Opened from a meeting link, it puts you in the call.

## Who can use it
- Anyone signed in to the hub can open it.
- You see only the meetings you are invited to, and the rooms that are yours. Master admins see every meeting and every room.
- The members' meeting room is shown only to church members.
- **New meeting** is shown to people on a team and to team admins.
- **Invite people** and **Cancel** are shown to whoever set the meeting up, admins of the meeting's teams, and master admins.

## Join a meeting
1. Open **Meetings** from the Menu. Under **Upcoming**, find the meeting and press **Join**.
2. Or press **Join** beside one of your rooms.
3. If you can host, you are asked. Press **Start as host**, or **Join as a guest**.
4. Everyone else types their name and knocks. The host lets them in.

## Host a meeting
1. The person who set the meeting up, a leader of the room's team, or a master admin can host.
2. Press **Start as host** when you join.
3. As host you let people in when they knock, and can share your screen or slides.
4. To show slides, press **Present slides** in the call. You can also let someone else present, and **Take it back**.

## Set up a new meeting
1. On the Meetings list, press **New meeting**.
2. Choose the **Meeting** type. Pick plain **Meeting** for a one-to-one or an interview. The other types are the church's own meeting kinds (from Services and roles), then a meeting for each of your teams.
3. Add **Title or notes (optional)**, the **Date**, **Start** and **End**.
4. **Video call**: leave **Yes, in a room of its own**, or choose **No video call**.
5. Under **People**, type a name from the address book and press **Add**.
6. Under **Someone not in the address book**, type their name and email and press **Add**.
7. Tick **Whole teams (optional)** only if a whole team should come. The teams are the church's own, from Teams & groups.
8. Press **Create meeting**. Everyone you added is emailed, and the page says who was.

## Invite someone to a meeting
1. On the Meetings list, press **Invite people** on the meeting.
2. Under **Someone in the address book**, type a name and press **Invite**. They are emailed a link.
3. Under **Someone who is not in the address book**, type their name and email and press **Invite by email**. They get a link for this meeting only.
4. **Invited by name** shows who is invited. Press **Remove** to take someone off.
5. Press **Done** when you have finished.

## Share the link
- Press **Copy invite link** on a room, or in the bar during a call.
- The link works for anyone, even without a hub login. They knock and the host lets them in.

## Cancel a meeting
1. On the Meetings list, press **Cancel** on the meeting.
2. Say yes. Anyone who was emailed an invitation is told, and its video room is closed.

## In the call
- **Full screen** fills the screen with the call. Press **Exit full screen** or the Escape key to come out.
- When you leave, press **Rejoin** to go back in, or **Back to hub**.

## Good to know
- Rooms are private. Nobody gets in without knocking unless they are the host.
- Every new meeting with a video call gets its own room, so two meetings never share a link.
- With no team ticked, only you, the people you add and master admins can see the meeting.
- You must add at least one person or tick a team.
- Guests can only join a meeting with a video call.
- Under-18s cannot be invited by name.
- A guest's link stops working if you remove them.
- Cancelling a meeting deletes it. This cannot be undone.
- If a room is in use by another meeting, the page tells you which one and until when.
- Someone with no email address does not get an email, but sees the meeting in the hub.

## Who to ask
- Whoever set the meeting up, if you cannot get in.
- The church office, if you should be able to join the members' meeting.
- A master admin, for anything else.

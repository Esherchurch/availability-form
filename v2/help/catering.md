# Catering orders

This page lists the refreshments booked with rooms over the next two weeks, so the kitchen knows what to make, for how many and when. It shows what is needed, never who booked.

## Who can use it
- The team that does the church's catering, admins and master admins see every building.
- A building's bookings admins see the buildings they look after.
- Anyone else is told the page is for the caterers and the church office.
- If there is more than one building, choose it in the **Site** box.

## See what is needed
1. Orders are grouped by day.
2. Each order shows the serving time, the room, how many people, what to make and any dietary needs, such as "2 vegetarian".
3. Any notes about food are shown under it.

## Mark an order prepared or served
1. When it is ready, press **Prepared**.
2. When it has gone out, press **Served**.
3. The label shows **To prepare**, **Prepared** or **Served**, with the name of who marked it.
4. Pressed **Served** by mistake? Press **Undo**.

## Print the kitchen sheet
1. Press **Print the kitchen sheet**.
2. Only the orders print, not the buttons.

## Good to know
- An order appears here once a booking with refreshments is confirmed.
- Dietary needs are only ever numbers, never names.
- If nothing is ordered in the next two weeks, the page says so.

## Who to ask
- The church office, about a booking or a change to an order.
- A master admin, if you cannot open the page.

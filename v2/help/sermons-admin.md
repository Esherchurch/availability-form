# Sermons

This page looks after the church's sermons. Sermons come in by themselves from the church's podcast on Spotify, and here you add the series, Bible passage and speaker, hide any you don't want shown, or upload one by hand. The live service and videos are on [Watch](watch-admin.html).

## Who can use it

- Master admins can always use this page.
- Anyone else can use it only if a master admin has added them under **Who can look after sermons**.
- Everyone else sees "Only the people named can look after sermons".
- Only master admins see the **Who can look after sermons** part and can add or remove people.
- The same people also look after the [Watch](watch-admin.html) page.

## Connect the podcast

Do this once. After that, new episodes come in by themselves.

1. Find the podcast's RSS address. In Spotify for Creators it is under Settings, as the RSS feed.
2. On this page, go to the podcast card and paste it into **The podcast's RSS address**. It must start with https://.
3. Press **Save the address**.

The podcast is read every hour, so new episodes appear within the hour. The card tells you what the last read found: when it was, how many episodes, and how many were new. If the last read did not work, it says why. Check the address.

To stop new episodes coming in, clear the address and press **Save the address**.

## Add the series and passage to a podcast sermon

Podcast sermons are marked **From Spotify**. A sermon with neither a series nor a book shows **add its series or passage**.

1. In the **Sermons** list, press **Add series and passage** on the sermon.
2. Choose or type the **Speaker**. Names used before are suggested.
3. Choose the **Series**, or **Not part of a series**.
4. Choose the **Bible book** and type the **Passage** (for example 1:1-11).
5. Tick or untick **Show it in the app**.
6. Press **Save**, or **Cancel** to leave it.

The title, words and recording come from Spotify and can't be changed here. What you add is kept when the podcast is read again.

## Show or hide a sermon

- Press **Hide** on a sermon to take it out of the app. It shows **Hidden**.
- Press **Show** to put it back. It shows **Shown**.
- A hidden podcast sermon stays hidden when the podcast is read again.

## Upload a sermon by hand

This is the backup way, for a sermon that is not on the podcast.

1. Press **+ Upload a sermon by hand (if one is not on the podcast)**.
2. In **The recording**, choose the audio file. An MP3 is best. It can be up to 300 MB.
3. Type the **Title**.
4. Fill in **Speaker** and **Date** (it starts at last Sunday), and the **Series**, **Bible book** and **Passage** if you like.
5. Add **Notes (optional)**.
6. Leave **Show it in the app** ticked to show it straight away.
7. Press **Upload**. A bar shows how far the upload has got.

Uploaded sermons are marked **Uploaded here**. To change one, press **Change**. You can choose **New audio (only if it needs replacing)**, edit the details, and press **Save**.

If the upload fails, the sermon is kept but hidden and marked **Needs its audio**. Press **Add its audio** to try again.

## Add a series

1. Under **Series**, press **+ Add a series**.
2. Type the **Name**.
3. Add **A line about it (optional)**.
4. Choose **A square picture (optional, JPEG or PNG)** if you have one.
5. Press **Add the series**.

Each series shows how many sermons are in it, and whether it has a picture.

## Who can look after sermons (master admins)

1. In **Add someone**, start typing a name and choose the person from the list.
2. Press **Add**.
3. To take someone off, press **Remove** next to their name.

## Good to know

- Sermons are never deleted here. Hide them instead.
- Series can't be deleted, renamed or given a new picture on this page once added.
- Nothing you do here changes anything on Spotify.
- Podcast sermons play from Spotify's own recordings.
- A new podcast episode arrives already shown in the app.
- If an uploaded recording's length can't be read, it is saved but not shown. Try again with an MP3.
- Shown sermons appear on [Watch & Listen](watch.html).

## Who to ask

- For podcast or sermon questions: whoever looks after sermons.
- To be added to the people who look after sermons: the church office, who can ask a master admin.

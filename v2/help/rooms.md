# Book a room

This page shows when the church's rooms are free, and lets you book one for a band practice, a meeting or anything else. It also lists your own bookings, so you can cancel them or say you have arrived.

## Who can use it
- Team leaders, the core teams, the church office and the bookings admins can open it.
- A bookings admin who is not one of the others only sees the rooms at the buildings they look after.
- Anyone else is told it is for team leaders and core teams, and to ask their team leader.
- Only rooms that members are allowed to book are shown.

## See when rooms are free
1. Press **Day** to see every room on one day, or **Week** to see one room for seven days.
2. Pick the date in the **Day** (or **Week from**) box, or use the arrows to go earlier or later.
3. In week view, choose the room in the **Room** box. If the church has more than one building, choose it in the **Site** box.
4. Your own bookings are marked. A room that is closed that day is not shown, or shows as closed.
5. Press **Compare rooms** to see two or more rooms side by side.

## Book a room
1. Click a free time on the calendar, or press **Book a room**.
2. If there is a **What kind of booking** box, choose one. Church or ministry use is free. A kind marked "(charged)" is priced.
3. Tick the room under **Room** or **Rooms**. Tick more than one if your event needs several rooms.
4. Fill in **Date**, **Start** and **Finish**.
5. Choose **Time to set up first** and **Time to clear away after** if you need them. The room is held for that time too.
6. Fill in **What is it for?** and **How many people**. Choose **Set out as** if you want the room laid out a certain way.
7. Tick **We need sound or projection**, refreshments or kit if you need them, and say what.
8. Read the messages under the form. They say if the room is free, and whether it is confirmed straight away or waits for the office.
9. Press **Book it**, or **Ask for it** if it has to wait for the office. Then press **Done**.

## Book the same time every week
1. In the booking form, choose **Repeats**: **Every week**, **Every two weeks** or **Every month, on the same weekday**.
2. Choose **Until**, the last date.
3. The dates are listed, each marked free or not. Dates that are not free are left out. Untick any you do not want.
4. Press **Book it** or **Ask for it**.

## Cancel a booking
1. Find it under **Your bookings**.
2. Press **Cancel**. For a repeating booking you can also press **Cancel this and the ... after it**.
3. Say yes to the question. The room is free again and you are emailed.

## Say you have arrived
1. On the day of a confirmed booking, press **We've arrived** under **Your bookings**.
2. It then says you are marked as arrived.

## Good to know
- You can book up to 100 bookings at once (rooms times dates).
- A charged booking always waits for the office to confirm the price. You see a price for it, at the members' rate if there is one.
- If a room can never hold the number of people you give, it says so and will not book.
- When a booking is confirmed you are emailed the details and a calendar file. When it waits, you are emailed again once the office decides.
- If a booking overlaps one the office made, you cannot cancel it here. Ask the office.
- If someone books the same time just before you, it tells you to look again.
- Bookings admins see a link to **Room bookings**, where they can approve, move or cancel any booking.

## Who to ask
- The church office, about prices, approvals or a booking you cannot cancel.
- Your team leader, if you cannot open the page.

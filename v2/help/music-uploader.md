# Upload Song

This is where you add one or a few music PDFs to the music database.

## Who can use it
- Leaders of the teams doing the worship job, and master admins.

## Add files
1. Drag files onto the box, or tap it to choose them.
2. Each file gets a row. Choose its **Type**, the kind of sheet.
3. **Key** is read from the end of the file's name, or after "in" or "key", for example **Song-chords-Em.pdf**. If the hub isn't sure, it is left blank: choose it.
4. Press **Upload**.

## Good to know
- The hub never guesses a key from a letter inside a word.
- For many files at once, use the Batch Music Importer.

## Who to ask
- The worship leader, about songs and keys.

# I'm worried about someone

This is where you tell the church that you are worried about someone. It goes, in private, to the people who look after pastoral care or safeguarding. The same page is also used to ask for pastoral care for yourself.

## Who can use it
- Anyone signed in to the hub can use it.
- What you send goes only to the people who deal with that kind of concern. The person you are worried about is never told it came from you.
- You cannot see what you sent after it has gone.

## If someone is in danger
- If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.
- This form is read by people, not watched all the time, so it is not the way to raise something urgent.
- If the church has set it up, the page shows the safeguarding lead's name and phone number to ring if it cannot wait.

## Raise a concern about someone
1. Under **What kind of concern is it?**, choose one:
   - **A pastoral concern**: you have not seen them for a while, they seem lonely, or they are going through a hard time. It goes to the pastoral team and the people the church has named for pastoral concerns. The safeguarding lead and deputy can read it too.
   - **A safeguarding concern**: a child or an adult may be at risk. It goes only to the safeguarding lead and deputy.
2. In **Who are you worried about?**, start typing a name. Pick them from the list, or just type a name. Either is fine.
3. In **What have you noticed?**, say what you have noticed, in your own words.
4. Leave **I am happy to be contacted about this.** ticked if they may get in touch with you. Untick it if not.
5. Press **Send it**. You will see "Thank you, it has been passed on."
6. Press **Cancel** to leave without sending.

## Ask for pastoral care for yourself
1. Open **Care & support** at the top of the hub and choose "I'd like some pastoral care". The page changes its title to match.
2. In **Anything you would like to say? (you do not have to)**, write anything that helps, such as the best time to ring.
3. Press **Send it**. Somebody from the pastoral team will be in touch.

## Good to know
- This page is always there, whatever parts of the hub the church uses. If the church has switched off **Care and prayer**, it offers only a safeguarding concern, which goes only to the safeguarding lead and deputy.
- Nothing here is automatic and nobody is being watched. It is only for when you decide to speak up.
- A request for pastoral care for yourself is read only by the pastoral team, the people the church has named for pastoral care, and the safeguarding lead and deputy. It is not put on your record. Nobody else will know you asked.
- You must choose the kind of concern, give a name, and say a little about what you noticed, or it will not send.
- The form opens when the church goes live on the new hub. Until then, the page says so. Speak to a church leader in person.
- If the church has not yet named who receives these messages, the page says so and does not offer to send one.
- If it does not send, try again, or tell a leader directly.

## Who to ask
- In an emergency, call 999.
- A church leader, in person, if the form is not open or will not send.
- The safeguarding lead, for anything about a child or an adult at risk.

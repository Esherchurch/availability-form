# The app

The church's phone app. It shows your week: when you are serving, the notices, your events and groups, and your team's tools. You can add it to your phone's home screen.

## Who can use it
- Everyone in the church's address book can sign in and use it.
- You see the parts for the teams you are on. Someone on no team sees **Just me**.
- A parent signed in who is not yet in the address book sees only **Home** and **Me**, with their children's Sunday. The church office can add them.
- Someone whose email the church does not know is turned away.

## Choose where to go
1. If you are on more than one team, the app asks "where to today?". Choose a tile: **Sunday worship**, **Kids and youth**, **The building**, **Church office** or **Just me**. You only see the tiles that are yours.
2. To move to another part, press **Switch** at the top and choose one. **See all** shows the tiles again.
3. The first time, it may ask **Open the app here every time?** Press **Always open here** or **Not now**.
4. Inside a part, the coloured buttons under the top bar move between your teams. The tabs along the bottom move between screens.

## Your Home
- **Just me** Home says what is on for you this week. It can show:
  - **This Sunday**: a plan you are on. Tap it to see the songs and the order.
  - **You are serving**: your next dates on the rota. **See the plan** opens the service plan. **Whole rota** opens the full rota.
  - **Watch live** while the service is on, and a video meeting you have today, with **Join**.
  - For parents: your child's check-in code before Sunday, and the collection code once they are in.
  - **Your family this week**, **Your events**, **Notices** and **Your groups**.
  - **My availability**, to say which Sundays you can do.
- Each other part has its own **Home** too. For example, **Sunday worship** shows the coming Sunday, how many roles are filled, and your serving.

## Read the notices
1. Press the bell at the top. A red number means notices you have not seen.
2. Read each notice in full. Press **Share** to send a link to it.
3. Tapping a notice on Home opens the same list.

## Care and support
1. Press **Care & support** (or **Care**) at the top. It is on every screen.
2. Choose what you need, such as "I'm worried about someone".
- If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.

## Say which Sundays you can do
1. On Home or **Me**, press **My availability**.
2. The [availability form](index.html) opens. Answer each date.

## Add your rota to your calendar
1. Go to **Me** and press **Add to my calendar**.
2. Choose **Just me**, **My household** or **The whole rota**.
3. Press **Set this one up** if it is not set up yet.
4. Press **Apple / iPhone**, **Google** or **Outlook**. Or press **Copy link** and paste it into your calendar.
- New dates then appear by themselves. Keep the link to yourself: anyone with it can see those dates.

## Give
1. Go to **Me** and press **Give**.
2. You see the church's account name, sort code, account number and reference. Press **Copy** by each one to paste it into your banking app.
3. If the church uses Gift Aid, there is a link to the form, or ask the church office.

## Your team's tabs
- **Rota**: when you are on, and who is on the team's coming dates.
- **This Sunday** (or **Sunday**): the order of service, the keys and notes, who is on, and **Listen** to hear each song.
- **Team**: the team's charter, **Pin boards**, the meeting room, resources and more.
- **Pin boards**: read the team's ideas. **Add an idea** puts a new note on the board. **The whole board** opens it to move and archive notes.
- Leadership teams have a **Plan** tab for the service plan, a **Rota** tab to add a service with **New service or meeting**, and **Meetings** to join a call.

## Me
- **My groups**, **My messages** (what the church has emailed you), **My availability**, **Add to my calendar** and **Notifications**.
- **Open the full website** opens the computer version.
- **Sign out**. You stay signed in until you do.

## View as somebody else (master admins)
1. On **Me**, press **View as somebody else**.
2. Choose **Everyone (no team)** or a team. Tick **As an admin of that team** if you want.
3. A strip at the top says who you are viewing as. Press **Back to me** to stop.
- It changes nothing for anyone else.

## Good to know
- Parts the church has switched off do not show.
- A section with nothing in it is left out.
- Some jobs, such as writing a notice, are easier on a computer. Use **Open the full website** on **Me**.
- Turn on notifications from **Me** to hear the day before you serve.

## Who to ask
- Your team leader, about your team's tabs and rota.
- The church office, if you cannot sign in or your details are wrong.
- A master admin, if a part of the app is missing.

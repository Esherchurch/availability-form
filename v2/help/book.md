# Ask to book a room

This is the form people outside the church use to ask to hire a room. Nothing is booked until the church office confirms it by email.

## Who can use it
- Anyone can open it. There is no need to sign in.
- Only rooms that are open for hire are offered. If there are none, the page says "No rooms are open for hire just now."
- Church members book on **Book a room** instead.

## Ask for a room
1. Choose the **Room**. If you came from a room's page, it is already chosen.
2. If there is a **What kind of booking** box, choose one. Tick **We are a registered charity** if that is true.
3. Fill in **Date**, **Start** and **Finish**.
4. Choose **Time to set up first** and **Time to clear away after** if you need them. The room is held for that time too.
5. Fill in **What is it for?** and **How many people**. Choose **Set out as** if you want the room laid out a certain way.
6. Tick **We need sound or projection** or **We would like refreshments** if you need them, and say what.
7. Under **Your details**, give **Your name** and **Email**. **Phone** and **Group or organisation** are optional.
8. Read the messages under the form, then press **Send the request**.
9. The thank-you page gives your reference.

## Check when the room is free
1. The top of the page shows the chosen room's day, with busy times marked.
2. Your chosen time is shown on it. If the room is not free, or is closed that day, the form says so.
3. **More about this room** opens the room's own page.

## Ask for the same time every week
1. Choose **Repeats**: **Every week**, **Every two weeks** or **Every month, on the same weekday**.
2. Choose **Until**, the last date.
3. Each date is checked. Dates that are not free are left out. Untick any you do not want.

## See a price
1. For a kind of booking that is charged, **Your quote** shows the price line by line as you fill in the form.
2. For a repeating booking it also shows the total for all the dates.
3. It is an estimate from the price list. The office confirms the price when they confirm the booking.
4. If there is no price list for that room, it says the office will send you a price.

## Good to know
- A copy of your request is emailed to you, and the office is told. You are emailed again when the office has looked at it.
- If the time is taken just before you send, it asks you to look again.
- A room can never be asked for more people than its fire safety limit.
- The charity rate is checked by the office.

## Who to ask
- The church office, about prices, dates or your request. Use the email on the room's page.

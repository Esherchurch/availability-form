# Rota Planner

This is where the Sunday rota is built. You add the services for a term, put people in each slot, check who said they are free, and send everyone their rota by email.

## Who can use it
- Leaders and admins of the teams doing the church's worship, children's or youth work can open it, and those teams' core teams. So can master admins.
- If you run or lead one team, you see and change only your own team's slots. A line such as "2 more handled by another team" tells you other slots are there.
- On a service that only your team is ticked for (a youth service, say), you can fill all of its slots.
- Master admins see every slot.
- Anyone else is turned away.

## Add the services for a term
1. Choose the term in the **Term** box. It offers this term and the next three, each with its year, such as **Spring 2027**. **Spring** is January to April, **Summer** May to August and **Autumn** September to December.
2. Press **Populate**. The hub adds the church's main service on every Sunday of that term, in that term's year.
3. Press **Add Practices** to add a practice every week of the term. A small window asks:
   - **What kind**: the church's practices and meetings, from Your services.
   - **Day of the week**: Thursday to start with.
   - **Starts** and **Ends**: the kind's usual times to start with (7:30 to 9:30pm for a Sunday team practice). Change them if your practice is at another time.
   Press **Add**. Nothing is saved if you press **Cancel** or the cross.

## Add one service
1. Press **+ Service**.
2. Choose the **Date** (it starts on next Sunday) and **What kind**.
3. Press **Add**. Nothing is saved if you press **Cancel** or the cross.

## Change a service
Each service is a row. Changes save as you make them.
1. Change the date, the team's start and end times, or the kind of service.
2. Type anything for the team in **Notes...**.
3. Type the service leader and the speaker in their boxes. Names come up from the address book, or type a guest's name.
4. Choose a video call room, or **No video call**. **Join** opens the call.
5. Tick the teams that are needed. The boxes are the teams this rota is for: the worship core team, then the worship, AV, choir, youth and children's teams (Teams and groups). Ticking the children's team adds its usual slots.
6. If the church shows Sundays in What's On: tick **In What's On**, type the public title, the time it **Starts**, and **A line for the public (optional)**.

## Put people on the rota
1. Press the round button with the box on it, on the right of the row. It shows **Available**, **Declined** and **Pending** (not answered yet).
2. Drag a name from **Available** into a slot.
3. Drag a name from one slot to another to move them.
4. Press the small **×** on a name to take them off.
5. Use **+ Role** to add another slot, such as a second singer. The choices are the rota places of the worship, AV and choir teams, then the children's teams (Teams & groups, **Skills and rota places**). Point at a slot's name and press its **×** to remove the slot.
- A name in gold means that person has not said if they are free that day.
- A green dot on a row means every main slot is filled.
- **Sundays served this month** shows how many of those main services each person is on that month. Everyone in a slot counts, also when a slot has several people.

## Fill the rota for you
1. Press **Auto-Populate** and say yes.
2. It fills empty slots with people who said they are free, keeping to each person's limit per month. If the church has listed its own kinds of service in Your services, it fills every service kind that has rota places. If not, it fills the main service only (the first in the list); other services are synced like practices.
3. It fills the usual slots for that kind of service first, then extra players and singers: the other rota places of the worship, AV and choir teams. Each place is filled by someone who has its skill ticked in the address book.
4. It only fills slots for your own team.
5. It also copies each filled service's team onto the practice in the 4 days before it. **Sync** on a practice row does this for one practice.

## Ask people when they are free
1. Press **Gather Avail**.
2. Choose the term from the list and press **Next**.
3. Say yes. Everyone on the teams for that term gets an email with a link to the [availability form](index.html). It lists every service in the term.

## Say your team's part is done
1. On the term heading, press the button with your team's name and "not done".
2. It changes to a tick. Press it again to undo.
3. If anyone changes your team's slots later, the tick goes and you need to press it again.
- "waiting" means another team has not finished yet.

## Send everyone their rota
1. Type your email in **Sending as**. Replies come to you. Leave it empty and replies go to the church's enquiry email (Church settings).
2. Choose a term in **Send term**, or leave **All active terms**.
3. Press **Send all rotas** and say yes.
4. Each person gets an email with their dates, PDFs of their own rota, their household's rota, a wall planner and the full rota, and calendar files.
- It will not send until every team has ticked its part as done. It tells you who is still to finish.
- The emails and PDFs carry the church's name and logo from Church settings. If none is set, they are left off. Calendar entries start with the church's short name.

## Send or print one person's rota
1. Choose the person in the name box.
2. **Indiv** makes a PDF of their rota. **Home** makes one for their household. **Wall** makes a wall planner.
3. **Send** emails that person their rota.
4. **Full PDF** prints the whole rota. **Excel** saves it as a spreadsheet.

## Finish a term
1. Press **Archive** on the term heading and say yes. Its services leave the list.
2. Archived terms are listed at the bottom. Press **Restore** to bring one back.

## On a phone
- The top bar has the term, **+ Service** and **More**. **More** holds every other button.
- Tap a service to open it.

## Good to know
- Past dates are hidden. Press **Show past dates** to see them. This is remembered on this device.
- **Hide Assigned** hides people who are already on that day's rota from the lists.
- The bin on a row deletes that service for good. It cannot be undone.
- Removing a slot also takes off whoever was in it.
- The service leader and speaker are the same ones the [Preaching plan](preaching-plan.html) shows.
- The **?** button opens the step-by-step guide.

## Who to ask
- Your team leader.
- A master admin, if you cannot open the page or see the wrong slots.

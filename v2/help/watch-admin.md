# Watch

This page looks after what people see in "Listen & Watch": this week's live service, full services put up for the church, and the church's YouTube channel. Sermons are on their own page, [Sermons](sermons-admin.html).

## Who can use it

- Master admins, and the people a master admin has named to look after sermons (on the [Sermons](sermons-admin.html) page).
- Everyone else sees "Only the people who look after sermons can do this".
- If the page says "Watch is switched off for this church", a master admin can switch it on in "What does your church use?".

## Put up this week's live link

1. On YouTube, set up the live stream and copy its link.
2. Under **This week's live service**, paste it into **The live link from YouTube**.
3. Check **What to call it** (it starts as "Sunday morning").
4. Check **Show from** and **Show until**. These are filled in from the church's usual times (see below), and you can change them for this week.
5. Under **Then**, choose **The card disappears**, or **It becomes "Watch again" until:** and give a date and time.
6. Press **Save the live link**.

A "Watch live" card then shows on everyone's Home in the app, between the times you set. The card above the boxes tells you what is happening now: not showing yet, showing, showing as "Watch again", or finished.

To take it down early, press **Take it down**.

## Add a full service for the church

Use this for a service that is not public on the channel.

1. On YouTube, set the video to **unlisted**. A private video cannot be shared this way.
2. Under **Full services, for the church**, paste the link into **The video's link**.
3. Type **Its name** (for example "Morning service, 11 October").
4. Check the **Date**.
5. In **Who sees it**, choose **Everyone signed in** or **Church members only**.
6. Press **Add it**.

To take one out of the app, press **Remove** and confirm. It stays on YouTube.

## Connect the church's YouTube channel

1. Find the channel's id in YouTube Studio: Settings, Channel, Advanced settings. It starts UC and is 24 characters long.
2. Paste it into **The channel's id**.
3. Press **Save the channel**.

The channel is read every hour, and its latest public videos come in by themselves. The card says what the last read found, or why it did not work.

## Hide or show a channel video

1. Under **The church's YouTube channel**, find the video. Up to 15 of the latest are listed.
2. Press **Hide** to take it out of the app, or **Show** to put it back.

## Set the usual times for the live card

1. Under **The live card, every week**, choose the day in **The service is on**.
2. Set **Shown from** and **Until**. "Shown from" must be before "Until".
3. Under **Then**, choose **The card disappears**, or **It becomes "Watch again" for** a number of days (1 to 31).
4. Press **Save**.

These are what each new live link starts with. You can still change them for one week when you put up the link.

## Good to know

- Nothing here is ever shown to people who are not signed in.
- Live links and full services are unlisted on YouTube: anyone who has the link can pass it on.
- The link must be a YouTube video link.
- "Show until" must come after "Show from", and "Watch again" must end after the card does.
- Hiding a channel video or removing a full service changes nothing on YouTube.

## Who to ask

- For the live stream or the YouTube channel: whoever looks after sermons and Watch.
- To be added to the people who can use this page: the church office, who can ask a master admin.

# Event risk assessment

This is the health and safety risk assessment for one event. It adds only what the event changes to the building's own assessments. The organiser signs it, then the health and safety lead or deputy approves it.

## Who can use it
- The event's organisers and leaders, the office, the trustees, the health and safety leads and master admins can open it.
- The organisers, leaders, the office and the building's health and safety lead or deputy can change it while it is a draft.
- Anyone else is told who the page is for.

## Say what kind of event it is
1. Under **The event**, choose **What kind of event**.
2. Fill in **How many people, at the most**.
3. Tick anything that applies to the event.
4. Tick **The organiser wants an assessment anyway** if nothing else asks for one.
5. The page says whether the event needs an assessment, and why.

## Check the hub's own numbers
1. **From the hub's own numbers** shows what the hub already knows, such as room sizes and the building's assessments.
2. A red line means it must not go ahead as described. Change the event before it can be signed.

## Get a first draft from the AI
1. Describe the event in the box, and press **Get a first draft**.
2. Check every line, then press **Save**.
3. A suggested answer to a question has **Use it** next to it.

## Read the venue's own arrangements (away events)
1. Under **The venue**, fill in **Where**.
2. Paste the venue's web page addresses or words, and press **Read the pages or words**. Or press **Read its document**.
3. Check what was read. Untick anything wrong, and set any ratings it asks for.
4. Press **Add the ticked lines**.

## Choose drivers and vehicles
1. If the church is driving, tick the vehicles and drivers under **Drivers and vehicles**.
2. The hub checks each driver is cleared, and in date, on the day.

## Add hazards and actions
1. Under **Hazards: what the event changes**, press **+ Add a hazard**.
2. Fill in **What could go wrong**, **Who could be hurt, and how**, the ratings before and after, and **The controls**.
3. Under **Actions**, press **+ Add an action**. Fill in **What needs doing**, **Priority**, **By** and **Who**.
4. Use **Take this hazard off** or **Take this action off** to remove one.

## Plan the day
1. Under **The plan for the day**, write about stewards, first aiders, timings and who is in charge.
2. Press **Save**.

## Answer every question
1. Under **Before it is signed: every question answered**, answer each one.
2. If one does not apply, write "N/A: " and the reason.
3. If anyone may need care or support, use general words only. No names, phone numbers, emails or medical details.

## Sign and approve it
1. The organiser presses **Sign it as the organiser** once every question is answered.
2. The health and safety lead or deputy presses **Approve it as the health and safety lead**.
3. To change it later, press **Change it**. This starts a new version, to be signed again.

## Good to know
- Press **Save** often. Changes are not kept until you do.
- **Print** at the top gives a copy, once it has been saved.
- If under-18s are going, the event's safeguarding assessment applies too. It is kept separately.
- The hub helps you record the assessment. The responsible person remains responsible for it.

## Who to ask
- The building's health and safety lead or deputy.
- The church office.

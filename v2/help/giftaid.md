# Gift Aid

This page is where you make a Gift Aid declaration, so the church can claim an extra 25p from HMRC for every £1 you give, if you pay UK tax. The church office and the treasurer also use it to see everyone's declarations and to record paper ones.

## Who can use it

- Anyone signed in can open the page and make, change or cancel their own declaration.
- You only ever see your own declaration.
- The church office, the finance team (the treasurer) and master admins also see a list of everyone's declarations, can download it, and can record a declaration someone made on paper.
- Nobody else can see your declaration.
- If the page says "Gift Aid is not used by this church", the church has switched Gift Aid off and there is nothing to do here.

## Make your Gift Aid declaration

1. Open **Gift Aid**. If you have no declaration yet, you see **Make a Gift Aid declaration**.
2. Choose a **Title (optional)**: Mr, Mrs, Miss, Ms, Mx, Dr or Rev, or leave it blank.
3. Check your **First name** and **Last name**. They are filled in from your details, but you can change them.
4. Type your **House name or number**.
5. Type **The rest of the address (optional)**.
6. Type your **Postcode**.
7. Read the declaration wording in the shaded box. It is HMRC's standard wording, in the church's name.
8. Tick **Yes, I am a UK taxpayer and I want to Gift Aid my donations, as above.**
9. Press **Make my declaration**. You will see "Thank you. Your declaration is made."

The page then shows **Thank you**, with the date your declaration was made.

## Change your name or address

1. Open **Gift Aid**.
2. Press **Change my name or address**.
3. Change what you need to.
4. Press **Save**.

Please do this whenever you move or change your name.

## Cancel your declaration

1. Open **Gift Aid**.
2. Press **I no longer pay enough tax, or want to stop**.
3. Press **Cancel my declaration**, then confirm.

Cancelling stops Gift Aid on what you give from today. It does not change anything claimed before. If you want Gift Aid again later, just make a new declaration.

## See and download everyone's declarations (office and treasurer)

1. Open **Gift Aid**. Below your own part you see **Declarations**, with how many there are.
2. Each one shows the name, house and postcode, the date it was made, and whether it was made **in the app** or **on paper**.
3. Cancelled ones are listed separately under **Cancelled**, with the date they were cancelled.
4. Press **Download the list (CSV)** to save a spreadsheet for the Gift Aid claim.

The spreadsheet has these columns: Title, First name, Last name, House name or number, Postcode, Declaration date, How made, Status, Cancelled on. It includes cancelled declarations too.

## Record a declaration made on paper (office and treasurer)

Use this when someone has already filled in and signed a paper Gift Aid form.

1. Under **Record a declaration made on paper**, start typing the person's name in **Who** and choose them from the list. They must be in the church's address book.
2. Their first and last name are filled in for you. Check them, and add the title, house name or number, address and postcode from the form.
3. In **The date on the paper form**, give the date written on the form. It cannot be in the future.
4. Tick **The signed paper declaration is held by the church.**
5. Press **Record it**.

Keep the paper form safe.

## Good to know

- A declaration is never deleted. When cancelled, it is kept and marked as cancelled from that date. This is because HMRC expects the church to keep declarations.
- A cancelled declaration cannot be changed or switched back on. Make a new one instead.
- What was declared, and when, never changes. Only the name and address can be updated.
- The postcode must look like a UK postcode, and you need a first name, last name and house name or number.
- Your name, address and tax status are private: only you, the church office, the treasurer and master admins can see them.
- Please tell the church if you change your name or address, or no longer pay enough tax.
- This page does not give tax advice. The declaration wording explains your part: if you pay less tax in a year than the Gift Aid claimed on all your donations, it is your responsibility to pay the difference.

## Who to ask

- For your own declaration, or if something on the page won't save: the church office.
- For questions about Gift Aid claims: the treasurer.

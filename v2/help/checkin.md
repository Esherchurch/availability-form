# Check-in

This page checks people in and out at the door of an event. It also has the fire roll-call, which shows everyone in the building now.

## Who can use it
- An event's team, its leaders and master admins can check people in to that event.
- Anyone else is told check-in is for the event team and its leaders, and to ask whoever runs it to add them.
- The fire roll-call is for master admins and the people named for it in Safeguarding settings. Some are named for one building only.

## Choose the event
1. Open Check-in. It lists the events today and this week that you run or lead.
2. Press **Open** next to the event.

## Check someone in
1. Find them by typing in **Find by name**, or scroll the list.
2. Press **Check in** next to their name.
3. The tiles at the top show **In now**, **Expected**, **Not yet arrived** and **Checked out**.

## Scan a code
1. Press **Scan a code**. Let the page use the camera.
2. Hold the person's code up to the camera. The page says who is in, or if the code is for a different event.
3. Press **Stop scanning** when done.
4. A hand-held scanner works too. Scan into **Find by name**.

## Check someone out
1. Press **Check out** next to their name.
2. If people are listed to collect them, choose **Who is collecting?**, or **Someone else** and type the name.
3. Press **Check out**.
4. If the event hands children only to a named person, and the person is not on the list, the page warns you. Check with the parent first, and write down why they may collect.
5. **Back in** checks someone in again.

## Add a walk-in or a leader
1. Press **Walk-in**. Type their **Name**, and email and phone if you like. Press **Add and check in**.
2. A walk-in takes a place like anyone else. If the event is full, raise the number of places in Events first.
3. Press **Leader** for a leader. Type their name and press **Check in**. Leaders are listed under **Leaders here**, not in the headcount.

## Take payment at the door
1. A booking that owes money shows the amount **to pay** by the first person's name.
2. Press it, choose how it was paid, and press **Paid**.
3. To undo it, press the **Paid** label and choose **Not paid after all**.

## Print name labels
1. Press **Name labels**.
2. Choose **Everyone booked** or **Only those here now**. Untick **Add the event's name and date** if you do not want them.
3. Press **Print the labels**.

## Check-in settings
1. Admins press the settings button next to the other buttons.
2. Tick **Children may only be checked out to a named collector** if the event needs it.
3. Choose **Which question lists who may collect**.
4. Tick the answers to show as a medical flag at the door. Press **Save**.

## Fire roll-call
1. Press **Roll-call: who is in now**.
2. It shows everyone checked in today and not yet out, room by room, with leaders apart. It keeps itself up to date.
3. Children in Sunday groups are shown as numbers only.
4. Where there are people who need help to get out, their plans are shown for today only. Do not copy or share them.
5. Press **Print** for a paper copy.

## Good to know
- With no signal, check-ins are kept on the phone and sent when the signal comes back. Keep the page open.
- People with a **Medical** flag: press it to see the detail.
- Several people can check in on different phones at once. Each phone updates by itself.
- **Registers and downloads** opens the registers for this event.

## Who to ask
- Whoever runs the event, to be added to its team.
- The safeguarding lead, about collectors or medical details.
- A master admin, about the fire roll-call.

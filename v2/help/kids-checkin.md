# Sunday check-in

This page checks children into their Sunday groups and gives each family a label with a collection code. A child goes home only with someone on their list, or with that code.

## Who can use it
- The leads run the desk and see every group. They are master admins, the safeguarding lead and deputy, and the admins of the children's teams.
- A group's leaders see and check in their own group's children only.
- This morning's Session Leader on the rota sees the whole roll-call, until the end of the day.
- Anyone else is told the page is for the children's team leads and each group's leaders.
- If the church has more than one site, choose the **Site** at the top.

## Check in a family at the desk (leads)
1. Open the **Desk** tab.
2. In **Find the family**, type a name, the parent's phone or the family code. Or press **Scan their code**.
3. Tick the children who are here.
4. Press **Check in**.
5. Under **Labels**, press **Print the labels**, or show the screen. Each child wears theirs. The parent keeps the slip with the code.

## Check in a new family (leads)
1. Open the **First time here** tab.
2. Fill in the **Child's name**, **School year**, and any allergies or medical needs. Press **Add another child** for brothers and sisters.
3. Fill in the **Parent or guardian** and **Their mobile number**. Add **Their email** so they get the full registration form.
4. The parent ticks to agree, and says whether leaders may give basic first aid.
5. Press **Check in** and print the labels.

## Check in a child in your group
1. Open the **Groups** tab (or **Your group**).
2. Under **Not here yet**, press **Check in** by the child.
3. Give the collection code to the parent, with the label. Press **Print** or **Done**.

## Check out a child
1. Find the child and press **Check out**.
2. Choose who is collecting from their list.
3. Or choose **Someone else, with the collection code**. Type their name and **The code on their slip**.
4. Press **Check out**.
5. If the code does not match, do not let the child go. Find a lead and ring the parent.

## Call a parent
1. In your group, press **Details** by the child.
2. Press **Call on their phone**. It sends a message to the parent's phone. Or press **Ring** to phone them.
3. If there is no answer, use **Show on screen** as a last resort. It shows the family's number on the service screen, never the child's name or the code.
4. When the parent comes, press **Done: the parent is here**. The number also clears itself after five minutes.

## Fire roll-call
1. Open the **Roll-call** tab.
2. It shows every child in now, by group, and the leaders who have checked in.
3. Press **Print the roll-call** for a paper copy.

## Registers
1. Open the **Registers** tab and choose the **Group**.
2. Choose **Which weeks**: the latest week, this term, last term, or your own dates.
3. Press **Download (CSV, for Excel)** to save it.

## New families to welcome (leads)
1. Open the **Visitors** tab. Choose this week or the last four weeks.
2. Each family shows whether the full form is back.
3. Press **We have said hello** once someone has spoken to them.
4. Press **Send the form again** if the form is not back.

## Good to know
- Your group shows how many are in now, how many leaders are needed, and who has gone home.
- Allergies and medical needs of the children in the room show together at the top, for snack time.
- If a child's consent has run out, let them in. Press **Show the renewal QR code** so the parent can renew on their phone.
- Nobody new may collect a child until a lead adds them on the Children's register.
- The screen number can be switched off on the Children's register settings.

## Who to ask
- The children's team leads.
- The safeguarding lead, if something does not feel right at the door.

# Session planner

This page is where groups such as the children's or youth groups plan their sessions, week by week. The preaching team can also plan sermons here. Each plan can hold what to show on screen, so it can be presented from the Present page.

## Who can use it
- A group's leaders can add and change every plan of their group. Master admins can too.
- The group's team can read the plans, but not change them.
- Master admins also set up the groups, under **⚙ Groups**.
- Anyone else is asked to speak to a master admin about being added to a group.
- If the church does not use the session planner, the page says so.

## See the term
1. Tap your group's name at the top.
2. **The term** lists each plan by date, with who is on and who is away.
3. Tap a week to open its plan.
4. Press **Print the term** for a paper copy.

## Plan a week
1. Press **+ Add a** and the day (for example **+ Add a Sunday**). The next date is filled in.
2. Fill in the **Lesson title**. Choose a **Programme**, a **Lesson code** and a **Link to the lesson** if you use them.
3. Under **This week**, choose **A special week (say what)** or **No session this week** if it is not a normal week.
4. Press **Choose a passage** to add the Bible passage.
5. Press **Save**.

## Who is on and who is away
1. Press **Fill in from the rota**. It fills in who is on from the rota and who is away from the availability form.
2. To add someone by hand, pick them in **Add someone** and choose **as on** or **as away**.
3. Press the cross by a name to take them off.

## Build the session
1. Use **+ Add a part** to add a part, such as a game, story or prayer.
2. Tap a part to open it. Fill in the **Title**, **Minutes**, **Leader's notes**, **Things to bring** and any links.
3. Press **Upload a file** to add a worksheet, picture or video to the part. Save the plan first.
4. Drag the ⠿ handle to put the parts in order.
5. Press **Remove this part** to take one out.
- Everything people need to bring shows together under **Things to bring**.

## Add things to show on screen
1. In an open part, choose from **+ Show something**.
2. Pick **Bible verses**, **Memory verse**, **Picture**, **Video file**, **YouTube**, **Song**, **Timer**, **Text slide**, **Quiz**, **PowerPoint** or **Open a link**.
3. Fill in the small form and press **Add it**.
4. For a picture or video, upload it to the part first.
5. For a quiz, type each question, then its four answers with the right one first. Leave a blank line between questions.
6. Drag the steps to put them in order. Press the cross to remove one.

## Choose a Bible passage
1. Choose where it comes from: **Free Bibles**, **API.Bible** or **Bible Gateway**.
2. Choose the **Version** and type the **Passage**, for example John 3:16-21.
3. Press **Show it**, then **Import into the plan**. With Bible Gateway, the plan keeps only the reference and a link.

## Plan a sermon (preaching team)
1. Press **+ Plan a sermon for a service** and pick the service.
2. The date, speaker and readers come from the Preaching plan. Change those there, not here.
3. Add the **Sermon title**, **Series**, passage and the outline, then **Save**.

## Share, print or present
1. Press **Email the plan**. It goes to every leader of the group and this week's team.
2. Press **Print** for a paper copy.
3. Press **Present** to show the plan's slides, verses and pictures.
4. Press **Delete this plan** to remove it. This cannot be undone.

## Set up a group (master admins)
1. Press **⚙ Groups**.
2. Fill in the card under **+ A new group**: name, kind, ages, where and which day it meets, and the team.
3. Add leaders with **Add a leader**. They can edit every plan and get every plan by email.
4. Add any programmes and the usual parts of a session.
5. Press **Add the group**. Use **Save** or **Remove the group** on an existing one.

## Good to know
- If you leave without saving, the page asks first.
- The page says who last changed a plan, and when.
- PowerPoint slides can be added once the church goes live on the new hub.
- A YouTube video may show an advert. An uploaded video never does.
- Removing a group keeps its plans, but nobody can open them from here.

## Who to ask
- The group's leader, about a plan.
- A master admin, to be added to a group or to set one up.

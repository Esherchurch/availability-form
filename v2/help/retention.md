# Due for deletion

This page lists form answers whose time is up. Each form says how long its answers are kept. Nothing is deleted until you confirm it here.

## Who can use it
- Only master admins can use it, because only they can delete records.
- Other admins who open it are told the list is for master admins.

## Delete answers that are due
1. Look under **Due now**. Each answer shows who sent it, the form, who it was for, and the dates.
2. Every answer is ticked. Untick any you want to keep for now.
3. Press **Delete the ticked answers**.
4. Say yes to the question. The answers go, with their medical details and any files sent with them.

## See what is coming up
1. Look under **Due in the next 30 days**.
2. These cannot be deleted yet. They move to **Due now** when their day comes.

## Check the forms against the register
1. Look under **What the register says**. It lists how long each kind of record is kept.
2. If a form keeps its answers longer than the register says, a yellow note names it.
3. Change that form, or change the register in **Data records**.
4. If the register is not started yet, start it in **Data records**.

## Good to know
- Look at this page once a month.
- Answers with medical details are marked.
- A deleted answer cannot be brought back.
- What was deleted, and who did it, is kept in a log.
- Incidents and concerns are never on this list. They are kept.

## Who to ask
- A master admin.
- Whoever looks after data protection for the church.

# Health and safety settings

This page sets who looks after health, safety and fire at each building, and the numbers the risk assessments use.

## Who can use it
- The church office and master admins can open it and change everything.
- A building's fire safety lead can open it, but only to name who may open the fire roll call at their building.
- Anyone else is told who sets these.

## Name who looks after each building
1. Under **Who looks after health and safety**, find each building.
2. Choose the **Health and safety lead**, the **Deputy** and the **Fire safety lead**.
3. The page shows who the responsible person is on the building's fire assessment.
4. Press **Save**.
- The fire safety lead may be the health and safety lead too.

## Choose who may open the fire roll call
1. Under **The fire roll call**, find **Every building**, or one building.
2. Under **Add someone**, choose a person.
3. Choose **Fire warden** or **Duty steward** beside their name. Press **Remove** to take someone off.
4. Press **Save**.
- The roll call shows names only: who is checked in today, and how many. Master admins can always open it.

## Say how many people the church employs
1. Under **The people the church employs**, type how many people the church pays, including part-time.
2. Fill in **A larger staff team, from**.
3. Tick **Someone works at a screen** if that is part of someone's job.
4. The page lists what the law asks for. Press **Save**, and each duty is added as a check to the buildings.

## Record your insurance cover
1. Under **Insurance cover**, type your **Public liability cover** and **Employers' liability cover**.
2. Set **Flag public liability under** to the lowest cover you are happy with.
3. Press **Save**. A yellow note shows if the cover looks too low.

## Set target dates and AI drafts
1. Under **Actions, events and AI drafts**, set how many months a **High**, **Medium** and **Low** action has.
2. Set how many people make an event need a risk assessment.
3. Set how many AI drafts a day the church, and one person, may have. 0 turns AI drafting off.
4. Press **Save**.

## Hirers' own risk assessments
1. Under **Hirers' own risk assessments**, tick each kind of booking where the hirer must send their own.
2. Press **Save**. Room bookings then reads each one, and the office confirms it.

## Good to know
- Leave a box empty if you do not know yet. It is kept as not answered, not as 0.
- The insurance certificates are kept with the insurance checks in Building compliance.
- The hub helps the church record its assessments. The responsible person remains responsible.
- Add your buildings in Places first.

## Who to ask
- The church office.
- A master admin.

# E-news editor

This is where you build an e-news or email: add blocks, type on the email itself, and set its look. Scheduling and sending are on the [E-news](enews.html) page.

## Who can use it
- The church office and master admins.
- The e-news has to be switched on for the church. If it is off, a master admin can turn it on in Church settings, under "What does your church use?".

## Find your way round
- Along the top: **E-news** (back), the subject, **Saved**, **Send me a test** and **Done**.
- Down the left: **Add**, **Look** and **Check**. On a phone these are along the bottom, with **This block** too.
- Above the email: computer or phone view, undo and redo, and **Preview**.
- Your changes save as you go. "Saving…" then "Saved" shows at the top.

## Add a block
1. Press **Add**.
2. Drag a block onto the email where you want it, or tap it: it goes after the block you picked, or at the end.
3. **Blocks**: Picture, Heading, Words, Button, Line, Space, Video, Social links, Logo, Footer.
4. **From the hub** (filled in for you): The week's picture, Section bar, Event, Bible verse, This Sunday, Meeting, Dates for the diary, Notices.
5. **Layouts**: 1 column, 2 columns, 3 columns, Picture and words, Words and picture, Two pictures.
- **Event** lets you pick one from What's On in the next two months.
- **Footer** takes you to **Look**: the footer is the church's.

## Change a block
1. Tap a block on the email. It gets an outline and its settings open on the left.
2. Beside it: **Move up**, **Move down**, **Copy** and **Delete**. You can also drag its handle to a new place.
3. Type straight onto its words on the email. A toolbar shows across the top for style, font, size, line height, letter spacing, bold, italic, underline, crossed out, colour, highlight, link, emoji and lists.
4. The block's settings have three tabs: **Content**, **Style** and **Who sees it**.
5. **Style** sets background, border, corners and space around. **Reset** undoes it; **Use on all** copies it to every block of that kind.
6. Deleted something by mistake? Undo brings it back.

## Add a picture
1. Pick the picture block, then on **Content** paste a picture's link or choose one from the picture library shown below it.
2. Or press **Search Unsplash**. Its credit is shown under the picture in the email.
3. Fill in **Describe it** for people who can't see pictures.

## Add the Bible verse
1. Pick the Bible verse block. Type the **Reference**, for example "Col 3:23-24", and choose the **Version**.
2. Press **Bring it in**. You can still change the words afterwards.
3. The version you choose is remembered for next time.

## Keep things for church members only
- On a block's **Who sees it** tab, tick **Church members only**. It gets a gold edge on the email.
- For just some words (a Zoom passcode, say), select them and press **Members only** in the toolbar. Press it again inside them to make them for everyone.
- Members-only parts are left out of everyone else's copy, the web version and shares.
- The church members' meeting is always members-only.

## Look
1. Press **Look**. Change **This issue: top line** (the first words people see in their inbox).
2. Change the starting look, colours, footer details and social links, then press **Save the look**.
3. The look is used for this and every e-news after it.

## Check, test and preview
1. Press **Check** to see what still needs doing. **Go to it** takes you there.
2. Press **Preview** to see it on a computer or phone, as **Church members see** it or as **Everyone else sees** it.
3. Press **Send me a test** to send the members' copy to your own email. Put right what Check lists first.

## Done
Press **Done** for: **Schedule it**, **Preview web version and share it**, **Back to this e-news**, **All e-news and emails**, and **Save as a template**.
- For a welcome email, **Done** offers **Back to the welcome emails** instead of scheduling. Welcome emails send by themselves once switched on.

## Good to know
- Before it can be sent or tested, add this week's picture, verse and meeting (or remove those blocks), and fill in any [square brackets] in a template.
- Links need to start with https://.
- An email can't play a video, so people tap its picture to watch it.
- A sent e-news can be looked at but not changed.
- Undo and redo also work with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y.

## Who to ask
- Whoever looks after the e-news, or the church office.
- A master admin, if the e-news is switched off or you can't open the page.

# Teams & groups

This is the one list of every team and group the church has. The Home choices, "View the site as", the address book ticks and the events pages all use it. It also says which team does each job in the hub.

## Who can use it
- The church office and master admins can open it, add teams, and change names, colours, order and archiving.
- Only master admins can change who does each job, what a group runs, pin boards and core teams. The church office sees these but cannot change them.
- Anyone else is told they cannot open the page.

## Add a team or group
1. Under **Add a team or group**, type its name.
2. Tick **A group, not a rota team** if it is a group without a rota.
3. Press **Add**.
4. Choose the name carefully. It cannot be changed later, but its label can.

## Change a team's label or colour
1. Find the team under **The list**.
2. Type a new label in its name box. It saves when you leave the box.
3. Press the colour square to choose a new colour.
4. **Saved** shows when it has saved.

## Change the order
1. Use the up and down arrows next to a team.
2. The new order saves straight away.

## Rota or group
1. Tick **Has a rota** for a rota team.
2. Untick it for a group without a rota.

## Skills and rota places
1. On a rota team, press **Skills and rota places** to open its two lists.
2. **Skills** are what people on this team can do, such as Guitar or Singer. They are the ticks in the address book.
3. **Rota places** are the slots the team fills on the rota, in order. Choose the skill each place needs. The rota planner fills it with people who have that skill. **By hand (no skill)** means nobody is filled in for it automatically.
4. To add a skill or a place, type it under its list and press **Add**. For a place, choose its skill first.
5. To rename one, type over it. It saves when you leave the box. Renaming a skill renames it on this team's places too.
6. Use the up and down arrows to change the order, and the **×** to remove one. Removing a skill leaves its places to be filled by hand.
7. Numbered places, such as **Singer 1** and **Singer 2**, all need the same skill. The planner adds more of them when more people with the skill are free.
8. A team can have up to 60 skills and 100 places. Each name can be up to 60 letters long.
9. Tick **Every week** on the places a new date starts with: the team's standard week. For example, a Session Leader and two helpers. If none are ticked, a new date gets every place, with two of each numbered place.

## A team's logo and title
1. Every team has **Logo and title** on its box.
2. Press **Add a logo** and choose a picture under 2 MB. **Remove the logo** takes it away again.
3. Type a **title** in the box beside it, for example "Youth Team". Leave it empty to use the team's name.
4. The team's own pages and apps show these. The youth app's header shows the youth team's. Without a logo, they show the church's logo.

## Archive a team, or bring it back
1. Tick **Archived** on the team. It goes from choices around the hub.
2. To bring it back, tick **Show archived**, find the team, and untick **Archived**.

## Choose who does each job (master admins)
1. Under **Who does each job**, each job says what it means, and which teams do it.
2. To add a team, choose it from **Add a team…** next to the job.
3. To take a team off, press the **×** on its name.
4. A job can be done by several teams, or by nobody.

## What a group runs, pin boards and core teams (master admins)
1. **Runs**: tick **Today**, **People**, **Bookings** or **Send** for the parts of the hub this team runs.
2. **Pin board**: choose **None**, the team's own board, or a board shared with other teams.
3. **Core team for**: tick the teams this one leads. Being on this team then counts as being on each ticked team.

## Good to know
- Changes save as you make them. There is no Save button.
- Teams are never deleted. Archiving hides a team from choices but never takes it off anybody, so nothing is lost.
- If a name is already on the list, or archived, the page tells you. Tick **Show archived** to bring an archived one back.
- Names can use letters, numbers and spaces.
- **Works with under-18s (DBS needed)** is shown, not changed here. A core team for an under-18s team works with under-18s too.
- If nobody does the care and support job, care requests stay closed until a team is named.
- If nobody does a job, the pages for that job are hidden.
- Until anybody edits a team's skills or places, it keeps the ones the hub started with.
- Renaming or removing a skill or place does not change rotas already made, or what people have already ticked. Those keep the old name.
- A place that no team lists counts as the worship team's.

## Who to ask
- The church office, for adding or tidying teams.
- A master admin, for who does each job, what a group runs, pin boards and core teams.

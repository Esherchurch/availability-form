# Room bookings

This is where the people who look after rooms approve or decline requests, move or cancel bookings, and keep track of arrivals, setup, invoices, hirers and payments. Members book free rooms themselves on **Book a room**.

## Who can use it
- Admins can open it for every building.
- Each building's bookings admins can open it for the buildings they look after.
- Anyone else is told the page is for the people who look after room bookings, and pointed to **Book a room**.
- If there is more than one building, choose it in the **Site** box at the top.

## Approve or decline a request
1. Open **Waiting**. The number on the tab shows how many things need you.
2. Each request says whether **The time is still free**.
3. If it is a charged booking, check the **Charges**. You can change any line, **Add a line**, take a line off, or **Start again from the price list**.
4. Press **Approve**. The person is emailed, with the price and a link to accept the quote.
5. Or press **Decline**, say **Why not?**, and press **Decline it**. Your words go in the email.
6. Press **Change the time** to move a request before you decide.

## Approve a repeating request
1. A repeating request shows once, with every date and whether each is free.
2. Press **Approve every free date**, or **Approve** or **Decline** one date at a time.
3. **Decline them all** declines every date, with one reason.

## Book over something else
1. If a time is no longer free, approving or moving it is refused.
2. To book it anyway, type a reason and press **Book it anyway**. The reason is kept with the booking.

## Move or cancel a booking
1. Find it under **Coming up**.
2. Press **Move**, choose the **Room**, **Date**, **Start** and **Finish**, and press **Move it**.
3. Or press **Cancel**, say **Why?**, and press **Cancel the booking**.
4. For a repeating booking, **Cancel this and the ... after it** cancels the later dates too.
5. The person is emailed each time.

## Answer a request to cancel
1. A hirer can ask to cancel from their own page. It shows under **Waiting** as **Asking to cancel**.
2. Type **Your answer**, then press **Cancel the booking** or **Keep the booking**. Keeping it needs a reason.
3. If they had paid, you are asked to record the refund.

## A room that is closed
1. A closed room shows under **Waiting**, with who is booked in it on those days.
2. Press **Warn the people booked** to email them all. Their bookings stay; you decide what next.
3. Press **Open it again** when the room can be used.

## Today's arrivals
1. Open **Today**. It lists today's bookings and whether people have arrived.
2. Press **Mark arrived** when they get there, or **Not arrived after all** to undo it.
3. A hire or member's booking not marked arrived a while after it starts is shown in red, and the bookings email address is told once. Nothing is cancelled.

## Day view and setup sheet
1. **Day view** shows every room on one day, with what is booked.
2. **Setup sheet** lists what to set up in each room: times, people, layout, sound, kit, refreshments and notes. The kitchen's list is under each day.
3. Choose **One day** or **A week**, then press **Print**.

## Payments and deposits
1. On a confirmed booking with charges, press **Record a payment**. Fill in **Amount**, **How**, **Date** and an optional **Reference**.
2. Press **Record the payment**, or **Record a refund**. The payer is emailed a receipt.
3. **Issue an invoice** sends one booking's invoice by email.
4. **Damage deposit taken** and **Return the damage deposit** keep track of the deposit. If you keep some, say why.

## Invoices, hirers and accounts
1. **Invoices**: choose a **Month** and press **Issue the monthly invoices** for hirers billed monthly. Every invoice sent is listed.
2. **Hirers**: press **Add a hirer**, or **Open** one. Fill in their details and tick charity, regular or monthly invoicing. Add their insurance and other documents with an expiry date.
3. A hire request from someone with no record shows **Add as a hirer**.
4. **Accounts**: choose the dates and download every charge, payment and refund as **CSV**, **Excel** or **JSON**.

## Good to know
- A building's colour for building checks may show at the top. It is a warning only; bookings carry on.
- A hirer's document that has expired, or expires soon, is flagged on their bookings.
- Some kinds of hire need the hirer's own risk assessment. Put it up with **Put up their risk assessment**, check it, then press **Confirm their risk assessment**.
- **Room use** shows each room's hours booked in a month, and can be downloaded.
- **Past and cancelled** keeps older bookings.

## Who to ask
- A master admin, if you cannot open the page or a building is missing.
- Whoever sets up rooms and prices, if a price list is missing.

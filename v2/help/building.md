# The building

This page is for the people who look after the building's safety checks. It shows the checks given to you, the jobs to do, and a big button to report a problem.

## Who can use it
- Anyone signed in can open it.
- You only see the checks that have been given to you. If none have, it says "Nothing has been given to you yet."
- Anyone can use **Report a problem**.
- Building compliance has to be switched on for the church. If it is off, the page says so.

## See what needs doing
1. Open the page.
2. **Due now** lists checks that are overdue or due soon.
3. **Jobs to do** lists maintenance jobs given to you, or raised from your checks.
4. **Coming up** lists checks that are in date.

## Record a check done
1. Press the check.
2. Read **How**, if it is there.
3. Fill in **Done on**. Add a **Photo or certificate** and a **Note** if you like.
4. Press **Done**. The page shows when it is next due.

## Do the extinguisher walk-round
1. Press the extinguisher check.
2. Go room by room. Tick each extinguisher or blanket as you see it, or as it is serviced.
3. Press **Done**. It says how many you ticked.
4. If one is missing or has been used, press **One is missing or used**.

## Something is wrong with a check
1. On the check, press **Something is wrong**.
2. Choose **Where?**, and say **What is wrong?**
3. Leave **It is a safety problem** ticked if it is.
4. Press **Report it**. It goes on the Maintenance list, marked safety.

## Report a problem
1. Press **Report a problem** at the top.
2. Choose **Which building?** and **Where?**
3. Say **What is wrong?** The page may tick **It is a safety problem** for you. Change it if it is wrong.
4. Press **Report it**. It goes on the Maintenance list.

## Working on your own
1. Press **Working here on your own? Sign in**.
2. It takes you to the Working alone page.

## Good to know
- Each check is marked **Legal requirement**, **Recommended standard** or **Good practice**.
- Until the church goes live on the hub, checks cannot be recorded here. Keep your paper record for now. **Report a problem** still works.
- Press the arrow at the top to go back to the list.

## Who to ask
- The church office.
- Whoever gave you the check.

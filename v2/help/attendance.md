# Registers

This page is for one event. It lets you download who came, print a door sheet, and print paper registers for when there is no signal.

## Who can use it
- The event's team, its leaders and master admins can open it.
- Anyone else is told the lists for this event are for its event team, and to ask whoever runs it to add them.
- You open it from an event: **Events**, then **Who is coming**, then **Registers and downloads**. Opened any other way, the page tells you this.

## Download who came
1. Under **Download who came**, tick the columns you want, such as **Name**, **Booked by**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Came** and **Checked in**.
2. The event's questions are listed too. Ones marked **Medical** start unticked.
3. Press **CSV** or **Excel**.
4. The heading shows how many came out of how many booked, and how many walk-ins.

## Print a door sheet
1. Under **Door sheet**, tick anything else to show, such as **Phone** or one of the questions.
2. Press **Show and print** to see it on screen, then **Print**. Press **Close** when done.
3. Or press **Door sheet (PDF)** to save it as a file.
4. The sheet lists people by surname, with a tick box, their ticket, places, and what is still to pay. It is not kept as a record of who came.

## Print registers for when there is no signal
1. Under **Printed registers**, press **Register (PDF)**. It has tick boxes for in and out, and a few spare rows.
2. Press **Walk-in sign-in sheet (PDF)** for a sheet where walk-ins write their names.
3. If the event is one of a series, **Registers for every date (PDF)** prints one page for each date.
4. When the signal is back, enter the ticks in **Check-in**.

## See attendance across a series
1. If the event is one of a series, **Across the series** shows each person down the side and each date across the top.
2. **Yes** means they came. **No** means they booked but did not come. Blank means they were not booked that date.
3. Press **CSV** or **Excel** to download it.

## Good to know
- Every download is recorded first. If it cannot be recorded, no file is made.
- Any download or door sheet with a **Medical** column records who made it, when, and which columns.
- **Medical** columns are only for the people looking after this event.
- A **Medical** column only appears on the printed register if you ticked it under **Download who came**.
- The buttons at the top go to **Check-in**, **Headcounts** and **Events**.

## Who to ask
- Whoever runs the event, to be added to its team.
- The church office, for anything else.

# Live risk register

This page shows how the church manages, day to day, the risks in its professional health, safety and fire risk assessments. It adds to those assessments. It does not replace them, and the trustees remain responsible for the building's safety.

## Who can use it
- The office, the trustees and master admins can open it. The page names who does the office and trustees jobs.
- The office keeps it: hazards, reports and the plan for visitors who need help.
- The trustees read it and sign off snapshots.
- Building compliance has to be switched on for the church. If it is off, the page says so.
- Until the church goes live on the hub, the page is read only and nothing is saved. Keep your paper record for now.

## Choose a building and a view
1. If there is more than one building, press its name at the top.
2. Choose a view: **Hazards**, **Professional assessments**, **Changes since**, **Snapshots** or **Visitors who need help (GEEP)**.

## Check the hazards
1. Open **Hazards**. The dials and **The building's own assessments** are at the top.
2. Each hazard has a colour. It comes from the building's checks that control it.
3. Press a hazard to see its **Controls (the checks)** and its **Issues**, open or resolved.

## Add hazards (office)
1. Press **Add the starter hazards** to add a ready-made list. Each comes with this building's checks as its controls.
2. Or press **+ Add a hazard**. Fill in **What could go wrong**, **Who could be hurt**, **Likelihood** and **Severity**, and press **Add it**.

## Change or close a hazard (office)
1. Press the hazard.
2. Under **Change it**, set **Likelihood** and **Severity**, choose its **Controls**, and add **Notes**.
3. Press **Save**. Or press **Close the hazard**.

## Put up a professional report (office)
1. Open **Professional assessments**.
2. Under **Put up a report**, choose **Which**: fire, or health and safety.
3. Fill in **Done on**, **Next review** and **The assessor**. Add **The report (PDF)** if you have it.
4. Press **Put it up**. The review check is recorded, and due again on the next review date.

## See what has changed
1. Open **Changes since**.
2. It lists hazards and checks added or changed since the last professional report.
3. Tell your assessor about these at the next review.

## Sign off a snapshot (trustees)
1. Open **Snapshots**.
2. Under **Sign off and keep a snapshot**, add a **Note** if you like.
3. Press **Sign off and keep a snapshot**. A dated copy is kept with your name.
4. Press **Open** on an old one to read it, or **Print or save as PDF**.

## The plan for visitors who need help (office)
1. Open **Visitors who need help (GEEP)**.
2. Fill in how you find out who needs help, where they wait, who helps them, and any equipment.
3. Press **Save the GEEP**. It is due for review a year later.

## Good to know
- The building's own health and safety and fire assessments are linked under **The building's own assessments**, with a **Print** link.
- Their findings that are still Medium or High are listed, with the checks that watch them.
- The duty team see the visitors plan on the fire roll-call.
- Safeguarding has its own register. Trustees see a link to it.

## Who to ask
- The church office.
- The trustees, about snapshots and sign-off.

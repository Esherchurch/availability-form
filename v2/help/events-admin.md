# Events

This is where the church's events are made and run. You set up sign-ups, see who is coming, keep a checklist, invite people and collect photos.

## Who can use it
- Admins see every event and can make new ones.
- Someone on an event's team who is not an admin sees **Events I run**: only their own events. They cannot make new events. Rooms, times and the under-18s settings are left to an admin.
- Anyone else is told to ask whoever runs an event to add them to its team.
- Only an event's team and master admins see who has signed up.

## Make a new event
1. Press **New event**.
2. On **Details**, fill in the **Title**, **Category**, **Description**, **Starts** and **Ends**.
3. To repeat it, choose **Repeat** and **Until**. Each date is its own event, joined in a series.
4. Under **Where**, choose **A room here**, **Somewhere else** or **Online**. For a room, choose the **Site**, the **Rooms**, and any time to set up or clear away.
5. Choose **Who can come** and the **Status**. A new event starts as not listed.
6. Add people to the **Event team**. They can change the event, see who is coming and check people in.
7. Add a **Picture**: **Upload picture** or **Choose from library**.
8. Press **Save**. Saving books the rooms.

## If a room is taken
1. The page says what is in the room then.
2. Press **Use … instead** to pick a free room.
3. Or, as an admin, say why and press **Book over it and save**.

## Under-18s and safeguarding
1. Tick **Works with under-18s** for a kids club or youth event. Every leader and helper then needs an in-date DBS check and training.
2. Choose **What kind of event is it?**. A trip, overnight, holiday club, off-site event or camp needs the safeguarding lead to sign it off before bookings open.

## Open sign-ups
1. Open the event and press the **Sign-ups** tab.
2. Tick **Let people sign up** and set **How many places altogether**.
3. Choose if **People may cancel their own place**, and if you **Offer an optional donation**.
4. Choose **Sign up to**: **This date only** or **The whole series**.
5. Under **Tickets**, press **Add a ticket type**. Set its name, price and places, and **How people can pay**.
6. Under **Questions**, press **Add a question**. Choose the answer type, and whether it is asked **Once per booking** or **For each person**.
7. Press **Save**.

## See who is coming
1. Press the **Who is coming** tab. It lists **Coming**, **Waiting list** and **Cancelled**, with their answers and what they owe.
2. Press **Came** to mark someone as there, or **Cancel** to cancel their place.
3. On the waiting list, press **Offer a place** to email them a link.
4. Press **Download the list**. Tick **Include medical details** only if you need them. That download is recorded.
5. Press **Email everyone** to write to everyone coming.
6. The buttons here also open **Check-in**, **Registers and downloads**, **Forms**, **Safeguarding** and **Health and safety**.

## Keep a checklist
1. Press the **Checklist** tab.
2. Type the **Task**, choose **Who** and **When**, and press **Add**. Tick **Email them about it** to tell them.
3. Tick a task when it is done. Overdue tasks show in red.
4. Use **Use a template**, or save this list as one. A template can start every new event in its category.
5. Your own tasks on any event show at the top of the page under **Your tasks**.

## Invite people
1. Press the **Invitations** tab.
2. Choose **Chosen people**, **A team** or **A small group**.
3. Write **Your message** and press **Send the invitations**. Each person gets their own email.
4. The list shows who is coming, who can't, and who has not replied. Press **Remind the … who have not replied** to nudge them.

## Collect photos
1. Press the **Photos** tab. Set how many days and photos, and press **Make a link**.
2. Share it with **Copy link**, **QR code** or **Share on WhatsApp**.
3. Under **Waiting to be looked at**, press **Approve** or **Reject**. Nothing is shown anywhere until approved.
4. Press **Download all approved (zip)** to keep them.

## Change, copy, cancel or delete
1. **Duplicate** copies everything but the sign-ups. Check the date, then save.
2. **Cancel the event** emails everyone who signed up and gives the rooms back.
3. **Delete the whole series** removes every date. Events with payment, safeguarding, health and safety or accident records cannot be deleted. Cancel them instead.
4. Tick several events in the list to change, download or delete them together.
5. **History** shows what changed and what was emailed.

## Good to know
- Sunday services come from the rota. Change them in the Rota Planner.
- **For the notices** gives the coming events as words to paste into the notice sheet.
- **On the church website** has the code to show What's on on the church's own website.
- **Event categories** lets you rename, recolour, add or retire categories.
- **Deleted events** lists every deleted series, who deleted it, and when.

## Who to ask
- Whoever runs an event, to join its team.
- The church office, for rooms and times.
- The safeguarding lead, for under-18s events.

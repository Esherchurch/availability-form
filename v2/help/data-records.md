# Data records

This page keeps the church's data protection records: what personal data the church holds and why, every data breach, and every request from someone about their data.

## Who can use it
- The church office and master admins can use the register, the data requests and **Who keeps these**.
- The data protection leads can use the register, the breaches and the data requests.
- The trustees can read the register, but not change it. The trustees are whichever team does the trustees job under "Who does each job" in Teams & groups.
- Anyone else is told these are kept by the church office and the data protection lead, and the trustees read the register.

## Start the register
1. Open **Information assets**.
2. If it is empty, press **Start from what the hub holds**. This adds one row for each kind of personal data the hub keeps.
3. Check each row, and press **Change** to put anything right.
4. If the hub later holds something new, a button **Add what the hub holds now** shows. Press it to add the new rows.

## Add or change an information asset
1. Press **Add one (paper, a spreadsheet…)** for something the hub does not hold, or **Change** on a row.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Why the church holds it** and **Lawful basis**.
3. Tick **Special category** for things like health or religious belief, and fill in **The condition for special category data**.
4. Tick **Whose data** it is. Fill in **What is held**, **Who sees it** and **Who processes it for the church**.
5. Set how long it is kept: **Kept for** (in days; 0 means until they ask to stop), **Counted** (from when) and **Then** (deleted, made anonymous or reviewed).
6. Fill in **Who is responsible (a role)**.
7. Tick **I have reviewed it today** if you have checked it.
8. Press **Save**. **Cancel** leaves without saving.
9. If the church no longer holds it, press **No longer held**.

## Record a breach
1. Open **Breaches** and press **Record a breach**.
2. Fill in **In a few words**, **It happened on** and **It was found**.
3. Fill in **What happened**. Use general words only: no names.
4. Tick **What kind** and **Which data**. Fill in **About how many people** and **The risk to them**.
5. Press **Save**.
6. A yellow note shows the time by which you must decide about telling the ICO (72 hours after it was found).

## Finish a breach
1. On **Breaches**, press **Open** on the breach.
2. Under **The ICO**, choose **Reported** or **Not reportable**. If reported, fill in the date and reference.
3. Fill in **Why** if it was not reported, or was reported late.
4. Fill in **The people affected**, **Told on**, **What was done about it**, and **What we learned, and what changes**.
5. Press **Save**, or **Close it** when it is finished.

## Record a data request
1. Open **Data requests** and press **Record a request**.
2. Fill in **Who asked**, **They asked** and **Received on**.
3. Under **Who they are**, say whether you have checked who they are.
4. Press **Save**. The list shows the date it is due by.
5. When it is done, press **Open**, set **Where it is** to **Answered** or **Refused**, fill in **Answered on** and **What was done**, and press **Save**.
- For a complex request, tick **Extended by two months** and fill in why.
- To find what the church holds about the person, use [What we hold about someone](privacy.html).

## Choose the data protection leads
1. Open **Who keeps these** (church office and master admins only).
2. Pick a person under **Add someone…**. It saves at once.
3. Press **×** next to a name to take them off.

## Good to know
- Rows not reviewed for a year say **Review it**.
- A data request must be answered within one month of receiving it. A complex one can take three months, if you tell them why within the first month. Overdue requests show in red.
- Every breach is recorded, whether or not it is reported.
- A breach cannot be closed until the ICO decision is recorded, and why, if it was not reported.
- A refused request must say why. An extended request must say why.
- Up to 10 data protection leads. Master admins can always do what a lead can.

## Who to ask
- The church's data protection lead.
- The church office.
- A master admin, if you cannot open the page.

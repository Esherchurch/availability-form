# Event

This page shows one church event, with all its details. Where booking is open, you sign up for it here.

## Who can use it
- Anyone can open a public event, signed in or not.
- Some events are only for attenders, church members or a team. Others are told "That event could not be found, or it is not open to you."
- If you are signed in, your sign-up is linked to your record, and your name and email are filled in for you.

## See the event
1. Open the event from What's on, or from a link you were sent.
2. You see when and where it is, who is organising it, and the write-up.
3. Where booking is open, it says how many places are left, or **Full — waiting list open**.
4. Press the picture to see it whole.

## Sign up
1. Under **Sign up**, fill in **Your name** and **Your email**. **Phone** is optional.
2. If there is more than one ticket, choose the **Ticket**.
3. Under **Who is coming**, type each person's name. Press **Add someone else** for more people, or the cross to remove one.
4. Answer any questions. A star (*) means you must answer it.
5. Add a donation if the event asks for one. It is optional.
6. Use **Anything we should know?** for anything else.
7. Press **Sign up**.
8. You see **You are signed up**, and a confirmation email is on its way.

## Sign up again quickly
1. If you have signed up before, press **Same as last time** to fill in the same people and answers.
2. If the hub knows your household, tick who is coming under **Who is coming from your household?**.
3. Check anything marked **From last time**. If health answers were filled in, tick the box to say you have checked them.
4. On a shared device, press **Not you? Clear** to forget the names.

## Join the waiting list
1. If the event is full, the form says **Join the waiting list**.
2. Fill it in the same way and press **Join the waiting list**.
3. You get an email, and another if a place comes free.

## A series of dates
1. Some events sign you up to every date in a series at once. The page lists the dates.
2. Press **Sign up to all … dates**.
3. If any date is already full, you go on its waiting list.

## Pay for your place
1. If there is something to pay, the page shows how much before you sign up.
2. After signing up, press **Pay by card** or **Pay by bank** where the church takes payment online. You pay on a secure page, and the money goes straight to the church.
3. Otherwise you pay at the door on the day.

## Share the event or add it to your calendar
1. Press **Add to calendar** to put it in your own calendar.
2. Press **Share on WhatsApp** to send it to someone.
3. Public events also have **Facebook**, **X** and **Email** buttons.

## Good to know
- Keep the link you get after signing up. It lets you see or cancel your place, even if the email has not come.
- If someone takes the last place while you are filling in the form, the page says so. Reload it to join the waiting list.
- If the event is cancelled, or bookings are closed, there is no sign-up form.
- **Bookings open after safeguarding sign-off** means the event is waiting to be checked. Please come back soon.
- Until the church goes live on the new hub, health questions are not asked here. Please tell the organiser directly.
- If you were sent an invitation, your name and email are filled in for you.

## Who to ask
- The organiser named on the event.
- The church office, if you think you should see an event and cannot.

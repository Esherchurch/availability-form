# What we hold about someone

This page finds everything the church's hub holds about one person. Use it when someone asks to see their data, or to check before deleting someone. It searches everything on the [information asset register](data-records.html).

## Who can use it
- The church's data protection leads and master admins can use it.
- Anyone else is told it is for the data protection leads and master admins. If you want to know what the church holds about you, ask the church office and they will pass it on.
- Data protection leads are chosen by the church office on [Data records](data-records.html), under **Who keeps these**.

## See what we hold about someone
1. Under **Who**, start typing a name and pick it from the list. Or type their email address.
2. If this is for a data request, pick it under **For a data request**. This is optional. Only open requests are listed.
3. Press **See what we hold**.
4. You see their name, their email addresses, whether they have ever signed in to the hub, and how many records were found.
5. Below that is one box for each kind of data on the register. Each says why the church holds it, the lawful basis, how many records there are, and what those records hold.

## Download the export
1. After a search, press **Download the export**.
2. A file downloads with every record shown on the page.
3. If you picked a data request, the download is logged against that request.
4. Check the file before you send it. Remove anything that is about someone else.

## Print a summary
1. After a search, press **Print this summary**.
2. The search box and buttons are left off the printout.

## Good to know
- Care, pastoral and safeguarding records are counted but never shown here. They say "Held, and not shown here" and who to ask. Ask that person to provide those records. They are left out of the export too.
- If a name is not in the address book, the page says so. Pick from the list, or type their email address instead.
- "Nothing on the register names this person" means no records were found.
- Record the request itself, and its deadline, on [Data records](data-records.html), under **Data requests**.

## Who to ask
- The church's data protection lead.
- A master admin, if you cannot open the page.

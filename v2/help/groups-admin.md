# Small groups

This page is where small groups are run. It holds the groups, who leads them, who is in them, their meetings and registers, and requests to join.

## Who can use it
- Groups admins: master admins, and the admins of the teams a master admin chooses. They add groups, choose leaders and see every group.
- A group's leaders. They see their own groups, change the details, look after members and answer requests.
- Anyone else is sent to **Find a group**.
- If nobody has been chosen to look after groups yet, the page says "Not set up yet".

## Add a group (groups admins)
1. On the **Groups** tab, press **Add a group**.
2. Fill in the **Name**, **Kind**, and **What it is**. That text shows on Find a group.
3. Choose the **Day**, **Time** and **How often**.
4. Choose **Where**: **A home**, **A room at church**, **Somewhere else** or **Online**.
5. Choose **Who can come**, and add leaders with **Add a leader**.
6. Press **Save**.

## Change a group's details
1. On the **Groups** tab, press **Edit** next to the group.
2. Change what you need. A home address or an online link is seen only by the group's members.
3. Untick **Taking new members** to stop new requests. Set **Most members** to 0 for no limit.
4. Add a **Picture** if you like, then press **Save**.
- Only groups admins can change the leaders, who can come, or whether it is **Running**.

## Groups with under-18s
1. Tick **Includes under-18s** on the group.
2. Its leaders then need an in-date DBS check and safeguarding training, the same as children's leaders.
3. The group shows each leader's checks. A master admin can press **Record a check**.
4. If a leader is not cleared, the save is refused. A master admin or the safeguarding lead can press **Record an exception**, give the reason, then save again.
5. Leaders also fill in the leader declaration, sent from Forms.

## Add or remove members
1. Open the **Members** tab and choose the **Group**.
2. To add someone, pick them from the address book list.
3. To take someone out, press **Remove** and say yes.
- A full group cannot take more. Raise its limit first.

## Answer requests to join
1. Open the **Requests** tab. The number shows how many are waiting.
2. Press **Accept** or **Decline**.
3. They get an email. If accepted, it says when and where the group meets.

## Meetings and the register
1. Open the **Meetings** tab and choose the **Group**.
2. Press **Notes** to add notes or a study plan. Members see them. Tick **Not meeting this time** if it is off.
3. Press **Register**, tick who came, add **Guests**, then **Save the register**.
4. To add a date not listed, use **Another date** and **Add**.
5. Under **Download who came**, pick the dates and press **Download (CSV, for Excel)**.

## Message the group
1. Open the **Message** tab and choose the **Group**.
2. Write a **Subject** and **Message**, then press **Send to the group**.
3. Each member gets their own email. They can read it again on Find a group.

## See every group (groups admins)
1. Open the **Oversight** tab.
2. You see each group's members, leaders, when it last met, how many usually come, and the trend.
3. **People in no group** lists people in the address book who are in no group and lead none.

## Choose the groups admins (master admins)
1. Open the **Settings** tab.
2. Tick the teams whose admins look after small groups, then press **Save**.

## Good to know
- Members' email addresses stay private from each other when you message the group.
- Every download of a register is noted.

## Who to ask
- The groups admins, or the church office.
- The safeguarding lead, for under-18s groups.
- A master admin, if the page is not set up yet.

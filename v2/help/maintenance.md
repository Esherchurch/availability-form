# Maintenance

This is where anyone at the church reports something broken, and the buildings team keeps track of the jobs. It is also where a room can be closed for repairs.

## Who can use it
- Anyone signed in can report a job and see the list.
- The buildings team, admins and each building's bookings admins can mark jobs done, open them again and see the photos.
- The same people can close a room.

## Report a job
1. Press **+ Report a job**.
2. Under **Where**, choose the room, or **Somewhere else** and type **Where exactly**.
3. Fill in **What is wrong**, and **More detail (if it helps)**.
4. Add **A photo (optional)**. Only the buildings team and the office see it.
5. Press **Report it**. The buildings team will see it.

## Mark a job done
1. Find it under **To do**.
2. Press **See the photo** if there is one.
3. Press **Mark done**. You can add a note about what was done.
4. If the job came from a building check, you may be asked to record that check as done too.
5. It moves to **Done**, with who did it and when. Press **Open again** if it is not fixed after all.

## Close a room
1. Under **Close a room**, choose the **Room**.
2. Choose **From** and **To (the last day)**.
3. Say **Why**, for example "Repainting".
4. Press **Close the room**. It disappears from Book a room on those days, and the office is told.
5. The office warns anyone already booked from Room bookings.

## Open a room again
1. Closed rooms are listed under **Close a room**.
2. Press **Open it again** when the room can be used.

## Good to know
- The **Done** list shows the last 20 jobs done.
- A link in the buildings team's email opens the page at that job.
- Closing a room does not cancel bookings. The office decides what to do.

## Who to ask
- The buildings team, about a job.
- The church office, about a closed room or bookings.

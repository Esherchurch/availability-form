# Giving details

This page holds the church's bank details. People who have signed in see them on the Give screen in the app and on the Give page on the website. You type them in once, and they are used everywhere.

## Who can use it

- The church office and master admins can open this page and change the details.
- Other people cannot open this page. They only see the details on the Give screen, and only once they have signed in.
- Master admins can also change the same details in Church settings. It is the same form, so both always match.

## Put in or change the bank details

1. Open **Giving details**.
2. Type the **Account name**.
3. Type the **Sort code**. It is six digits; you can type it with or without the dashes.
4. Type the **Account number**.
5. Type a **Suggested reference**. This is what you would like people to put in the reference box of their bank payment, so the treasurer can tell who it is from (for example, their own name).
6. Choose what to say about **Gift Aid** (see below).
7. If you like, type one line in **Anything else to say**. It is shown under the bank details.
8. Press **Save the giving details**. You will see "Saved."

## Mention Gift Aid

1. In **Gift Aid**, choose **Mention Gift Aid, with a link to the form**. (Choose **Do not mention Gift Aid** to leave it out.)
2. A box called **Link to the Gift Aid form** appears. Paste the web address of the declaration form. It must start with https://.
3. Press **Save the giving details**.

If you leave the link empty, the Gift Aid line still appears, but without a link.

## Good to know

- Every box starts empty for a new church. Nothing is filled in for you.
- Saving replaces the details that were there before. There is no history of old details on this page, so check the numbers carefully before you save.
- The bank details are not shown to anyone who is not signed in. This makes it harder for someone to copy them into a convincing fake.
- Only the church office and master admins can change where people are asked to send money. Other admins cannot.
- Account name: up to 120 characters. Suggested reference: up to 60. Anything else to say: up to 400.

## Who to ask

- For the right bank details: the treasurer.
- If you cannot open the page or save: a master admin.

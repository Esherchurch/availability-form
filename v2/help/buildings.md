# Your buildings

This page lists each building the church uses, its rooms, how each room can be set out, and who looks after its safety. Photos, prices and opening hours are kept in Places.

## Who can use it
- Admins can open it and change it.
- Only a master admin can turn building checks on or off.

## Add a building
1. Under **Your first building** or **Another building**, fill in **Name**, **Postcode** and **Address**.
2. Press **Add the building**.

## Change a building's details
1. Find the building's card.
2. Change its **Name**, **Postcode** or **Address**.
3. Press **Save the building**.

## Add a room
1. On the building's card, under **Its rooms**, fill in **A new room** and **How many people it holds**.
2. Press **Add the room**.
- Add each room people meet in or book, including the hall and the church itself.

## Change a room and how it can be set out
1. Press the room's name to open it.
2. Change its **Name** or **How many people it holds**.
3. For each way the room can be set out, type how many people it holds. Leave it blank for a way it cannot be set out.
4. Press **Save the room**.

## Name who looks after its safety
1. On the building's card, under **Who looks after its safety**, choose the **Health and safety lead**, the **Deputy** and the **Fire safety lead**.
2. Press **Save who looks after it**.
- The lead and the deputy must be two different people. The fire safety lead may be the lead.
- They are told about the building's checks, its assessments and anyone working alone there.

## Turn building checks on or off (master admins)
1. Under **Building checks**, tick or untick **The church uses building checks**.
2. It saves straight away. Turning it off deletes nothing.

## Good to know
- Building checks cover fire and health and safety checks with their dates, risk assessments, and working alone.
- Each message beside a button tells you if it saved.

## Who to ask
- The church office.
- A master admin, to turn building checks on or off.

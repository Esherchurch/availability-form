# Video meeting (guest link)

This is the page a guest sees when they open the link in a meeting invitation. It lets someone who is not in the hub join one video meeting.

## Who can use it
- Anyone with the link from their invitation email. No hub account or sign-in is needed.
- The link opens only the one meeting it was sent for.
- People who are in the hub join from the [Meeting](meeting.html) page instead.

## Join the meeting
1. Open the link in your invitation email. Use the whole link.
2. The page shows the meeting's name and when it is, and says hello to you.
3. Press **Join the meeting**.
4. You will be asked to knock. Wait for the host to let you in.

## During the meeting
- Press **Full screen** to fill the screen with the call.
- Press **Exit full screen** or the Escape key to come out of full screen.
- Leave with the call's own leave button. Press **Rejoin** to go back in.

## If it does not work
- "This link is not complete": open the whole link from your email again.
- "This meeting cannot be opened": the meeting may have been cancelled, or the link may have stopped working.
- "The video service did not load": check your internet connection and press **Try again**.

## Good to know
- The link stops working if the meeting is cancelled, or if you are taken off it.
- It also stops working two hours after the meeting ends.
- Nobody gets in without knocking. The host decides who comes in.

## Who to ask
- Whoever invited you to the meeting.
- The church office, if you cannot reach them.

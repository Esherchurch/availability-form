# Email Compiler

Write an email to a team, or to the church, with words, photos, GIFs and buttons, then send it straight from the hub.

## Who can use it
- Team leaders (anyone who looks after a team), the church office team and master admins can open it.
- Team leaders can send to the people on their own teams.
- The church office and master admins can send to any team, or to **Everyone** in the address book.
- Saved drafts are shared: anyone who can use this page can open, change or delete them.

## Write your email
1. Type the **Subject** at the top. People see it in their inbox.
2. Optionally fill in the preview text under it: the grey line shown in the inbox next to the subject.
3. The header shows the church's name (and your team, for a team leader) and the church logo if there is one. Click its words to change them.
4. Click in the white area and type. Paste works too: odd fonts and spacing are cleaned away.

## Format words
1. Select the words first, then use the toolbar.
2. **B** for bold, **I** for italic, and a bullet list button.
3. **Size**: Small, Normal, Large or Huge.
4. **Font** changes the font of the whole email.
5. **Spacing**: Tight, Normal or Roomy, for the selected lines (or all of them if nothing is selected).
6. **Fix spacing** tidies uneven spacing, for example after pasting.
7. The three colour dots (teal, amber, black) colour the selected words. The align buttons set left, centre or right.

## Add a button, heading or layout
1. Put your cursor where it should go.
2. Use **+ Insert** and choose **Button / link**, **Heading**, **Divider line**, **Spacer**, **Two columns** or **A meeting (Join button)**.
3. For a button, type its words and the web address it links to (starting https://).
4. For a meeting, pick it from the list. A room marked "Church members only" lets in church members only, so send that one to church members.

## Add a photo or GIF
1. Put your cursor where it should go, then press **Photo** and choose a picture. It is uploaded and put in.
2. To change its size, click the picture, press **Resize** and type a width, like 400 or 100%.
3. To make an animated GIF, press **GIF**. Press **+ Add images** and choose 2 to 8 pictures. Click a frame to remove it.
4. Set the **Delay (ms)** per frame (500 is half a second) and the **Width (px)**. **Dither** smooths colour banding.
5. Press **Generate & insert**.

## Choose who gets it
1. Under **Send to**, tick one or more teams (or **Everyone**, if you may).
2. Press **Pick individual people** to add people one by one, on top of any teams. Search by name or email. People with no email can't be picked.
3. The count under it shows how many people are selected, and how many have no email.

## Send a test, then send
1. Under **Your details**, type **Your email (reply-to)**. Replies go there. If it is empty, replies go to the church's enquiry address.
2. Press **Review & Send**. You see the email exactly as it will arrive, and who it goes to.
3. To test, put your own address in the box and press **Send test**. A test only goes to your own email address, with "[TEST]" before the subject.
4. Press **Send to ... people**. Each person gets their own copy.
5. At the end you see how many were delivered and failed. Press **Retry ... failed** to try the failed ones again.

## Save and open drafts
1. Press **Open saved drafts**.
2. **Save current as new draft** and give it a name. There are 10 draft slots for the church; delete one to make room.
3. **Load** opens a draft. While one is loaded, **Save changes to this draft** saves over it.
4. The bin button deletes a draft.
- Your work is also kept on this device as you type ("Autosaved · unsent"), so it is still there if you reload. That copy is on this device only; save a draft to carry on elsewhere.

## Good to know
- People selected who have no email are listed in Review & Send and won't get it. Add their address in the Address Book.
- Deleting a draft can't be undone.
- A sent email can't be called back.
- Your name is saved with drafts you create or edit. Use **change** at the bottom of the panel to change it.
- Emails sent from here are listed on the Emails sent page, which master admins can see.

## Who to ask
- The church office, for sending to people beyond your own team.
- Your team leader, or a master admin, if you can't open the page.

# Home (the hub)

This is the hub's home page, where you land after signing in. It shows the church's notices, your serving dates, today's video meetings and your team's own things, and it is the way into every other page.

## Who can use it
- Anyone signed in can open it. What you see depends on your teams.
- Someone on no team sees "Everyone": the notices for the whole church, and a **Coming up** card from What's On.
- **Post a notice** is shown to the church office and master admins (who can post to anyone), and to team admins (who can post to their own teams only).
- **Edit** and **Administration** (the cog) are shown to team admins and master admins.

## Get around
1. Press **Menu** (on a computer the Menu is down the left side, and the bar has **Search**). Type in **Search pages** to find a page.
2. **Hub** at the top always brings you back to the top of Home.
3. On a phone, the bar at the bottom has **Home**, **What's on**, **Watch**, **My serving** and **More**. **Meet** appears only on a day you have a video meeting.
4. **Care & support** (or **Care** on a phone) is at the top of every page.
5. Press your initials for your own things: **My details**, **Add to my calendar**, **My availability**, **Sign out** and more.

## Choose what Home shows first
1. If you are on more than one team, press **Home shows:** at the top (on a phone, **Change** under the banner).
2. Pick a team, or **Everyone**.
3. This only changes what Home puts first. It does not change what you can open. Notices from all your teams still show.

## Read the notices
1. The **Notices** card shows the newest two. Press one to open the full list.
2. Press **See all** on the card, or **Latest** at the top, to see every notice. The number on **Latest** is how many are new to you.
3. A pinned notice shows at the top of Home under **Please read**.
4. Press **Share** on a notice to send its link. People still need to sign in to read it.

## Confirm you have read a notice
1. Some notices ask you to confirm. They show in **Waiting for you**.
2. Press **Confirm**, or **I've read it** on the pinned notice.

## Post a notice
1. Press **Post a notice** (on the Notices card or at the top of Home).
2. Fill in the **Title** and the **Notice**. You can paste an email straight in.
3. **Date label (optional)** is shown instead of the day you posted it.
4. **Show until (optional)**: the notice takes itself off the page after this day.
5. Under **Who sees it**, pick the teams. **Everyone** is for things for the whole church.
6. Tick **Pin it to the top** for things everyone really must see.
7. Tick **Ask people to confirm they have read it** to see who has and who has not.
8. Press **Post**.

## Send an urgent notice to phones
1. Only the church office and master admins see this, and only when the church uses notifications.
2. In the notice, tick **Urgent: send it to people's phones as well**.
3. Press **Post** and say yes. It goes once, straight away, to everyone it is for. It cannot be called back.

## Change or remove a notice
1. Press **Edit** at the top of Home. It turns into **Done**.
2. Open **Latest**. Press **Edit** on a notice to change it, or the bin to remove it.
3. Or open **Administration**, then **Notices**, and use **Edit** or **Remove**.
4. Press **Done** when you have finished.

## Video meetings
1. On a day you have a video meeting, a **Video meetings** card shows it.
2. Press **Join** to go in. Type your name and knock; the host lets you in.
3. If you set it up or lead its team, you also see **Invite people** and **Cancel**.
4. **New meeting** and **All rooms** take you to the [Meeting](meeting.html) page.

## My serving
1. **My serving** shows your next three dates on the rota, with your role.
2. Press **The whole rota** to see everything.

## Put your rota in your calendar
1. Press your initials and choose **Add to my calendar**. Or open **Menu** and press **My rota**.
2. Choose **Just me**, **My household**, or one of the full rotas.
3. Press **Add to my calendar**, then **Google**, **Apple / iPhone** or **Outlook**. Or **Copy link**.
4. If a link has got out, press **Reset this link**. The old one stops working at once.

## Pin board
1. The **Pin board** card shows the newest notes on your team's board.
2. Press **Add an idea** to add one, or **Open the pin board** to see them all.

## Your team's charter
1. The charter card shows what your team is expected to do, folded into sections.
2. Press a heading to open it, or **Open them all** (or **Read it all**).
3. If you are on more than one team, use the buttons above it to read another team's charter.

## Change the banner
For team admins and master admins.
1. Press **Edit**, then **Edit banner** on the banner.
2. A master admin is asked which banner: OK for the team's own, Cancel for the church-wide one.
3. Fill in **Heading** (team banner only) and **Line underneath**.
4. Drop a photo in the box, or click to choose one. A wide photo works best, under 8 MB.
5. Drag the photo to move it, use **Zoom**, or **Reset**. **Remove picture** takes it off.
6. Press **Save**.

## Write the team charter or team panel
For team admins and master admins.
1. Press **Edit** at the top of Home.
2. On the charter, press **Edit** (or **Write the charter** if there is none). Fill in **Title** and **Charter**, then **Save**.
3. On your team's panel, press **Edit**. Fill in **Heading (optional)** and **Text**, then **Save**. Only your team sees it.
4. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.

## Administration
For team admins and master admins. Press the cog (on a phone, it is in your initials menu).
- **People**: everyone who has signed in. Someone marked **Needs linking** signed in with an email the address book does not hold. Choose who they are and press **Link**.
- On a linked person, tick their teams and choose **Member**, **Leader** or **Admin**. This updates the address book too. A team admin can only change their own teams.
- **Wrong person?** or **Unlink** takes a sign-in off the record it was linked to.
- **Notices**: every notice you can manage, with **Edit** and **Remove**, and **+ New notice**.
- **Youth codes**: for admins of the children's and youth teams. Under 16s cannot sign in, so a code is emailed to their parent. Choose the young person and press **Send**. Use **Resend** or **Revoke** on a code.
- **Pages**: what appears in the menu, and who sees it.

## Other cards you may see
- **Watch live** or **Watch again**: the service, on the day.
- **This Sunday**: for the people who plan Sundays, with the leader, speaker, roles filled and songs.
- **Sundays** (on a phone): your Sunday planning pages.
- **My events**: events you have signed up to. Press **Your place** to see your booking.

## Good to know
- A notice for no team goes to everyone in the church. You are asked to say yes first.
- A team admin can only change a notice that is wholly for their own teams.
- An expired notice is hidden, not deleted. It still shows in Administration, Notices, marked "Expired".
- Removing a notice cannot be undone.
- An urgent phone message is sent once and cannot be called back.
- Treat calendar links like passwords: anyone with one can see that rota.
- A card with nothing in it does not show.
- If the page says "Half of this page is out of date", press Ctrl and F5 together, or **Reload**.

## Who to ask
- The church office, about notices or your details.
- Your team leader, about your team's charter, panel or pin board.
- A master admin, if you cannot see something you should, or for Administration.

# Children's register

This page holds the church's Sunday children's groups, the children registered for them, and registration. Parents register once a year with the consent form.

## Who can use it
- The leads see every child and every tab. They are master admins, the safeguarding lead and deputy, and the admins of the children's teams.
- A group's leaders see only their own group's children, with their medical details. They see one tab, **Your group**.
- Anyone else is told the page is for the children's team leads and each group's leaders.
- If the church has more than one site, choose the **Site** at the top.

## Find a child
1. On the **Children** tab, choose a **Group**, or leave it on every group.
2. Type a child's or a parent's name in **Find**.
3. Each child shows their group, consent, and flags such as **Allergies**, **Medical** or **No photos**.
4. Press **Open** to see the parent, phone, emergency contact, who may collect and the family code.

## See medical details
1. Open the child.
2. Press **Show medical details**. You see medical needs, medication, allergies and additional needs.

## Change a child's group or status (leads)
1. Open the child.
2. Choose their **Group**, and their **Status**: **Registered**, **Visitor** or **Left**.
3. Change who may collect if needed. This changes it for the whole family.
4. Press **Save**.

## Send the registration form (leads)
1. Open the **Registration** tab.
2. Under **Send the registration form**, type the parent's name and email.
3. Press **Send it**.
4. Under **Sent, not back yet**, press **Remind** to send a reminder.

## Add a family to the register (leads)
1. Open the **Registration** tab. The number shows how many forms are waiting.
2. Under **Ready to add to the register**, check the children and their groups.
3. Press **Add to the register**. If the family is already there, this updates them.

## Set up the groups (leads)
1. Open the **Groups** tab and press **Add a group**, or **Edit** on one.
2. Fill in the **Name**, **Room**, **School years**, **Day**, **From**, **Until** and **Children per leader**.
3. Add leaders with **Add a leader**. They see this group's children and medical details.
4. Press **Save**. Children go into the group for their school year, unless moved by hand.

## Settings (leads)
1. Open the **Settings** tab.
2. **The children's teams**: tick the teams whose admins are the leads, then **Save**. A master admin or the safeguarding lead changes these.
3. **The registration form**: press **Set up the registration form** if it is not set up yet.
4. **Calling a parent**: choose whether a leader may show a family's number on the service screen.
5. **Label size**: choose the labels the desk printer takes.
6. **Term dates**: set this year's three terms, then **Save the term dates**.

## Good to know
- Consent lasts a year. A child shows when it is running out, and when it has run out.
- Leaders always call a parent's phone first. The screen number is a last resort, and never shows a child's name or the collection code.
- At the door, nobody else may collect a child without the family code.
- The form's wording is changed on the Forms page.
- **Sunday check-in** opens the check-in page.

## Who to ask
- The children's team leads.
- The safeguarding lead, about medical details or who may collect.
- A master admin, if the page is not set up yet.

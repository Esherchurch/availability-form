# Team emails

This page sets up how a team can send emails as itself. The email says it is from the team, and replies go to the team's own address, or to the sender if the team has none.

## Who can use it
- Master admins only.
- Team emails have to be switched on for the church. If they are off, the page says so.
- Anyone else is told "This page is for master admins."

## Choose the Pastor and the office admin
1. In **The Pastor and the office admin**, pick a person under **Pastor** and under **Office admin**.
2. Press **Save**.
3. They may send as any team except a safeguarding team.

## Set up a team
1. Find the team's box under **Teams**.
2. Type the team's **Shared email address**, if it has one. This is optional.
3. Under **Leads** and **Deputies**, pick people from **Add someone…**. Press **×** next to a name to take them off.
4. Tick **Safeguarding** if it is a safeguarding team.
5. Press **Save** on that team's box.

## Clear a team's settings
1. Press **Clear these settings** on the team's box, and say yes.
2. Nobody can send as that team until it is set up again.

## Good to know
- Team emails open when the hub goes live. Until then, a note says so, nothing can be saved, and nobody can send as a team.
- A team's leads and deputies can send as it. So can the Pastor and the office admin, unless it is a safeguarding team.
- A safeguarding team can only be sent as by its own leads and deputies: not the Pastor, the office admin or a master admin. The hub keeps only the outline of each email: when, who, and the subject.
- Emails still leave from the hub's one sending address. The team's mailbox gets a copy.
- Teams come from [Teams & groups](teams-admin.html). Add, rename or archive a team there, and it shows here.

## Who to ask
- A master admin.

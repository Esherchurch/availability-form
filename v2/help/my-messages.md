# My messages

This page lists what the church has emailed you in the last year. That means sign-ups, bookings, forms, reminders and the like.

## Who can use it
- Anyone signed in can open it.
- You only ever see your own emails.

## See what the church has sent you
1. Open the page.
2. Each email shows its subject, when it was sent, and what it was about.
3. To read the email itself, open it in your own email.

## Good to know
- Only the outline is shown here, not the whole email.
- If an email could not be sent, it says so. Check your email address is right, or ask the office.
- Emails your team leaders send from their own email are not listed here yet.
- Rota emails are not listed here yet.

## Who to ask
- The church office, if an email could not be sent or you think one is missing.

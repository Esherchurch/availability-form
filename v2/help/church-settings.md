# Church details

This page holds the church's settings that only master admins change: the hub's web address, giving details, notifications, which parts of the hub the church uses, the private forms, who gets told about care requests, and some keys and connections.

## Who can use it
- Only master admins can open it.
- Anyone else is told they cannot open the page.
- The church's name, enquiry email, logo and words are changed on [Your church](your-church.html), not here.
- The church office keeps the giving details on [Giving details](giving-details.html).
- Who may do what, section by section, is on [Permissions](permissions.html).

## Your church and the hub's web address
1. The first card shows the church's name, enquiry email and logo.
2. To change them, press **Change the name, email, logo and words**. It opens Your church.
3. **The hub's web address** shows where every email and link from the hub goes. Press **Copy** to copy it.
4. The address is made from the church's short name. To change it, ask Church HQ.

## Giving details
1. Under **Giving**, fill in **Account name**, **Sort code**, **Account number** and **Suggested reference**.
2. Under **Gift Aid**, choose **Do not mention Gift Aid**, or **Mention Gift Aid, with a link to the form**. If you mention it, paste the **Link to the Gift Aid form**.
3. Add one line under **Anything else to say** if you like.
4. Press **Save the giving details**.
5. These show to people who have signed in, on the Give screen in the app and the Give page on the website.

## Turn notifications on or off
1. Under **Notifications**, press **Send notifications**.
2. Turning it on asks you to be sure. It starts sending to the whole church, by phone, and by email to anyone without the app.
3. When it is off, nothing is sent to anybody. "Send me a test" on the Notifications page still works for whoever presses it.
4. Each person chooses which notifications they get, on their own Notifications page.

## Choose what your church uses
1. Under **What does your church use?**, each part of the hub has a switch.
2. Press a switch to turn that part on or off. It saves straight away.
3. A part switched off goes from the Menu, the app and Home for everyone.
4. Parts marked **add-on** are extras a church may not need.

## Open the private forms
1. Under **Private forms**, press **Open the private forms**.
2. This covers Care & support, prayer requests and "I'm new here".
3. Turn this on only on the day the church goes live, once the hub's data is locked. The page asks you to be sure.
4. Under **When they open, in words**, type a date such as "1 November 2026" and press **Save**. The forms show this until they open.

## Choose who gets told
1. Under **Who gets told** there are three boxes: **Requests for pastoral care**, **Concerns about someone (pastoral)** and **Prayer requests**.
2. In each, start typing a name in **Add a person** and pick them from the list. They are added straight away. Or press **Add**.
3. To take someone off, press the **×** on their name.
4. You can also type **A dedicated address** for each, such as a pastoral team email address. It is optional.
5. Press **Save who gets told**.

## API.Bible key
1. Under **API.Bible**, paste the church's own key in **API.Bible key**.
2. Press **Save the key**. It is never shown again.
3. To take it away, press **Remove the key** and say yes.
4. Without a key, the session planner still has the free Bibles and Bible Gateway links.

## Connect Microsoft 365
1. Under **Microsoft 365**, press **Connect Microsoft 365**.
2. The page explains the two screens Microsoft will show. Press **Continue to Microsoft**, or **Not now**.
3. You need to be a Global administrator of the church's Microsoft 365.
4. When connected, PowerPoint slides in Present are drawn by Microsoft's own PowerPoint. To stop, press **Disconnect**.

## Canva keys
1. Under **Email and Canva keys**, paste the **Canva client ID** and **Canva client secret**.
2. Press **Save**. A saved key is never shown again. Paste a new one to replace it.
3. The same card says whether the hub's email keys are set. Those are not pasted here.

## Good to know
- Who gets told is told that something has come in, never who or what.
- Care requests and concerns never go to the church office by default. If you type the church office's address on purpose, the page reminds you to make sure only the right person reads that inbox.
- With nobody named, requests go to the safeguarding lead and deputy.
- Safeguarding concerns go only to the safeguarding lead and deputy. They are set in Safeguarding settings, not here. If none is set, the page warns you, and the Care & support forms send nothing.
- Switching a part off deletes nothing. Switching it back on brings it all back.
- Before the church goes live, the private forms save nothing, and the API.Bible and Canva keys cannot be saved yet. The page says so.

## Who to ask
- Another master admin.
- Church HQ, to change the hub's web address or about going live.

# Forms

This page is for consent forms, declarations and questionnaires. You build them here, send them for an event or to one person, and see who has not filled them in.

## Who can use it
- Anyone who is an admin of something can open it.
- A team's admins see and send their own team's forms.
- A site's safeguarding lead or deputy sees that site's forms.
- Master admins see every form.
- Signed in but not an admin, you are turned away.

## Start a new form
1. Under **Forms**, open **New form from…**.
2. Choose **A blank form**, or one of the templates. The parent consent form is the usual first one.
3. The form opens below for you to fill in.

## Set up the form
1. Type the **Title**.
2. Fill in **Why we ask**. It is shown at the top of the form.
3. Choose the **Team**. Its admins send the form and see the answers.
4. Choose the **Site**. Its safeguarding lead sees the medical answers.
5. Choose **Who can fill it in**: **Anyone with the link**, **Attenders, signed in**, **Church members only**, or **Its team**.
6. Choose how long **An answer lasts**: one event, the end of the term, the end of the school year, a number of days, or a number of months.
7. Set **Keep answers for**: how many months to keep answers after they run out.

## Add and change questions
1. Press **Add a question**.
2. Type the **Question** and choose the kind of **Answer**.
3. For a choice, type the **Choices**, separated by commas.
4. Add **Help under the question** if it needs explaining.
5. Tick **Must be answered** if it is needed.
6. Tick **Medical: keep private** for health questions. Some questions are kept private anyway, from their wording. They show **Private anyway, from its wording**.
7. Use the arrow buttons to move a question up or down. Use the bin button to remove it.
8. For a section asked once for each child, press **Add a question to this section** to add to it. Its first question should be the child's name.
9. Press **Save the form**.

## Change a form
1. Press **Edit** next to the form.
2. Make your changes and press **Save the form**.

## Send a form for an event
1. Under **Send for an event, and chase**, choose the **Event**.
2. Open **Ask for a form…** and choose the form.
3. Press **Send to … not yet asked**. Each booking gets an email with its own link.
4. People who already gave a still-valid answer are asked "still correct?" instead of filling it in again.

## Chase and see answers
1. Each form for the event shows how many are done, and the status of each booking.
2. Press **Remind … not finished** to email everyone who has not finished.
3. Press **Answers** to see what someone sent.
4. To stop asking for a form at this event, press the cross next to it and say yes. Answers already given are kept.

## Send to one person
1. Under **Send to one person**, choose the **Form**.
2. Type their **Name** and **Email**, and press **Send**.
3. This is for forms not tied to an event, such as a leader's declaration or a hirer's safeguarding form.
4. The most recent ones are listed below with their status. Press **Remind** or **Answers**.

## Good to know
- Medical answers are kept apart. Only the site's safeguarding lead and master admins can see them. Others are told medical answers were given.
- While an answer lasts, the same family is asked "is this still correct?" for the next event.
- A form needs a title, a reason, and wording for every question before it will save.
- Only people with a confirmed booking are sent the form.
- Sending for an event needs you to be on that event's team, or a master admin.

## Who to ask
- Your team's admin, for your team's forms.
- The safeguarding lead, about medical answers.
- A master admin, if you cannot see a form you need.

# Pastoral visits

This is where the church's trained pastoral visitors record their visits, set follow-up reminders and look back at past visits. It is also where the list of visitors is kept.

## Who can use it
- The church's named pastoral visitors can record and read every visit.
- A master admin, or the pastoral lead (the person named for pastoral concerns in Who gets told), keeps the list of visitors.
- Keeping the list does not let you read visits. A master admin or the pastoral lead can read them only if they add themselves to the list.
- The church office cannot read visits.
- Anyone else is told the page is for the church's named pastoral visitors, with a link to get care and support.
- When you are viewing the site as someone else, the visits are not shown.

## Record a visit
1. Under **Record a visit**, type a name in **Who was visited**. Pick them from the list if they are in the address book, or just type their name.
2. Check **When** (it starts as today) and choose **How**: **At home**, **In hospital**, **By phone** or **Somewhere else**.
3. Tick anyone under **Who went with you (optional)**.
4. If you want a reminder, choose a day in **Follow up on (optional)**.
5. Add **A short private note (optional)** if it helps.
6. Press **Save the visit**. It says "Saved."

## Mark a follow-up as done
1. Follow-ups that are due show under **Follow-ups due**.
2. When you have followed up, press **Followed up** on that visit.

## Delete a visit
1. Find it under **Visits** and press **Delete**.
2. Say yes. This cannot be undone.

## Review old visits
1. Visits are kept for 2 years after the person's last visit.
2. After that, the person shows under **Due for review**.
3. Press **Keep another year** if there is still a reason to keep them.
4. Or press **Delete their visits** and say yes. Every visit to that person is deleted. This cannot be undone.

## Keep the list of visitors
For a master admin or the pastoral lead.
1. Under **Who the visitors are**, type a name in **Add a visitor** and pick them from the list.
2. Press **Add**.
3. To take someone off, press the **×** beside their name.
4. Press **Save the list**. Nothing changes until you do.

## Good to know
- Only name people who are trained for pastoral visiting. Everyone on the list can read every visit.
- If nobody is on the list, nobody can record or read visits.
- A visitor must be in the address book.
- The list holds 500 visitors at most.
- Reminder emails only say that a follow-up is due. They never say who or what.
- Deleted visits cannot be brought back.

## Who to ask
- The pastoral lead, about visits or being added as a visitor.
- A master admin, if you keep the list and something will not save.

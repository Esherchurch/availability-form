# Give regularly

This page lets anyone set up a regular gift to the church by Direct Debit. The money goes straight to the church. The giver can come back later to change or stop it.

## Who can use it
- Anyone. You do not need to sign in. If you are signed in, your name and email fill in for you.
- If the church does not take Direct Debits online yet, the page says so.

## Set up a regular gift
1. Type **How much (£)**.
2. Choose **How often?**: **Monthly** or **Every 4 weeks**.
3. For monthly, choose **On which day of the month**.
4. Type **Your name** and **Your email**.
5. Press **Set up my Direct Debit**.
6. You go to GoCardless's secure page to give your bank details. Then you come back here.

## Add Gift Aid
1. Tick **Add Gift Aid** if you pay UK tax. The church can then claim 25p for every £1.
2. Read the words that appear.
3. Type your **House name or number** and **Postcode**. The rest of the address is optional.

## Change your gift
1. Open the link in the email you were sent. It shows **Your regular gift**.
2. Change the amount, **How often?** or the day.
3. Press **Change it**. It starts from the next collection, and an email says so.

## Stop your gift
1. Open the link in your email.
2. Press **Stop my gift** and say yes.

## Good to know
- The church never sees your bank details. GoCardless handles them, and the Direct Debit Guarantee protects you.
- Just after setting up, the page says GoCardless is confirming it. It updates by itself.
- Keep the link to your gift page. It is how you change or stop your gift.
- Stopping ends the gift. Your Direct Debit stays with your bank, unused, until you cancel it there if you wish.

## Who to ask
- The church office, or whoever looks after giving.

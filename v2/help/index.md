# Availability Form

This is where people on a team say which dates they can serve. The people who plan the rota see your answers.

## Who can use it
- Anyone on a team whose email the church has in its address book.
- You do not need to sign in. You type your email address.
- If your email is not found, it says "Email not found". Ask the church office to add you.

## Open your dates
1. Type your email in **Enter your email**.
2. Press **Check My Services**.
3. If more than one person shares your email, such as a family, choose your name and press **Load Form for me**.
4. Your dates show, grouped by term. Each one shows the kind of service, the date and the times.

## Say if you can come
1. For each date, press **✓ Available** or **✗ Unavailable**.
2. Your answer is saved as soon as you press it.
3. When you have answered them all, press **Confirm Rota Availability**.
4. If any date has no answer, it lists them and asks if you want to go on.

## Change an answer
1. Open the form again with your email.
2. Your earlier answers show. Press the other button for any date.

## Finish
- Press the round button at the top right to leave the form.

## Good to know
- You only see dates for your own teams.
- A date you do not answer is left blank. The team sees no answer from you. It is not counted as free.
- If you try too many times, it may ask you to wait.

## Who to ask
- Your team leader, about the dates.
- The church office, if your email is not found.

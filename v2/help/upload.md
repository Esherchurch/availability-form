# Send photos

This page lets you send your photos of a church event to the church. You reach it from a link someone at the church gave you.

## Who can use it
- Anyone with the link. You do not need to sign in.
- If the link has run out, has taken all the photos it can, or has been switched off, the page says so.
- If the link is not complete or cannot be found, the page says so. Check you have the whole link.

## Send your photos
1. Press **Choose photos** and pick the photos on your phone or computer.
2. Each photo is listed. **ready** means it can be sent. Any that cannot be sent say why.
3. Type **Your name** if you like. It is optional.
4. Press **Send**. The button shows how many photos will go.
5. The photos go one at a time. Each one then says **sent** or **not sent**.
6. When all are sent, you see **Thank you**. Press **Send some more** to send others.

## Good to know
- Photos can be JPEG, PNG, HEIC or WebP, under 15 MB each.
- Each link can take only so many photos. The page says how many more it can take. If you choose too many, it asks you to choose fewer.
- Nothing you send is shown anywhere until someone at the church has looked at it.
- Please only send photos you took, and think of anyone in them, especially children.

## Who to ask
- Whoever gave you the link, if it has stopped working or you have more photos to send.

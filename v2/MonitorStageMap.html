Monitor Setup

Wedge assignments and channel lists for the Performance App

Service

Pick the service, then tap a musician and tap the wedge they're standing at.

Stage

Tap a musician on the right, then tap their wedge.

Bridge

The PC on the church network running start-monitor-bridge.bat — not the mixer's own address.

Wedges

One row per foldback wedge, with the aux bus that feeds it. Drag a wedge on the stage map to move it.

Channel names

The app reads which channels are in each wedge straight off the mixer, but MIDI carries no channel names — so give the ones you use a name here, matching the desk. Anything left blank shows as "Ch 12". One-off job; only needs revisiting when the input list changes.

Channels per role — optional

Leave this empty and each performer sees everything the desk has routed into their wedge. Add a role here only to narrow that down — the channels listed are the only ones that role will be shown. Role names must match the rota ("Keyboard", "Lead Vocal"), and a group named default covers anyone without one of their own.

Mix Analyser

Drag in audio files. It detects tempo, the beat grid and the first downbeat, then lets you audition a beat-matched blend between any two tracks and export it. Everything runs locally in this page — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Drop audio files here or click to choose  ·  mp3, wav, m4a, flac, ogg, aac

Blend lab

Stretches both tracks to a common tempo, aligns downbeats, and crossfades with a bass swap.

Stretch budget. Keep each track within about 6% of its own tempo or it starts to sound processed. The target BPM box turns red past that. If two tracks are too far apart, that join wants a hard cut, not a blend.
Checking a grid by ear. Detection is only as good as the recording. Press Check grid on any track to hear 8 bars from the detected downbeat with a click on every beat. If the click drifts off the snare, correct the BPM or downbeat by hand — the fields are editable and everything downstream re-reads them.

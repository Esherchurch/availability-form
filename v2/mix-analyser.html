Mix Analyser

Drag in audio files. It detects tempo, the beat grid and the first downbeat, then lets you audition a transition between any two tracks — either a beat-matched blend, or a drum bridge that strips the outgoing track down to its beat before the next one arrives. Everything runs locally in this page; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Drop audio files here or click to choose  ·  mp3, wav, m4a, flac, ogg, aac

Transition lab

Stretches both tracks to a common tempo and aligns their downbeats, then joins them.

Stretch budget. Keep each track within about 6% of its own tempo or it starts to sound processed. The target BPM box turns red past that.
Setting points on a waveform. Click to set the entry point — gold line — for skipping a quiet intro. Shift-click to set the mix-out point — red line — which is where a blend or a drum bridge begins working backwards from. Both snap to the nearest bar. The mix-out point defaults to the last audible bar, not the end of the file, because most releases have a fade-out and trailing silence that would otherwise become your "beat". And press Check grid to hear 8 bars from the detected downbeat with a click on every beat; if the click drifts off the snare, correct the BPM or downbeat by hand.

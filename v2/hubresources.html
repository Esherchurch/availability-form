Install the app

An Android app, downloaded from here rather than the Play Store.

1
On your Android phone, tap Download below, or scan the QR code with the phone's camera.
2
When it has downloaded, tap the file to open it (or find it in Files > Downloads).
3
If the phone asks, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
4
Tap Install, then Open. To update it later, download the new version the same way.

# Live audit, 15 Oct 2026 (reviewing window)

Done on the LIVE site in the reviewing window's browser, signed in as Martin
(master admin). Every page was loaded inside a test frame with every write
switched off first: Firestore set/update/delete/add/batch/transaction,
Storage uploads, callables, sign-out, form submits, and every non-GET
request except Firestore reads. confirm() answered No, prompt() answered
nothing, window.open() recorded and not opened. Buttons whose words mean a
change (Save, Send, Delete, Pay, Sign up, Book, Approve...) were listed and
not pressed.

## What was covered

- 70 pages: every Menu page, the app, and the public pages.
- Layout at 360x780, 412x915 (Martin's phone) and 1440x900, as Martin.
- Access at 412 as: Martin, "Everyone (no team)", a Worship member, a Kids
  Church member (View as).
- Buttons: 1,705 found on 68 pages; 1,157 tapped (pages with more than 70
  buttons: the first 70). 305 opened another page, 554 changed the page,
  188 were not pressed on purpose (they would change data), 18 go to other
  sites, 75 did nothing - all 75 checked: every one needed something typed
  first, was a download/PDF/file picker (blocked or invisible in a test), or
  was stopped by the automatic "No" to "Are you sure?". No dead button was
  confirmed.

## 4. "View as" can't be trusted on some pages

Viewing as "Everyone (no team)", these still opened with full admin views,
because they check who is really signed in (a master admin), not who is
being viewed: Room bookings (approve/decline), Care & support inbox ("you
are the safeguarding lead"), Small groups (Oversight, Settings), Children's
register, Safeguarding settings, Event check-in, Registers. A real member
may well be refused - but the preview can't show it. Proof needs real test
accounts (the main window's audit-everything with seeded users).

Pages that do refuse also throw a JavaScript error underneath ("Cannot set
properties of null"): Planner, Sunday Service Planner, Forms, Headcounts,
Picture library, Youth Service Planner, Monitor Setup.

## 5. Can you find it? (taps from Home on the website)

| Everyday job | Where it is | Taps |
|---|---|---|
| Send an email to a team | Menu > Core Team > People and email > Email Compiler, then "Let's go" | 4 + 1 |
| Set up team email addresses | Menu > Core Team > Admin > Team emails | 4 |
| Add or change an event | Menu > Core Team > Events and rooms > Events | 4 |
| Plan Sunday / the rota | Menu > Core Team > Planning > ... | 4 |
| Address book | Menu > Core Team > People and email > Address Book | 4 |
| Church settings | Menu > Core Team > Admin > Church settings | 4 |
| Post a notice | only the "Post a notice" button on Home | 1, not in the Menu |
| Watch a service / sermons | not on the website for members | none |
| Rota, What's on, Give, Book a room, Maintenance, Payments, Prayer, Care | top of the Menu | 2 |

The problem: everything that isn't a team's own page sits under "Core
Team", grouped by team instead of by job. People and email, Events and
rooms, and Admin are not Core Team things.

## Recommended navigation (for Martin's approval)

- Top-level sections by JOB, each shown only to those who can use it:
  Sundays (Rota, Rota planner, Sunday plan, Preaching plan); People and
  messages (Address book, Send an email, Team emails, Notices); Events and
  rooms; Church admin (Church settings, Teams and groups, Payments,
  Safeguarding, Emails sent); then each team's own space.
- Any everyday job within 2 taps of Home; a "Send an email" and "Post a
  notice" button on Home for anyone allowed to.
- The app's spaces the same way.
